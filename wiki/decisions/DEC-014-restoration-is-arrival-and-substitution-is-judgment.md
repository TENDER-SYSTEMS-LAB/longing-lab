# DEC-014 — Restoration counts as arrival, and substitution counts judgment

## The decision

[[technology-waves]] gives every wave two phases: arrival, which raises issuance of the BEARER BOND in [[DEC-008-bearer-bond-is-perpetual]], and substitution, which raises calls. The AI wave's connection coefficient was set from substitution alone. On 2026-09-21 the user asked whether an ALS speech decoder — AI that gives back a capacity illness took — runs toward more romance ([[SRC-2026-09-21-ai-enablement-and-substitution]]), and the same day, after asking what counting restoration as arrival would mean and for examples of execution and judgment, decided ([[SRC-2026-09-21-restoration-is-arrival-and-substitution-is-judgment]]):

> 복원을 도착으로 세고, substitution은 판단 대체를 셈하자.

**Two parts, `user-confirmed`:**

1. **Restoration counts as arrival.** A capability that gives back a way of reaching a person that illness, distance or circumstance had taken raises issuance in the same phase as a capability that creates a way that did not exist. The arrival phase is therefore *new or recovered* modes of connection, not new ones only. The AI wave's connection coefficient — a tenth of the social web's — was set without this and must be reset; its value is not decided here.
2. **The substitution channel counts substitution of judgment.** What raises calls is the system deciding what the person could have decided — the content, the consequential choice — not the person handing over the doing. Execution handed off with the choice retained is not, by itself, substitution.

## The user's qualifications in the same turn

Stated as observations and a *should*, `user-originated`, not decided:

- **Not all execution is restoration**, but some execution has a restoring side — voicing what a person chose to say, reading a text aloud in a voice learned from another person.
- **Some execution is substitution**, because handing over the step hands over the deliberation with it: sending *how are you* and letting AI translate it hands over, whole, the work of deciding how to ask after someone. The assistant had placed that example under execution; the user moves it.
- **The split may be ambiguous.** The user asks whether cases exist that are neither clearly execution nor clearly judgment.
- **A per-security ontology.** The user proposes analysing each security's practice for how much execution and how much judgment it involves, and how exposed each is, and fixing that carefully per security. This would be the rule by which part 2 is applied; it is not written.

## What it changes

- On [[technology-waves]], the definition of the arrival phase widens and the AI wave gains an arrival effect the design had denied it; the conclusion in [[loop-simulation]] that every advance after 2010 is short covering is now conditional on the reset coefficient.
- On [[data-sources]], the proposed measurement design's three quantities — enablement, substitution, retained agency — are affected: substitution is no longer the share of execution delegated with agency as a control beside it; what the channel counts is the loss of the choice. The design is `llm-proposed` and must be redrawn against this decision.
- On [[reflection]], the tie the assistant proposed between the measured object and 사유 as room for one's own judgment is closer to the decided rule than before, and remains a proposal.

## What this does not decide

- The AI wave's reset connection coefficient, and whether any earlier wave's coefficient changes once restoration is counted — the alumni-finding boom of 1999 is described on [[technology-waves]] as restoring severed relationships.
- The rule that sorts an act between execution and judgment, including the ambiguous middle the user names; the per-security ontology that would carry it.
- How substitution of judgment is measured per act, and from what data.
