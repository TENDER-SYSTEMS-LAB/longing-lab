# DEC-011 — Positioning stays in the engine; the audience reads it as hearts cooling

## The decision

Asked to settle the `Joint` line's computation and display, the user stepped back to the ledger itself and stated what the audience is for:

> positioning을 꼭 해야할까? 물론 금융시장에서는 필요한 것이긴 하지만 해당 작품에서 관객들이 알필요는 없는 부분이야. 관객들로 하여금 지표의 장기적인 하락과 여러 리서치 리포트들을 읽어보고 시대가 점점 낭만이 사라져 가고 있구나.. 라는걸 인식하도를 질문하는게 목적인데 너무 현실적인 것을 녹여내는 느낌이야.

**The audience need not know finance.** The work's purpose is that the long decline of the index and the accumulated research reports bring the viewer to recognise that romance is leaving the age. A ledger line the viewer has to be a market professional to read is realism at the expense of that purpose. `user-originated`.

The assistant separated the **engine** from the **receipt**. Positioning cannot leave the engine: every rally mechanism the work has — the crowded-short squeezes of [[DEC-004-secular-decline-with-rallies]], the default clusters that contract float in [[DEC-007-standard-return-numeraire]], the demand-driven rallies and the long liquidation that signed positioning produced in [[loop-simulation]] — runs through it, and without it the chart is a smooth thirty-year line. But nothing obliges the published receipt to name it in market language. Asked whether real research houses put other lines on their receipts, the assistant described the strategist's long-horizon split — *did it earn more, or did people pay more for the same thing* — and offered that split as the audience translation of positioning: not *romance diminished* but *romance valued less*. The user chose it:

> 마음이 식은 몫으로 바꾸자. 결정 기록해줘

**Positioning stays in the engine and is published to the audience as the share from hearts cooling** — 마음이 식은 몫 — the part of the week's move in which people valued romance less rather than romance itself diminishing. The distinction the line draws, between the thing changing and the regard for it changing, is the question the thirty-year decline puts to the viewer, so the line is not hidden but translated. `user-confirmed`.

## The receipt the user accepted

The five-line audience receipt the assistant printed, accepted by the user's choice of its third line. The Korean names are the ones spoken in the conversation; the published wording in either language is unset.

| Audience line | Ledger line beneath it |
|---|---|
| 기술이 가져간 몫 — what technology took | declared schedule (substitution) |
| 낭만 자체가 줄어든 몫 — romance itself diminished | priced factors |
| 사람들 마음이 식은 몫 — hearts cooled, romance valued less | positioning |
| 지난주 보고서가 만든 몫 — what last week's report caused | reflexive |
| 모름 — unknown | `Unexplained` |

The numeraire line — the ruler moved — is not on this receipt; where it goes for the audience is not decided. `Unexplained` is on it, which answers the question [[attribution-ledger]] carried since 2026-09-06: the viewer sees it, as *unknown*.

## What it settles beside the line

- **The measured-versus-estimated status of positioning**, which the assistant had put to the user as open, is answered by the corpus: [[DEC-007-standard-return-numeraire]] already has the institute *estimating* positioning because a bearer instrument has no register, under a `MODELED` tag. Positioning is therefore an estimated line and under [[DEC-010-declared-order-with-a-joint-line]] it stays after the measured block. The order stands as published there.
- **The plain-language layer is a layer**, not a replacement. The engine's lines and the attribution's order are unchanged; what the audience reads is a named translation of each.

## What this does not decide

- The `Joint` line's ~~computation and~~ display: the assistant's proposals — the range over the six orders that respect the measured-first and reflexive-last constraints, blocks fitted jointly so that within-block order does not arise, `Joint` shown as a width beside each line rather than an additive row, and no change to the exact sum — were put and not answered here. The first was decided later the same day on [[DEC-010-declared-order-with-a-joint-line]]: the range over every admissible order, `user-confirmed`. The joint fit, the display as a width, and the receipt's treatment of it remain `llm-proposed`.
- Where the numeraire revision appears on the audience receipt.
- Whether the security-fundamentals layer of the worked example on [[attribution-ledger]] has an audience line.
- The published wording of the five lines.
