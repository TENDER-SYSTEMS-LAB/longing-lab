# DEC-002 — The form is a research house, not a trading app

## Decision

LONGING presents itself first as a **research institution**, not as a brokerage or trading application. The user set this direction explicitly: the work should feel like a research center, showing the current price and chart with analyst reports beside them. The visual register is a Bloomberg-terminal-like professional system — high information density, no onboarding, no concession to newcomers.

Marked `working` rather than `confirmed`: the user accepted the direction with "일단은 그렇게 가보자" ("let's go with that for now").

## What this settles

- **No Buy / Sell.** There is no order entry, no simulated portfolio value, no money. The one interaction offered to the viewer is a position — LONG · HOLD · SHORT — recorded and acknowledged with nothing more than `Position recorded.` See [[analyst-system]].
- **Price is the conclusion; research is the argument around it.** The institution publishes a number and does not explain it. The explanation is left to analysts who disagree.
- **Order of appearance: market data first, humans later.** The home screen leads with the index, sub-indices, movers, latest research, and dry data lines. Personal, remembered material surfaces only deeper in — inside a security page or a report — never on the landing screen.
- **Borrow the attitude, not the palette.** The reference is the posture of a professional terminal (density, codes, numbers, indifference), not Bloomberg's black-and-amber styling specifically.

## Rationale

The subject is romance and human value; the strongest treatment is to place it inside the system least equipped to handle it. A romantic subject in a romantic container (a nostalgic Instagram feed, a mood-photo channel) would be commonplace. A financial research system is the maximal contrast, and it is also the system whose language — value, decline, coverage, consensus — already claims to decide what matters.

## Formats considered and set aside

Eight sibling formats were generated from the same premise. All applied the same grammar of putting human material into an indifferent system, and each is a candidate for a future, separate work rather than part of LONGING:

- **Recruitment site for abolished jobs** (night-walk companion, letter writer, first-snow witness), ending in `This position was abolished on 17 June 2013.`
- **Government / IR-style white paper**, "2026 Romance Market Outlook", closing on the single line *romance declines as uncertainty is removed*.
- **Cloud storage archive** with storage classes, where `first_love.zip` sits in DEEP ARCHIVE, last accessed 4,812 days ago, restore estimate 7–12 years.
- **Romance as a Service**, a B2B SaaS console with pricing tiers and an API returning `410 Gone — This moment has permanently expired.`
- **Parcel tracking**, ending `Reason: the recipient is no longer the same person.`
- **A social network with no people**, where every account is a moment (`@summer_2007`, `@last_train`) and the single follower is you.
- **An archive of a radio station that never existed**, RBS 92.7 FM, hours of near-nothing, with viewers writing their own memories into the comments.

Status: `deferred`, not rejected. They are recorded here so they are not re-invented as parts of LONGING, and because the artist grammar in [[system-grammar]] treats them as possible later entries in the same series.
