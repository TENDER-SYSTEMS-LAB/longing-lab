# DEC-006 — LONGING absorbs the reserve function; no separate holding institution

## The decision

The backing layer of LONGING's world — what a romantic good rests on, and where that backing goes when it leaves — now lives **inside LONGING**, carried by two named instruments the research house covers rather than by a separate system.

The user's closing statement of the choice:

> 아니면 그냥 LONGING 안에 BOND와 TRUST를 넣는건 어때?
> 리서피 리포트에 녹여도 되고, 터미널 화면에서 확인할 수 있도록 해도 되고..

and the reason it holds:

> LONGING RESEARCH는 원래 낭만의 시세를 다루는 연구소였어.

The backing is therefore not a new subject. It is the dataset the institute was always missing. [[reserve-instruments]] owns the mechanics.

## What is confirmed

- **The reserve function belongs to LONGING.** It is not a separate system and does not require a separate repository, identifier, or public surface.
- **The instruments are BEARER BOND and BLIND TRUST.** BEARER BOND is trust between two people: unregistered, bilateral, defaultable. BLIND TRUST is trust placed in AI: contents unseeable by the depositor, one-way, non-convertible, never in default.
- **The departing trust accumulates toward AI, and the total is also falling.** Both curves are required; either alone tells a different story than the intended one.
- **Nobody in the world argues the decline.** It happens the way a real financial market moves — see the evolution note on [[DEC-004-secular-decline-with-rallies]].
- **RS-001 — THE RESERVE is concluded.** Its function is inherited here. See its own [conclusion decision](https://github.com/TENDER-SYSTEMS-LAB/the-reserve-lab/blob/main/wiki/decisions/DEC-012-system-concluded-and-function-transferred.md) and the institutional registry entry.

## What was rejected, and why it matters

Two earlier repairs were withdrawn by the user on message grounds, and both are recorded so they are not proposed again:

**Dilution.** Falling backing per issued unit produces falling prices, but reads to an audience as *love became common*. The user rejected it explicitly:

> 함량이 묽어진다고 하면 자칫 관객에게 "사랑/이해/신뢰가 흔해졌다." 라는 메시지가 전달될 수 있는데 나는 그걸 원하지는 않음.

**Withdrawal under suspicion.** A Gresham reading in which the people capable of the practice stop performing it for fear of being mistaken for counterfeit. Rejected as the wrong subject:

> "편지 쓸 줄 아는 사람이 편지를 안 씀. 오해받을까봐, 가짜들 사이에 섞일까봐." 라는 내용은 정말 내가 작품으로 전달하고 싶은 내용이 아님.

The subject the user named instead is that love and understanding between people **turn into** hatred and misunderstanding while trust disappears, and that trust moves to AI — with the explicit qualification that the work does not claim romance is right, and exists to give the audience room to think.

## The withdrawn institution

Before the fold, a separate neutral institution was designed to issue and hold both instruments, with the counter line *"BEARER BOND로 하시겠습니까, BLIND TRUST로 하시겠습니까"* as its central scene. Candidate names reached `JOINT CUSTODY`, with `BEARER ASSURANCE`, `BLIND CUSTODY`, `COMMON COUNTER`, and `GENERAL REGISTRY` as alternatives. A survey prompt for external models was drafted and **never dispatched**; no external model was consulted.

All of it is withdrawn. Two reasons were on record when the user chose the fold:

1. A research institute is the stronger neutral narrator. A custodian earns its fee whichever way the flow runs, so its neutrality is compromised by construction; an institute only observes. The even-handedness the work requires becomes structural rather than authorial.
2. An unresolved name disappears. The first word before `CUSTODY` was never settled.

The loss is the counter's choice scene, which an institute cannot stage. Its proposed recovery — a transcript in a report appendix, which the institute cannot comment on — is `llm-proposed` and unadopted.

## Resolved — the work is LONGING RESEARCH

This decision originally logged a contradiction: the user referred to the work as **LONGING RESEARCH** while [[DEC-001-project-name-longing]] recorded **LONGING** with no descriptive suffix. It was held for one turn rather than acted on.

The user then directed the rename. The work is **LONGING RESEARCH**, and the governing rule is the one stated here — two words per work in the series. DEC-001 owns the change, the precedent it reverses, and what the reversal costs. The identifier `LN-001` is unchanged.

Still open: the Lab repository is `longing-lab`, and the social accounts are recorded under `LONGING`. Neither was renamed.

## What this does not decide

No unit, numeraire, currency, coefficient, curve, historical span, factor, or listing rule. No delivery surface: the research report and the terminal are both the user's proposals and neither was chosen over the other. See [[reserve-instruments]] for the full boundary and [[Q-004-unit-of-account]] for the question this sharpens rather than answers.
