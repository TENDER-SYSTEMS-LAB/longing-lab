# DEC-016 — Securities are chosen top-down, from an ontology of what is being handed over

## The decision

Reading a summary of the decided world, the user objected to how LONGING RESEARCH's only defined security came about and set the direction for choosing the rest ([[SRC-2026-10-03-top-down-securities-and-liquidity]]):

> 1. 정의된 증권 LETTER는 LLM의 제안을 내가 받아들인 것인데 왜 해당 종목이 나왔는지, 왜 하필 해당 종목이었는지 등의 설명이 좀 부족했어.
> 2. 따라서 해당 증권을 기준으로만 세계관을 구성하기 시작하면 바텀업 방식의 작업이 되는데 난 해당 작품은 탑다운으로 가야한다고 생각함.
> 4. 증권을 결정하기 위해서는 일단 무엇이 낭만인가? 무엇이 인간적인 것인가? 무엇을 우리는 AI나 근대화에 내어지고 있는지를 분석해서 추상화해야함.
> 5. 추상화된 개념을 바탕으로 온톨로지를 고려해야함.

**`user-confirmed`:**

1. **LETTER was an LLM proposal the user accepted, and its selection was not explained well enough.** Why it came up, and why that security rather than another, was never made clear.
2. **The work is built top-down.** Constructing the world from LETTER alone would be bottom-up work, and the user rejects that direction for this work.
3. **Securities follow from analysis and abstraction.** Before any security is chosen, the work analyses and abstracts three questions: what romance is, what is human, and what we are handing over to AI and to modernization.
4. **An ontology comes from the abstracted concepts.** The securities are to be considered on the basis of that ontology.

The order is therefore: analysis → abstraction → ontology → securities.

## What this changes, as the assistant reads it

The following is the assistant's review of how the decision meets earlier records, `llm-proposed`. None of it is decided by the user.

- **LETTER is neither rejected nor the starting point.** The user did not discard LETTER. It loses its place as the reference from which the world grows, and it is to be judged against the ontology like any other candidate. The 2026-09-05 conversation's proposed order — complete `LETTER` as a single reference security before sketching a universe — was already an LLM proposal and is now superseded by this decision. [[letter-practice-dynamics]] stays a preserved, unadopted trial.
- **It agrees with the 2026-10-01 order.** The user had already said securities come later and must be chosen with great care, because they carry a very important part of the work ([[SRC-2026-10-01-weights-first-securities-later]]). This decision says how that care is exercised.
- **It answers the shape of two open gaps, not their content.** What may become a security, and what kinds of security there are, were open as an entry rule and as three unchosen directions (see [[current-state]]). Both now follow from the ontology rather than from a rule written first. The assistant's earlier caution — not to choose securities because data happens to reach them — is consistent with a top-down order and remains the assistant's wording.
- **One tension is carried forward.** The weight procedure of [[DEC-014-restoration-is-arrival-and-substitution-is-judgment]] splits a security into steps. If the ontology produces states without steps, as [[reflection]] already is, the procedure and the ontology must be reconciled. This is the open question of kinds of security, restated.
- **The top already exists in part.** Several confirmed decisions sit above any security and are the natural first inputs to the analysis: the subject, the conditions of human experience that modernization is removing ([[overview]]); the split between execution restored and judgment substituted ([[DEC-014-restoration-is-arrival-and-substitution-is-judgment]]); and the trust instruments, trust between people and trust placed in AI ([[DEC-008-bearer-bond-is-perpetual]], [[reserve-instruments]]).

## What remains open

- The analysis itself: what romance is, what is human, and what is being handed over. Not begun.
- The method of abstraction and the form of the ontology.
- Which securities follow, how many, and whether LETTER is among them.
- How states without steps receive execution and judgment weights.

## Evolution — the analysis begins from the author's view (2026-10-04)

The user started the analysis and chose its method: the author's own view first, then checked against thought ([[SRC-2026-10-04-romance-analysis-author-view]]). The author's answers to the three questions, and the assistant's abstractions of them, are on [[romance-analysis]]. The check against thought and the history of what modernization took before AI are to be researched, by the assistant directly and through a round of LLM opinions, both chosen by the user. No ontology or security follows yet.

## Evolution — the abstraction method and the ontology's form chosen (2026-10-05)

The analysis closed on 2026-10-05 with BLIND TRUST settled. The author then chose, from the assistant's options ([[SRC-2026-10-05-abstraction-method-and-ontology-form]]): the four layers origin → exchange → balances → handed over are the official frame; concepts are placed by layer and tested against the five loss layers, a concept making no difference between records becoming a ground; and the ontology is a layered graph with named relations, only the balances and BLIND TRUST marked as quantities. The second item under "What remains open" above is therefore decided. The ontology itself and the securities are still open. See [[romance-analysis]] section 10.
