# Security Universe Round — six models read the thirty candidates

The LLM round the author chose on 2026-10-05 to widen [[security-universe]] before choosing ([[SRC-2026-10-05-security-universe-method]]). Six services answered the registered prompt ([[SRC-2026-10-05-security-universe-round-prompt]]): ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek, Gemini, GLM and Grok. Qwen and Kimi were not consulted.

**Status.** This page is the assistant's synthesis, `llm-synthesis`. Every response is `llm-proposed`; nothing here is adopted. Counts below are how many of the six raised a point; they show convergence, not correctness. The decisions put to the author are at the end.

**Sources.** Only ChatGPT cites sources: seven entries marked `verified` with links (the penny post's 1839–1840 mail volumes, TTY access, Rosenfeld et al. 2019 on meeting partners online, Pew on peer health support and teen play, Be My Eyes, nineteenth-century home music and Sousa's 1906 essay). The assistant has not checked them. GLM states it cites none; the others cite none.

## 1. Critique of the thirty

### A finding against a decided rule

ChatGPT notes that "in one's own words" (candidates 4 and 5) makes **composition** the condition of origin. The author decided on 2026-10-04 that origin lies in choosing, sending and standing behind words, **not in composing them** ([[SRC-2026-10-04-romance-round-decisions]], decision 4). The wording of 4 and 5 therefore conflicts with a decided rule, whatever happens to the candidates.

## 3. Coverage

- **Restoration is the thinnest channel** — all six.
- **The thinnest balance** is belief for Gemini, DeepSeek and ChatGPT; Claude finds belief thick by count but made of one-off stranger encounters, and **trust fed almost only by declining practices**, so trust is where the decline looks most selected; GLM finds understanding to be many tickers of one act; Grok finds love thinner.
- **Nineteenth-century arrivals barely touch the list** (Claude): post reaches only 4 and transport only 9; railways, cheap print and the telegraph reach nothing, so 1811–1990 has almost no gives.
- **A structural gap in the ontology** (Claude): there is no channel for rises that are not technological — longer lives, smaller families, shorter hours, love marriage. Such rises are credited to no channel or to the wrong one.
- **Missing life:** civic and public life (5), and Claude notes the two-person rule excludes much of it by design; agriculture and shared manual work (4); rites, death and ritual (4); courtship and intimacy (3); childhood and old age as active participation (4); the receiving side of care (GLM).
- **The sense-of-loss layer is thin** (ChatGPT): fewer acts cannot show that people stopped noticing the absence.

## 4. States priced through practices

All six give carriers for waiting and serendipity. Other states raised by two or more: anticipation; anonymity or privacy; an open obligation carried between people; being expected or a promised reunion; time together with no assigned outcome; presence or full attention; voluntary exposure to another's response.

**What could go wrong — two opposite risks:**

1. **A state that moves only with its carriers adds a ticker without adding information**, and whoever picks the carriers decides its path (Claude). GLM adds that "being trusted" would be the trust balance with a lag.
2. **A state can vanish while its carriers hold** (GLM, ChatGPT, Grok): people keep writing while the reply becomes instant; calls are scheduled by a nudge; settlement tools close the open account while favours continue. Carrier volume then shows a healthy state that no longer exists.

The assistant's reading, `llm-proposed`: the author's ETF idea ([[SRC-2026-10-05-state-securities-after-the-round]]) meets risk 1 directly — a fund of carriers is exactly a state that moves only with its carriers — and does not meet risk 2 unless each state fund also holds a measure of the state itself, such as reply latency for waiting.

## 5. Decisions for the author

*2026-10-10: decisions 1 to 4 answered — see [[security-universe]] and [[SRC-2026-10-10-security-universe-round-decisions]]. In short: 1 kept as written, in tension with the origin rule; 2 reviewed one by one, 26, 22, 28 and 8 recast; 3 both sides kept; 4 kept. Decisions 5 to 7 are open.*

1. **"In one's own words."** Rephrase 4 and 5 so that composition is not the condition, as decided on 2026-10-04?
2. **Recasts.** Accept the agreed recasts of 17, 26, 22, 28 and 8; recast or drop 7 and 10?
3. **Pairs.** Merge each mirror pair (13/14, 3/19, 6/26, 12/27, 23/24) into one practice, or keep both sides?
4. **29.** Drop as a bundle, or keep for arrival coverage?
5. **New candidates.** Which of the convergent themes in section 2 enter the list?
6. **Coverage gaps.** Whether to address the missing non-technological channel, the thin nineteenth century and civic life against the two-person rule — each touches a decided rule and needs the author.
7. **States.** Waiting and serendipity, and whether a state fund needs its own measure beside its carriers.
