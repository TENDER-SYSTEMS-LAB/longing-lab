# Romance Analysis — what romance is, what is human, and what is handed over

This page holds the analysis [[DEC-016-securities-chosen-top-down]] puts before any security: analysis → abstraction → ontology → securities. It is the first stage. No ontology and no security follows from it yet.

**Method, chosen by the user (2026-10-04):** the author's own view first, then checked against thought. The user chose this from four options the assistant wrote ([[SRC-2026-10-04-romance-analysis-author-view]]). The check against thought, and the history of what modernization took before AI, are research not yet done; see `## Open`.

Attribution on this page is kept per section. The user's statements are `user-originated`; the assistant's abstractions of them are `llm-proposed` and are not accepted or rejected by the user unless a section says so.

## 1. What romance is

### The user's view, `user-originated`

- Romance is people gathering, sharing and understanding one another's thoughts, loving and trusting one another: the things that make humans most human.
- What people give and receive between them is **issuing bonds to each other**, and this is the base of romantic things. Love, understanding and trust arise only by owing each other.
- **The boundary is the origin.** Even when people gather in one place, a conversation whose source is not a person's thought is not romantic — "AI가 이렇다던데?" Letters, texts and favours are all the same: romance when human judgment and reflection are the origin, not when everything is left to AI.
- Time saved by AI and spent with people can increase romance. Thinking less, communicating less, and trusting only AI's pleasing words, so that hatred and misunderstanding grow, is not romantic.
- **Solitary reflection is romantic.** If one must count the amount of romance, it is larger when reflection is shared.

### The assistant's abstraction, `llm-proposed`

Three layers, revised after the user's answer on solitary reflection:

| Layer | What it is | What breaks it |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | A person's own judgment and reflection; the medium does not matter | Leaving the judgment to AI |
| Exchange | People issuing bonds to each other: talk, favours, giving and receiving | Communicating less |
| Balances | Love, understanding, trust and belief — what exchange accumulates | Trusting only AI's pleasing words and light rumours |

- **The condition of romance is the origin alone.** Exchange does not make something romantic; it makes the romance larger. Solitary reflection is romance at its base amount; shared reflection is more; a conversation relaying AI's answer is none.
- **AI is not the enemy as such.** What counts is which layer it takes over. This matches [[DEC-014-restoration-is-arrival-and-substitution-is-judgment]], where substituted judgment raises calls and restored execution counts as arrival, and the rule that technology raises before it takes ([[technology-waves]]).
- The layers map onto instruments already confirmed: exchange onto the BEARER BOND ([[DEC-008-bearer-bond-is-perpetual]]); trust in AI's pleasing words onto BLIND TRUST ([[reserve-instruments]]); the origin onto 사유, room to form one's own judgment ([[reflection]]).
- **Two consequences for securities, not decided:** a security defined by its medium or form — LETTER is defined as a physically handwritten message ([[letter-practice-dynamics]]) — may not fit a view in which the medium does not matter; and the unit of a security may be a layer or a kind of balance rather than a practice.

## 2. The financial market stands on belief and credit

### The user's view, `user-originated`

The origin of the financial market is mutual belief and credit, and all of the complex, mathematical financial market stands on it. The user wants this reflected in the world.

### The assistant's reading, `llm-proposed`

- **The real market and this one share a floor.** In the real market, loans, bonds and settlement stand on credit, and securities, derivatives and models stand on those. In LONGING RESEARCH, trust between people (the BEARER BOND) is the floor, exchange stands on it, and the securities, indices and research the audience sees stand on that.
- **The irony is that this market trades its own floor.** When the belief between people declines, the price is not all that falls; the floor the market stands on thins. This is the author's position of 2026-10-03, that liquidity between people dries up ([[DEC-004-secular-decline-with-rallies]]).
- **BLIND TRUST cannot be a floor.** Trust placed in AI is one-way and non-convertible, so it cannot support a market of exchange. As trust accumulates toward AI, the tradable trust shrinks.
- Volume shown beneath the price ([[DEC-015-no-listing-and-a-stopped-price-stays]]) is where the audience would see the floor thinning.

## 3. What is human

### The user's view, `user-originated`

- What a person does **with a person** is human. And, for the same act, what **a person** does is human.
- **Mistakes are human, and romantic.** Fewer mistakes through AI and technology are efficient and rational, but efficient and rational is not romance. People sometimes do irrational, inefficient things for romance: someone walks forty minutes home from school instead of a ten-minute bus ride, because the walk is forty minutes of thinking.
- Jealousy, looked at closely, is a feeling that comes from liking the other.
- **The problem is the balances decreasing, not hatred and misunderstanding as such.** Through light facts and rumours obtained from SNS and AI, love, understanding and trust between people are lost. The main problem is that love, understanding, trust and belief decrease.
- AI can talk and judge like a person. The problem is when people stop gathering and become more individualised because of it. If AI gathers people and grows critical thinking so that they reflect, that is good.

### The assistant's abstraction, `llm-proposed`

- **Two axes:** who does it (a person, or AI in the person's place) and with whom (alone, or with people). A person alone — reflection, the forty-minute walk, a mistake — is human and romantic. A person with people — a conversation sharing thought — is human and the largest romance. AI in a person's place, alone, is individualisation. AI in a person's place among people — "AI가 이렇다던데?" — is not romance even when people gather.
- **A right to inefficiency.** Romance often takes the slow, irrational path on purpose. The friction, mistakes and time technology removes may be exactly where romance lives. This echoes the early index ideas Latency, Human Friction and Unstructured Time on [[index-architecture]], which are earlier jointly developed material and are not thereby restored.
- **The work measures balances, not negative feelings.** What declines is love, understanding, trust and belief. The user added belief to the three of the first answer; whether trust and belief are distinct is not yet asked.
- **Two tests for AI:** does it gather people or scatter them; does it grow reflection or replace it.
- **Rumour links the world to the market.** Light facts and rumours erode trust between people in the world, and misreporting and rumour move prices in the market's information layer, which the user wanted modelled ([[information-quality]]).

## 4. What is handed over, and how

### The user's view, `user-originated`

- **Neither taken nor given.** Like Odysseus's men on Circe's island, intoxicated by comfort and forgetting even what they are giving up, things simply drift that way.
- YouTube, Shorts, Instagram and Reels keep supplying what people will like through algorithms; what one needs comes from the fingertips, and that is comfortable. With AI, ever more personal, personalised content is made and supplied; real-time video generation is now possible. These provide comfort.
- From the 2026-09-23 conversation ([[SRC-2026-09-23-circe-and-the-sense-of-loss]]): a line from a book the user read — "마지막에는 자기가 무엇을 잃고 있는지를 느낄 감각마저 내준다" — drawn from the year on Circe's island. The user wants the audience to ask whether thinking, feeling, expressing and exchanging for oneself is the most human thing. The user agrees with the book's line that what made people human in ancient Greece was gathering, deliberating together and going back and forth, and feels this has declined: Zoom replaces visits, and people work and talk with AI each on their own.
- **What modernization took before AI** is not something the user knows well; the user wants academic sources and other people's opinions gathered.

### The assistant's abstraction, `llm-proposed`

- **What is handed over, from the user's answers:** judgment and reflection; communication between people; gathering; the time of inefficiency, and mistakes; trust in people.
- **Loss has two layers.** The first is losing those things. The second is losing the sense that one is losing them. The work's name, LONGING, meets the second layer: a world in which longing itself stops happening.
- **The cause is comfort, not malice.** This agrees with the confirmed rule that nobody in the world argues the decline and everyone acts correctly on their own facts ([[DEC-004-secular-decline-with-rallies]]).
- **Personalisation is the opposite of gathering.** The more each person is supplied what fits them, the fewer reasons remain to be with others — the same axis as the first test for AI above.

The 2026-09-23 export also carries ChatGPT's proposals — reading Odyssey book 10 as the men reminding Odysseus of home, an archive notice that discontinues an estimate for lack of sample, an internal production sentence, and two cited studies. They are `llm-proposed`, recorded in the source, and not adopted.

## 5. The check against thought — the assistant's research, `llm-synthesis`

Two research notes by the assistant's subagents, 2026-10-04: [[SRC-2026-10-04-romance-analysis-thought-check]] (the author's eight points against about forty-five works) and [[SRC-2026-10-04-romance-analysis-modernization-history]] (eighteen waves of modernization, about 1800–2020, sixty-six works). Existence of the works was checked by web search; what each argues is paraphrased and not checked against pages. Nothing here is accepted by the user.

**Strongest support.** Inefficiency is not a defect (Weber, Borgmann, Illich, Rosa, Han, Solnit on walking); drift through comfort (Postman's Huxley-versus-Orwell foreword, Borgmann, Han); gathering and deliberation (Aristotle, Arendt, Habermas); the market standing on credit (Muldrew's early-modern economy of obligation, then Simmel, Graeber, Luhmann, Giddens); exchange as obligation (Mauss). The history note finds the movement of credit from persons to institutions and scores the best-documented loss (Muldrew, Zucker, Lauer, Giddens).

**Complications.**
- **Circe or the Lotus-eaters.** In *Odyssey* 10 Circe's victims keep their minds and weep, so they still feel the loss; forgetting the homecoming itself is the Lotus-eaters of book 9. The note proposes the two as two stages of drift.
- **Exact accounts.** Graeber supports debt as a social tie but warns that keeping exact accounts turns a relationship into a settleable deal; beneath it he sees sharing with no account kept. This bears on a work that prices relationships as bonds.
- **Balances or hostility.** Affective polarisation research (Iyengar et al. 2019) finds hostility itself rising alongside falling trust.
- **Contested evidence.** Filter bubbles, social media and youth wellbeing, and AI cognitive offloading have mixed or weak evidence; the original "Google effect" experiment did not replicate.
- **Raises before it takes is partial.** Letters, the telephone, the early internet and mobile phones first strengthened existing ties; television displaced gathering with no connecting phase. The literature frames this as stimulation versus displacement, depending on use. The confirmed rule on [[technology-waves]] stands as the author's decision; the literature does not establish it as a general law.
- **The sense of loss recurs.** Every new medium brought the same fear of loss (Marvin, Standage), a counterpoint to losing even the sense of loss.

**What the author's view lacks, as the notes read it:** uncontrollability as the general principle behind mistakes and inefficiency (Rosa); forgiveness, the write-off a bond model has no place for (Arendt); debts left unsettled on purpose (Graeber, Hyde); the distinction between trust in persons and trust in systems (Luhmann, Giddens); modernity's gains (Simmel, Habermas); society's push-back, Polanyi's double movement; and skill and attention as measurable forms of reflection (Crawford, Vallor, Bainbridge).

**Candidate concepts.** The thought-check note proposes twenty-four concepts for the ontology — among them reciprocity, non-settlement, forgiveness, promise, plurality, focal practice, device, resonance, uncontrollability, friction, attention, deskilling, cognitive debt, personal and system trust, disembedding, fictitious commodity — each with its sources. They are proposals.

## 9. BLIND TRUST settled, and three concepts borrowed (2026-10-05)

From options the assistant wrote, the author chose ([[SRC-2026-10-05-blind-trust-shakes-and-scope]]), `user-confirmed`:

- **Three concepts borrowed, as grounds rather than new rules.** *Attention as a gift* (Weil, Murdoch) grounds the BEARER BOND principal being attention: relying on someone asks for their attention, and granting a favour gives it. *Unsettled debt and promise* (Graeber, Hyde; Arendt): a relationship continues while a debt stays a little open — "다음엔 내가 살게" — and a promise is an act of issuing a bond. *The device paradigm, deskilling and cognitive offloading* (Borgmann; Bainbridge; Risko and Gilbert) form one chain — a device hides the effort, the unused capacity rusts, people hand over more — which is the mechanism of the capacity-loss layer and leads to the occasion-loss layer: no need to ask a stranger the way, so no bond is issued.
- **BLIND TRUST never refuses, but it is shaken.** "Never defaults" becomes "AI never refuses to respond". Two shakes are added: *trust events* — wrong answers, leaks, suspicions of manipulation — in which people briefly lean on people again and BEARER BOND rises; and *service retirement or replacement*, in which accumulated conversations and memory disappear and BLIND TRUST is written down at once. AI's special position is kept.
- **BLIND TRUST's scope is a rule whose counterpart changes by period.** BLIND TRUST is trust placed in a non-human counterpart that responds, decides or cares in place of a person, carrying no obligation to return and forming no relationship with a person. What divides is not who made the thing but whether the counterpart faced in that moment is a person: a bank counter faces a person, a search box does not. The counterparts are placed on the world's timeline, so the curve rises in steps across the whole history and jumps most at generative AI, the first non-human counterpart that converses. The page's examples — the pattern-programmed loom, broadcast voices, the search engine, navigation and recommendation, generative AI — are the assistant's, `llm-proposed`, and not selected.
- **"A new institution needs review" meant an organisation.** Adding a new organisation to the world needs review; nothing here adds one.

## 10. The abstraction method and the ontology's form (2026-10-05)

From options the assistant wrote, the author chose ([[SRC-2026-10-05-abstraction-method-and-ontology-form]]), `user-confirmed`:

- **The four layers are the work's official frame.** Origin → exchange → balances → handed over, first written as the assistant's abstraction in section 1 and in the ontology study note's map, is adopted as the frame of the ontology.
- **Abstraction places concepts by layer and tests them against loss.** Each concept is placed in one layer. It is then tested against the five loss layers — practice, capacity, occasion, balance, sense (section 7): a concept that makes no difference between records in at least one loss layer becomes a ground, not a part of the ontology. This is how the three concepts borrowed in section 9 were already treated.
- **The ontology is a layered graph.** Concepts are nodes placed in the four layers, joined by named relations — issue, repay, accumulate, hide, hand over. Only the balances and BLIND TRUST are marked as quantities, so rules that are not quantities, such as the origin test, stay in the ontology.

Rejected options, as the page stated them: a concept list per DEC-016 question, abstraction starting from loss alone, a classification tree, and a stock-and-flow ledger. The relation names are the assistant's examples, `llm-proposed`, and are not a closed list.

**What follows, as the assistant reads it, `llm-proposed`:** the next questions are what a security is in this graph — a node, a relation, a quantity or more than one — which restates the open question of kinds of security; how states without steps receive execution and judgment weights; and where LETTER, defined by its medium, stands against a frame in which the medium is not the condition.
