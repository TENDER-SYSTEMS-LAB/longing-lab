## The selection method — proposed

A practice enters the long list only if it passes four tests taken from the ontology:

1. **Origin.** Done well, the practice has a human origin: someone chooses and stands behind it.
2. **Issue.** It issues a BEARER BOND between two people: someone leans, someone gives attention.
3. **Exposure.** At least one handing-over channel reaches it — a device removing its occasion, or judgment substituted — or an arrival channel raises it.
4. **Loss record.** Its decline or rise would make a difference between records in at least one loss layer: practice, capacity, occasion, balance or sense.

The universe as a whole must then meet three coverage rules:

- **Every balance is fed.** Love, understanding, trust and belief each have several practices that issue into them, so an index can read each balance.
- **Every channel is present.** Device, substitution of judgment, restoration and arrival each touch several practices, so factors are not supported by two constituents.
- **Not only declining practices.** A universe chosen because each practice seems to be disappearing hard-codes the decline ([[factor-architecture-review-consensus]]). The list includes practices that arrivals raised strongly and that may keep rising for a long time.

## The definition rule — proposed

Two ways to define a practice:

- **By act**, medium-free: "writing to someone in one's own words". The medium, body and place become attributes or variants.
- **By form**: "a handwritten letter". This is how LETTER and the earlier working examples (payphone call, mixtape, film interval) were defined.

The decided rule that the medium is not the condition favours definition by act. Under it, LETTER would be the handwritten form of an act-defined security, not a security of its own, unless the author keeps form-defined securities.
