# Weight-Setting Procedure Review — seven procedures for a security's execution and judgment shares

Seven models answered [[SRC-2026-09-22-weight-setting-procedure-prompt]], the round the user set on 2026-09-21 to design *how* a security's two weights are set before deciding it themselves ([[DEC-014-restoration-is-arrival-and-substitution-is-judgment]], evolution notes). The prompt fixed five points — restoration is arrival, substitution is of judgment, a fixed pair summing to one, exposures measured separately per era, revision only under conditions (a) and (b) — and asked for a procedure in seven sections with LETTER as the worked case. **Everything on this page is `llm-proposed`.** No security's weights are set; every number a model printed was marked illustrative by that model and is reproduced only to show the spread. The user's condition on the outcome — *very carefully and precisely* — is the standard the round was asked to meet, and the synthesis below is organised around whether it does.

## Scope and source quality

All seven responses are complete. Word counts run from about 1,600 (Qwen) to 4,400 (ChatGPT). ChatGPT alone used web search and cites four documents; it alone omits Objections and alone withholds any illustrative pair. Gemini and Grok give no cut-off date, which the prompt made conditional; five models do. Gemini uses fenced flowcharts against the *no code* rule and gives four failure modes where the others give seven to ten; Qwen also gives four. GLM closes with a claim about a working copy at a path outside the response, which is a capture artefact and nothing more. Qwen's escalation ends in a "majority vote of the Security Custodian", one office voting, which reads as an unrepaired draft. Detailed capture notes per model are in `raw/sources.md`.

## Where the seven agree

Agreement across seven independent responses is evidence about the procedure's shape, not a decision.

- **The unit is a step of the canonical occasion, induced from the definition text.** All reject the whole practice and the single occasion as units. All produce a finite, listable decomposition for LETTER.
- **The sorting test needs a consequence qualifier.** Every model takes or restates the record's candidate — *did the step handed over contain more than one live option* — and adds that the options must differ in the practice's *product* or its meaning, so that pen, paper, route and handwriting style are never live options. Manner is not judgment.
- **The three hard cases sort the same way in all seven.** Voicing a chosen sentence and reading in a learned voice are execution; the restoring side goes to arrival exposure. The translated greeting is judgment substituted. The interpretation read before the original changes no weight; whether it is a labelled judgment unit or no unit at all is the only split.
- **Failure-frequency weighting is rejected by all**, and time weighting by six of seven, on the same ground: both drift with the era and would breach the fixed-weight point.
- **No bare pair reaches the user.** All put a decomposition with per-step reasons in front of the user; all use at least two classifiers working independently; five state that the classifiers must not see prices.
- **One pair for all three markets**, six of seven; a confirmed cross-market disagreement is a definition problem, fixed by harmonising wording or splitting the listing, never by averaging.
- **Old readings are never overwritten.** All seven; the revision record names the condition, both pairs, the evidence and the effective date; outcome fit is barred as evidence, which is condition (c) restated as a rule.
- **The hardest failure leaves no trace.** Five rank first an error at the level of the definition or the construct — under-registered judgment, shared anachronism, the definition written to fit a desired pair — precisely because the paper trail stays internally consistent.

## Where they split, and what the user would be choosing

**The counting rule — three families.** This is the choice the round did not resolve.

| Family | Models | What it assumes | What it distorts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Equal count per step | DeepSeek, GLM; Claude at the clause level | Granularity is the only lever, so the definition's wording decides the pair | Over- or under-cutting steps moves the pair; a step that matters little counts as much as one that decides the letter |
| Importance weighting | ChatGPT (100-point budget), Gemini (criticality 1–5), Grok (definitional load 1–3) | Steps differ in how much of the practice they carry, and that difference can be read from the definition alone | The weighting is a second judgment layered on the first; Grok stops the procedure if its weighted pair and the equal-count pair differ by more than 0.10 |
| Time weighting | Qwen alone | The median minutes a competent practitioner spent per step in 1996 | Six models say this is era-bound by construction; Qwen ranks it first among its own failure modes |

**Tolerance for repeatability.** 0.02 (ChatGPT), 0.05 (Claude, DeepSeek, Gemini, Grok), exact reproduction with a published band (GLM), none stated (Qwen).

**Steps that are partly both.** Claude and Grok split on a quarter grid, default ½; GLM allows a declared mixed step at ½ inside a published band; DeepSeek forbids mixed units and defaults an unsplittable one to judgment; Qwen never splits and assigns by primary bottleneck; ChatGPT rejects any automatic fraction and would rather keep both classifications on file than manufacture a compromise.

**How much the user may touch.** GLM is strictest: the user's levers are *definitional only*, amend the definition or resolve a named ambiguity, never edit a label or a number. Claude and Grok have the user rule on contested items, after which the pair follows mechanically. DeepSeek allows an override with reasons; Qwen a numeric override of up to ±0.05. Votes are accepted by Qwen and Gemini and rejected by GLM and ChatGPT.

**Cross-market detection of condition (b).** Thresholds run from 0.02 to 0.10. Claude and ChatGPT contradict each other directly: for Claude, two markets labelling the same unit differently satisfies (b) even when the pairs coincide; for ChatGPT it does not, and is an audit warning only. Qwen alone starts with one pair per market; Gemini alone allows market-specific pairs as an outcome of (b).

**Restating history after a revision.** A clearly labelled parallel series is allowed by five (ChatGPT for (b) only, Claude, DeepSeek, GLM, Grok for study only); Gemini and Qwen never restate.

## The LETTER decompositions

| Model | Steps | Illustrative execution / judgment |
|---|---|---|
| GLM | 4 — select, compose, form, release, each anchored to a phrase of the definition | 0.50 / 0.50 |
| Qwen | 4 — conception, composition, physical production, dispatch | 0.25 / 0.75 |
| DeepSeek | 5 — recipient, content, wording, handwrite, release | 0.40 / 0.60 |
| ChatGPT | 7 responsibilities under 5 parents, including *authorise release* as judgment | none given |
| Gemini | 7, including transit and handover as a step | 0.26 / 0.74 |
| Claude | 9 units under 5 clauses, transit flagged as the route's | 0.50 / 0.50 |
| Grok | 10, from occasioning to releasing, with *revising-in-ink* as the mixed case | 0.45 / 0.55 |

Every model puts recipient choice and composition on the judgment side and inscription and release on the execution side. The illustrative execution share runs from 0.25 to 0.50, and the whole of that range is produced by three choices the definition does not settle: whether releasing contains a judgment, whether carriage is a step at all, and which counting rule is used. That is the round's clearest finding about *precisely*: the pair is only as precise as the definition sentence, and the sentence LETTER now has leaves a quarter of the scale open.

## What the round says about the fixed points

Objections were optional. Claude and Qwen object to the second fixed point: Claude that the interpretation-read-before-the-original case is neither substitution nor arrival and so "the model gives it no reading", though it may be the late period's most characteristic change; Qwen that the boundary between doing and choosing is porous, with audience filtering as its example. Gemini objects to the third, that thirty-year immutability strains when a wave redefines the practice. DeepSeek objects to condition (b), as the point most likely to create false revisions if applied mechanically. GLM and Grok object to their own designs — that the procedure measures the rigour of definition-drafting rather than the practice, and that a test fed by the definition sentence is circular in principle — and accept the cost. ChatGPT states none but notes a *harder limit*: a defective pair that agrees across markets under an unchanged definition has no eligible revision trigger.

## Provenance limits

ChatGPT's citations — a NASA systems-engineering process document, the OECD/JRC composite-indicator handbook, GOV.UK Green Book guidance on multi-criteria analysis, and the GRRAS reporting guidelines — are unverified here and are precedent for method, not evidence about the practice. Claude and Grok assert facts about Korean and Japanese greeting and honorific practice; these are claims from training, not sourced. Qwen assumes a 1996 median duration per step is measurable; nothing in the record supports that. Every mechanism name on this page — responsibility ledger, anchor phrase, definitional load, criticality weight, canonical step — is one model's coinage and carries no standing.

## What this round does not decide

- The counting rule, the tolerance, and the treatment of mixed steps — the three places the seven split — are the user's to choose, after the synthesis the user said they would make.
- Whether the user's levers are definitional only, or extend to labels and numbers.
- Whether LETTER's definition sentence is rewritten before its weights are set; the round makes it plain that the pair cannot be more precise than the sentence.
- Anything about exposure, which the companion round [[exposure-sources-review]] collects.
