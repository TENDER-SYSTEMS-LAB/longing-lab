# Exposure Sources Review — twelve collections of data that might measure execution restored and judgment substituted

Twelve responses from seven models answered [[SRC-2026-09-22-exposure-sources-brainstorm-prompt]], the collection round the user set on 2026-09-21 for the per-era exposures of [[DEC-014-restoration-is-arrival-and-substitution-is-judgment]]: how far the technology of a year gave back the *doing* of a practice, and how far it took over the *deciding*. Five models were run twice on the same prompt. The brief asked for breadth, a status per row, and a first sort by quantity and quality without selection, with LETTER as the worked case. **This page pools the twelve into one inventory and records where they conflict. It selects nothing.** Every row is `llm-proposed`; a source is *verified* only in the sense that a model said so, and the round's own failure mode — an invented series — is present in it, as the provenance section below records. The narrowing by quantity and quality that the user set as the next step has not been done.

## Scope and source quality

Word counts run from about 2,500 (Qwen, second run) to 6,800 (ChatGPT). Three files claim live web verification — ChatGPT with 38 footnoted URLs and a marker on every post-cut-off item, and both Grok runs — and one partly (DeepSeek, second run); the other eight say they did not check online. ChatGPT alone notices that 1996–2026 inclusive is 31 years and 93 cells, and reports its counts against 90 with 2026 apart. Every file has all five sections; Gemini's first run and Qwen's first run sort by category rather than by row. The second runs are not revisions: for Claude, DeepSeek, Gemini and Grok the two runs list largely different sources under the same cut-off statement, and Qwen's second run replaces an official-statistics spine with vendor series. This matters for weighing agreement: a source named in both runs of one model is still one model's knowledge.

Two mis-pastes at delivery — a Qwen file first supplied as Gemini's second run, then a procedure-round GLM file supplied under the same name — were caught by hash before registration and never registered; `raw/sources.md` records them.

## What the round found, in one paragraph

No source in any market observes either exposure for LETTER as the world defines it — personally composed, physically handwritten, addressed to one person, released through a route. What exists observes adoption, ownership, volume or capability, and the exposure sits two to five inference steps behind it. The material nearest to the ALS reference case — relay-service minutes, speech-generating-device claims, voice-banking programmes, assistive-technology grants — restores speech, typing or calling, not handwriting: "the right kind and the wrong object", in Grok's second run. Judgment substituted before the generative-AI era is unmeasured in all twelve files; the pre-2023 proxies are printed card verse, Japanese card-printing software, letter-writing manuals and telegram menu phrases. Korea is the thinnest market in ten files. The instrument that would close the gap is described in nearly the same words by eight files and exists nowhere: a household or per-message diary recording who composed, who produced the marks, who sent, and what assistance was used.

**The boundary.** Every file fills Section 3, and the acts named are the same twelve times: predictive text and IME, suggested replies, AAC stored phrases against spelled or generated ones, the language-model share in a speech neuroprosthesis, voice banking against voice cloning, machine translation, summarisation, dictation with auto-punctuation, handwriting recognition, printed cards with a handwritten line. What would tell restoration from substitution is also nearly the same across files, and is nowhere collected: the length of the accepted span relative to the message; the edit distance between what the system proposed and what was sent; the ratio of input to output tokens in a voice or AAC device; whether a banked message was the person's own or written for them; and, for AAC, "who authored the stored phrase" (ChatGPT). The BCI papers' own ablations — word error with and without the language model — are the one place the boundary has actually been measured.

## The first sort, and where the files disagree

On **quantity**, the files agree that patents, connectivity indicators, postage tariffs and postal volumes fill the grid and that nothing assistive or decision-level fills more than about a third of it. Four files break from that: Qwen's second run gives assistive-device deployments, relay minutes and e-greeting volumes 70 to 80 of 90; Gemini's runs give clinical registries and stylus sales 40 to 84; Qwen's first run gives national surveys about 70. In each case the cell count rests on a series the other files say does not exist in that form.

On **quality**, the fewest-step sources by rough agreement are relay minutes and device claims for execution, and the decision-level platform classifications for judgment — the Smart Reply share, the OpenAI editing-versus-generating split, the Anthropic index, the CoAuthor sessions, the Moonpig attach rate, the OHSU stored-phrase item — each a point or a handful of cells.

## The gaps, where the twelve agree

- **Korea.** Ten files name it the thinnest market: no household mail diary, no card census, no AAC panel, no time series for the relay before about 2005, no Korean AI-draft poll. Japan is weak for disability-disaggregated ICT use and AAC panels; the United States has the weakest official split of personal from transactional mail.
- **Before 2005.** Thin for everything except postal totals, tariffs, ICT penetration and patents; the judgment-substituted gap runs to about 2014 in three files and to 2022 in one.
- **Judgment substituted before the AI era.** All twelve. Decision-level tagging begins 2024–2025.
- **Execution restored for LETTER's own act.** Seven files say it outright; the rest by their rows. Handwriting restored by an aid at population scale is observed nowhere.
- **The ideal instrument**, in the files' words: a diary item on who chose the words in the last letter sent (Claude); "who decided what to say, how, and who produced the marks" as three survey items (DeepSeek, second run); an annual postal characteristics study flagging handwritten, printed or hybrid and personal or transactional (Grok, second run); a yearly barrier-attributed adoption survey asking whether the tool restored something lost or sped up what one could already do (GLM); a per-message provenance record from intent to dispatch, which "is the ideal series. Not published." (Grok, second run).

## Provenance warnings

**The round's failure mode is in the pool.** Four files — both Qwen runs and both Gemini runs — mark as *verified* annual series that the other files say do not exist, and several of these are, on the reviewers' reading, invented or conflated: Tobii Dynavox and Prentke Romich unit reports for three markets from 1996; inmate e-messaging series from Japanese and Korean justice ministries; an annual NHIS assistive-technology module 1996–2026; an ALS Association registry of AAC prescriptions; "NIPA Korean Language IT Usage Reports"; AI-generation transparency reports from Meta, LINE and Kakao; an ASHA registry reporting a technology-restoration percentage; a "National AAC & AT Utilization Database"; a Japanese postal statistic separating handwritten correspondence; garbled Japanese product and practice names. The pattern is consistent: a real holder, a plausible title, and a span stretched to the whole grid. GLM's count for a Japanese Google Books corpus is unsupported because no such corpus exists. Grok's second run carries several 2026 items attested only by its own search. ChatGPT names nothing the reviewers would call invented and marks every unverified field with a question mark. None of this is verified here; the narrowing step must begin by checking existence, not by comparing cell counts.

**Two runs are not two witnesses.** A source that appears in both runs of a model counts once. Agreement on this page is counted by model where it matters.

**Status words do not mean the same thing across files.** *Verified* ranges from "I am confident it exists with that name" (Claude, stated) to a mark on a series the file has projected forward to 2026 (Gemini, second run, stated).

## What this round does not decide

- Which sources are carried into the narrowing by quantity and quality, which the user set as the next step and which has not begun.
- Whether an exposure is measured from statistics at all, or derived from the wave curves on [[technology-waves]]; the round found nothing that measures either exposure for LETTER directly, which bears on that choice.
- Whether the four datasets already on [[data-sources]] survive; all four reappear here among many others.
- Anything about the weights, which the companion round [[weight-setting-procedure-review]] treats.
