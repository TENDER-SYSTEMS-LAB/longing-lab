# Prior Art and Differentiation

Comparable works surfaced when the user asked whether anything similar had already been published. No work was found matching LONGING exactly — a mobile financial service that lists disappearing human practices as securities and ties their long-term decline to personal memory. But the central device, pricing feeling like a stock market, has direct precedent.

All items below were gathered by web search inside the source conversation and are recorded here as reported there. They have not been independently verified in this repository.

## Closest precedent — Maurice Benayoun, *Emotional Stock Exchange (e-stock)*, 2005

A French media artist's project the artist himself describes as a stock market of the world's emotions: internet data analyzed for emotions attached to roughly 3,200 cities, with states like `insecure`, `ecstatic`, `alone` quoted in real time. Part of the larger series *The Mechanics of Emotions*, which extended into emotion maps, markets, vending machines, and forecasts, treating the internet as a nervous system for the world.

So the chain — emotion → data → price → the language of finance — already exists.

- https://digitalartarchive.at/database/work/2152/
- https://www.streamingmuseum.org/post/maurice-benayoun
- https://www.benayoun.com/lebazar/e-stock/index.htm

## Emeka Ogboh, *The Way Earthly Things Are Going*, 2017

Live global market data on an LED ticker set against an ancient Greek lament, shown at documenta 14. Not emotion-as-stock, but the same aesthetic contrast LONGING is built on: an indifferent financial system beside intensely personal feeling.

- https://jamescohan.viewingrooms.com/artworks/26291-emeka-ogboh-the-way-earthly-things-are-going-2017/

## Lise Autogena & Joshua Portway, *Black Shoals Stock Market Planetarium*, c. 1999

The stock market rendered as a night sky: each company a star that brightens when traded, price correlations acting as gravity until firms drift into constellations. The transferable lesson is the method — **keep the mechanics of the market intact and change only what they refer to.** Applied here: companies → romances, price → residual value, volume → how many people did it today, market cap → romance's share of the whole society, delisting → a practice no longer performed, IPO → a newly born romance, index → the total romance of an era.

- https://www.blackshoals.net/the-project

## Cream on Chrome, *The Environmentalist Stock Exchange*, STRP Festival 2022

A fictional exchange where visitors invest in species and ecosystem services instead of companies, with investment causing those organisms to flourish in a simulation. Structurally the nearest relative: **listing what the market does not contain, in order to question its value.** Substitute romance for ecology.

- https://en.artoffice.info/kunstenaar/jonas-althaus/

## Soun-Gui Kim, *Stock Garden*, 2022 / *Stock Garden — Temple*, 2023

A Korean artist who has explored finance, traditional markets, ways of living, and capitalism since the 1990s; the Carnegie International work combines live market indices with market scenery.

- https://carnegieart.org/people/soun-gui-kim/

## How LONGING differs

Benayoun asks *what is the world feeling right now?*, so his data is real time. LONGING asks a different question:

> How much of what we once considered important is still left?

That shifts the work from **measuring emotion** to **recording disappearance**, which is why prices are shown across decades (1998 / 2008 / 2018 / 2026) rather than as a live tick.

Two consequences are treated as binding:

1. **Abstract emotions must not be securities.** `LOVE ▲2.4%`, `LONELINESS ▼3.1%`, `HOPE ▲1.2%` is explicitly rejected — it lands almost on top of *Emotional Stock Exchange*. Securities are units of behavior, situation, culture, and memory instead: a payphone call to someone you like, a handwritten letter, waiting without contact, the last train, talking until dawn, a window on a rainy day, a drive with no destination, making someone a mixtape, the interval before film is developed. See [[index-architecture]].
2. **A second inversion is the differentiator.** In a market, a collapsing price marks a worthless asset. Here, the further value falls in the market, the more precious the thing becomes to the person — surfaced as personal disclosures beneath a decimated quote. See [[system-grammar]].
