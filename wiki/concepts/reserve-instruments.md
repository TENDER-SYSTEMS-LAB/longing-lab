# Reserve Instruments — what backs a romantic good

This page owns the backing layer of LONGING's world: what the traded conditions rest on, why the quoted price falls, and where the backing goes when it leaves. It absorbs the function previously assigned to the separate RS-001 system. See [[DEC-006-reserve-function-absorbed]].

It does not own the market model, the factor architecture, or the index. Those stay in [[pricing-model]], [[index-architecture]], and [[attribution-ledger]]. What this page adds is the reason the model's drift term has a direction at all.

## The originating premise and its defect

The user's opening formulation was that the romantic things traded in LONGING rest on a value stored elsewhere; that letters are exchanged because of love, tolerance of a late reply, and trust that a reply will come; and that the essence of those three is unchanged while their quantity falls, so prices fall with them.

The premise does not by itself produce falling prices, and the reason is worth keeping on record because it constrains every later fix:

- If the traded goods are themselves made of the backing, both sides of the ratio shrink together and the quoted price is flat. A price movement requires the two to shrink at **different rates**.
- Under strict convertibility a shrinking reserve forces issuance down, which makes each remaining unit scarcer and its price **rise**.
- Falling prices therefore require either thinning backing per unit, or a demand-side fall. The first reads to an audience as *love became common*, which the user explicitly rejected as a message.

The dilution repair was withdrawn on that ground. What replaced it is on the rest of this page.

## Sign reversal, not depletion — `llm-proposed`

Love does not vanish into zero; it turns into its opposite. The proposed accounting reading is a balance-sheet sign flip rather than a drawdown:

| Human quantity | Becomes | Balance-sheet reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Love | Hatred | Asset becomes liability |
| Understanding | Misunderstanding | Credit extended becomes a non-performing loan |
| Trust | Distrust | Reserve becomes insolvency |

The resulting world is not an empty vault but a vault holding debt, which is the worse of the two: transacting in it deepens the hole. The user's own statement of intent — that love and understanding between people turn into hatred and misunderstanding while trust disappears — is `user-confirmed`; this accounting expression of it is the assistant's.

## Where the backing goes — `user-confirmed`

The user confirmed that the departing trust accumulates **toward AI**, and that the **total is also falling**. These are two different curves and the work needs both, because either one alone reads as a different story:

- **AI share** — rising, converging on effectively all of it.
- **Total stock** — falling.

Late in the history the holder of nearly everything holds less than an earlier loser did. Showing only the share reads as *AI took it*; showing only the total reads as *it all disappeared*. Neither is the intended subject.

## Why the total falls — `jointly-developed`

Transfer alone does not reduce a total, so the decline needs a mechanism. Three were proposed and the user accepted the turn containing them as a direction; no parameter, rate, or curve was selected.

1. **Credit creation stops.** Trust between people behaved like fractional-reserve banking: circulating it created trust that had not previously existed, because being trusted produces trust in return. Trust placed in AI is unilateral — the counterparty has no need to trust back — so it is a mattress rather than a bank. Natural attrition continues while new issuance ends. This is the primary mechanism, and its value to the work is that it explains decline with **no theft and no villain**: AI does not take anything, it simply returns nothing.
2. **Non-convertibility.** Understanding received from AI cannot be spent on a person. This is the precise condition under which *accumulating there* and *total falling* hold at the same time.
3. **Depreciation.** Tolerance of being misunderstood behaves like a muscle, not a stock. Unexercised, what falls is the **limit**, not the balance.

## AI's institutional position — `llm-proposed`

Proposed as a **clearing house** rather than a competing bank. It does not merely hold the deposited trust; it lends it back as mediation — drafting the letter, softening the message, interpreting the other person's intent.

The consequence is that two people can still love each other while the transaction clears through a third party. Direct bilateral settlement disappears, and neither side accumulates any reserve against the other. Nobody left the market; people simply stopped settling directly.

This also supplies the price mechanism the original premise lacked: the quoted price of a romantic good falls because it carries **counterparty risk**, and the counterparty is a person. The discount is not dilution of the backing. It is the credit rating of the issuer.

## The two instruments — `user-confirmed` names, `llm-proposed` mechanics

Named and accepted by the user as a pair. As instruments inside the institute's coverage they are contract terms, and are supposed to read as contract terms.

### BEARER BOND

Trust between two people. A bearer instrument has no register, so it exists only between the parties, cannot be observed by any custodian, and cannot be reconstructed once lost. *Bear* carries the other readings the world needs: to endure, to carry, and to give birth — the last being credit creation.

Bilateral by construction, issuable, and **defaultable**. High yield against a real probability of loss.

### BLIND TRUST

Trust placed in AI. A blind trust is one whose contents the depositor cannot see, which states the black box as a matter of institutional form rather than as an accusation. One-way and non-convertible are built into the word.

Never defaults. Low yield, no risk.

The pair is the world's central comparison: a junk bond against a risk-free asset. **Everyone who moved was right to move.** If any individual case is drawn as foolish, the work acquires an argument it does not want.

### Withdrawn in the same conversation

`JOINT CUSTODY`, `BEARER ASSURANCE`, `BLIND CUSTODY`, `COMMON COUNTER`, and `GENERAL REGISTRY` belonged to a separate holding institution that would have issued both instruments. That institution is withdrawn — see [[DEC-006-reserve-function-absorbed]]. They are recorded here only so the names are not proposed again as current.

## The letter, restated — `llm-proposed`

The letter is a credit instrument. Sending one extends credit; the reply settles it; a late reply is a maturity extension, and it requires understanding to grant. Silence is default.

The scene this produces turns on one asymmetry:

| Reader's reserve | A late reply reads as |
| --- | --- |
| Positive | Something must have happened — maturity extended |
| Negative | I am being ignored — default signal |

The event does not change. The letter is the same letter and the delay has the same cause. **Only the reader's balance is different.** Alongside it, the AI channel answers in under a second and has no maturity at all.

This supersedes nothing in [[letter-practice-dynamics]]; that page's cohort, panel, qualifying-dispatch and coefficient trial remain unadopted, and nothing here supplies a count.

## How a market of this kind dies — `llm-proposed`

The user's constraint is that nobody argues the decline and nobody drives it: it happens the way a real financial market moves. The proposed consequence is that the market must not crash. It dries up, in order:

1. **Volume thins first.**
2. **The spread widens** — the price a sender wants and the price a reader will pay move apart.
3. **Quotes remain and nothing fills.**

For letters: writers exist, readers exist, and nothing settles, because the two sides no longer want the same price. There is no collapse scene and no last trade announcement.

A related consequence: **nobody sees the top.** The peak of human trust was an ordinary Tuesday on which nothing happened, visible only afterwards and only in a chart. This is how the work can deliver loss without mourning it aloud.

## Keeping the institute from arguing — `llm-proposed`

Four devices, all structural, recorded because the user asked for them and none confirmed:

- **Individually right, collectively falling.** Show case files in which every choice is correct, then the aggregate table. The gap between them is the work; no sentence has to close it.
- **The institute does not know the cause.** Publish competing hypotheses side by side and settle none — transfer to AI, credit-creation halt, and measurement error, the last holding that the decline is an artefact of a ledger denominated in human-trust units. An institution that genuinely does not know puts the ambiguity into the structure instead of into authorial restraint.
- **Close on a model revision, not a eulogy.** The previous period's model understated the decline, so the coefficient is adjusted.
- **The revision history is the spine.** Reports are periodic and each marks the forecast down. Nobody says anything and the number keeps shrinking.

The measurement-error hypothesis is the one place the work stays genuinely open: a ledger written in human-trust units structurally cannot record value accruing outside those units. Leave it unresolved. Confirming it comforts; denying it preaches.

The failure mode of all four is flatness, and the proposed antidote is **precision rather than argument** — dates, case numbers, decimal places. `BEARER BOND new issues 3,112, −18.4% year on year` carries further than *trust declined*.

## Evolution — the instrument is perpetual, and it leaves by call (2026-09-20)

This page defines BEARER BOND as bilateral, issuable and defaultable and leaves
its mechanics `llm-proposed`. [[DEC-008-bearer-bond-is-perpetual]] fills them in,
and one of them changes the shape of the decline recorded above.

The bond has **no maturity**. What is outstanding is attention placed in another
person, and people do not form relationships with a term in mind. The coupon
carries the only deadline, and it is deferrable — which is this page's own
sentence about a late reply requiring understanding, written as a contract term.

The decline is therefore made of **calls**, not defaults. The issuer redeems early
because they can do alone what once required asking. That is why *credit creation
stops* above is arithmetic rather than metaphor: AI is what makes the call
available, so using it repays what was owed to people, and BLIND TRUST fills from
redemption rather than from breach.

Default survives and is no longer the main channel. Nobody has to be betrayed for
the total to fall.

## What this does not settle

- No unit, numeraire, or currency. The backing layer does not answer [[Q-004-unit-of-account]]; it sharpens it, because a price is now explicitly a ratio between a romantic good and something else that must be named.
- No coefficient, rate, curve shape, historical span, or date series. The two curves are directions, not a schedule.
- No listing, delisting, or relisting rule. The drying-up sequence describes how quotation ends in this world, which bears on [[Q-002-listing-lifecycle]] without answering it.
- No factor. Whether transfer-to-AI becomes a published factor line, a macro variable, or stays outside the ledger is untouched. See [[attribution-ledger]] and [[index-architecture]].
- No claim about real AI systems, real trust, or any measured quantity. Every number in this world is authored.

## Evolution — AI care is BLIND TRUST (2026-10-04)

In the romance analysis the author decided that care received from AI is BLIND TRUST: it comes from a non-human being, carries no obligation to return, and forms no relationship with a person ([[SRC-2026-10-04-romance-study-decisions]], `user-confirmed`). In the same analysis the author fixed the market's floor as a shift in form from chosen belief between people to confidence in the system built on it, and asked for a review of whether BLIND TRUST's scope should widen from AI to systems in general ([[romance-analysis]]). The scope is not changed.

## Evolution — BLIND TRUST stays AI, and may change by period (2026-10-04, later)

Asked whether BLIND TRUST should widen from AI to systems in general, the author decided ([[SRC-2026-10-04-romance-remaining-decisions]], `user-confirmed`): BLIND TRUST points to trust in AI for now, because systems are made between people and their root is a human origin; AI's special position is kept. The author wants a BLIND TRUST curve across the whole history from the Luddites and so wants a scope that differs by period considered, and thinks "never defaults" may need fixing, to be discussed. Neither change is made yet.

## Evolution — BLIND TRUST never refuses but is shaken, and its counterpart changes by period (2026-10-05)

From options the assistant wrote, the author decided ([[SRC-2026-10-05-blind-trust-shakes-and-scope]], `user-confirmed`):
- **"Never defaults" becomes "never refuses to respond".** Two shakes are added: trust events (wrong answers, leaks, suspicions of manipulation), in which people briefly lean on people again and BEARER BOND rises; and service retirement or replacement, in which accumulated conversations and memory disappear and the balance is written down at once.
- **The scope is a rule:** trust placed in a non-human counterpart that responds, decides or cares in place of a person, with no obligation to return and no relationship formed. The test is whether the counterpart faced in that moment is a person, not who made it. The counterpart changes by period on the world's timeline, so BLIND TRUST has a curve across the whole history, rising most at generative AI, the first counterpart that converses. AI's special position is kept. Which counterpart belongs to which period is not selected.

The definition above — "never defaults; low yield, no risk" — is the earlier one.
