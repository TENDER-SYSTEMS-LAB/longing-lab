- **LONGING RESEARCH owns the backing layer.** What a romantic good rests on is no longer held by a separate system. It is carried by two instruments the institute covers: **BEARER BOND**, trust between two people — unregistered, bilateral, defaultable — and **BLIND TRUST**, trust placed in AI — contents unseeable, one-way, non-convertible, never in default. - **A BEARER BOND is perpetual, and what it measures is attention.** The principal is the reliance one person has placed in another — a balance, not a count of relationships or of unanswered letters — and both directions of a pair stay outstanding gross. The one who leans is the issuer; no institution issues anything. There is no maturity: the decline is made of **calls**, the issuer redeeming early because they can now do alone what once required asking. AI is what makes the call available, so using it repays what was owed to people. Write-downs answer the counterparty with a severity that varies by person, and the severe tail is where melt-ups come from. See [[DEC-008-bearer-bond-is-perpetual]].
- **Technology raises before it takes.** A wave's arrival phase creates modes of connection that did not exist and substitution overtakes it only later, so every wave contributes a rise followed by a decline. The scheduled revival era is retired; the long advances are structural. The span is thirty years, 1996–2026, and the driver is measured where national statistics exist and extrapolated beyond, with the institute publishing the boundary. See [[technology-waves]].
- **Shorts are not the only thing that lifts a price.** Positioning is signed and follows a trend, so an arrival phase attracts real buyers and a crowded long liquidates when the revival ends — which gave the market a downside mechanism it had never had. Advances before 2000 are driven by arrival; every advance after 2010 is short covering. See [[loop-simulation]].
