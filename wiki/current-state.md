# Current State

One source is registered: a single concept conversation of 2026-09-04 that established the work from a blank start.

## Confirmed

- **The work is named LONGING**, capitals, no suffix. Stated directly by the user. Sub-brands (Terminal, Markets, Research, News, Data, Archive) exist inside the fiction, never in the title. See [[DEC-001-project-name-longing]].
- **The work is a fictional independent research house**, not a trading app: current price and chart with analyst reports beside them, in a Bloomberg-terminal-like professional register. No Buy / Sell. See [[DEC-002-research-house-form]].
- **The subject is the conditions of human experience that modernization is removing** — not "romance" as a category. See [[overview]].
- **The system never argues.** It publishes numbers and reports; opposed views settle into a price; emotion happens in the viewer.

## Working

- **Index architecture**, accepted provisionally ("let's go with that for now"): flagship LNGI over six sub-indices — Latency, Serendipity, Human Friction, Materiality, Unstructured Time, Unmediated Interaction — with Efficiency, Optimization, Automation, Predictability, and Mediation shown alongside as contrast indicators. Tickers and composition are first-pass. See [[index-architecture]].
- **Analyst system**: opposed analysts publishing LONG / HOLD / SHORT on the same security, each a coherent theory of what counts as value; four sketched (Eleanor Vale, Adrian Kessler, Mina Seo, Julian Hart); persistent track records; polite unnamed rebuttals; the viewer as a fifth analyst whose accumulated positions form a portfolio of their own values. See [[analyst-system]].
- **Series grammar** shared with OTHER GOODS: human things processed by systems never made for them — *Unownable → Goods* there, *Unquantifiable → Price* here. See [[system-grammar]].
- **A quiet bias toward the human, expressed structurally** rather than through characterization: the most mechanical analyst is usually right, and his accuracy is what drives the index down. Acknowledged by the user but not explicitly decided. See [[Q-003-calibrating-the-bias]].
- **Securities are behaviors and situations, never abstract emotions** — the deliberate break from *Emotional Stock Exchange*. See [[prior-art]].

## Unknown

- **How any price or index value is produced** — mechanism, data basis, and unit are all unanswered, and the index formula depends on it. See [[Q-001-price-formation]].
- **Listing lifecycle**: who lists, whether scarcity raises or lowers price, delisting, where a delisted romance goes, whether one person resuming a practice relists it. See [[Q-002-listing-lifecycle]].
- **How far the bias may go** before the work stops asking and starts arguing. See [[Q-003-calibrating-the-bias]].
- **Flagship index ticker** (`LNGI` / `LX` / `LCI`) and the security-code scheme.
- **Trademark, domain, and existing-work collision check on the name LONGING** — planned in the source conversation, never carried out.
- **Medium and delivery**: mobile-first was the starting assumption, but no platform, technology, or exhibition context has been decided.

## Next steps proposed in the source

In order: index system and formulas → analyst personas (two to four) → the first covered securities → the information architecture of the LONGING Research mobile home screen. Proposed by the LLM at the close of the conversation; not confirmed by the user.

## Deferred

Seven sibling formats built on the same grammar — an abolished-jobs recruitment site, a government-style romance white paper, a cold-storage memory archive, Romance as a Service, parcel tracking, a social network of moments, and the archive of a radio station that never existed. Recorded as possible future works, not as parts of LONGING. See [[DEC-002-research-house-form]].
