**Readings and assumptions**, stated up front because the specification is silent on them and the rules forbid filling gaps silently:

- **A1.** The 33-security universe is in force for the entire span, 1996–2026. The round fixes the universe; I take the triple as effective from the first strike.
- **A2.** I read the ledger's "nine factor lines" as its *total* attribution lines — numeraire revision, substitution exposure, priced factors, positioning, reflexive component, Unexplained — because the specification enumerates that list and then gives the count. Under the alternative reading (nine *priced* factors) the answer differs, and I state where in Section 3.
- **A3.** The panel is balanced: every practice is listed in every market, every week. Float declines; listings do not suspend or delist. (The specification gives float redemption, not delisting. A3 is load-bearing; failures are noted where they matter.)
- **A4.** External observables exist and are published: the companion frozen-yardstick index; a declared wave-exposure schedule at practice×market granularity (arrival and substitution dates per market, exposure per practice); flow records sufficient for the positioning line.
- **A5.** Loadings are treated as constant within an estimation window and allowed to drift across windows. The stated convergence forces this.
- **A6.** Estimation runs on weekly strikes. The monthly research cadence does not constrain the tests.

## Section 1 — The identification frontier

There is no single scalar answer. The frontier is layered, and each layer has its own conditions.

**Layer 1 — rank ceiling: 33.** Thirty-three securities generate a 33×33 return covariance of rank at most 33. No set of lines *estimated from returns* can exceed it. Lines anchored to external observables (A4) do not consume this rank: they are measured, not estimated. This is the sense in which the ledger can carry more lines than securities without contradiction — an interpretation of the author's first position, not a challenge to it.

**Layer 2 — named-contrast ceiling: 13.** The listing is a two-way layout: each security is exactly one practice and exactly one market. The additive space — the span of the 11 practice indicators and the 3 market indicators, with the shared common direction counted once — has dimension 1 + (11−1) + (3−1) = **13**. This is the Heston–Rouwenhorst (1994) country/industry identification: with a complete two-way layout, world, practice, and market effects are separately named by construction, under sum-to-zero constraints on the practice and market effects. Conditions: balance (A3); additivity of loadings (the practice effect common across its three listings, the market effect common across its eleven); idiosyncratic covariance not collinear with the contrast directions.

The complement, 33 − 13 = 20 = (11−1)(3−1) dimensions, is the **interaction space** — practice×market patterns that are not the sum of a practice term and a market term. The substitution channel is the leading resident: wave exposure varies by practice *and* by market, since arrival dates differ by market. Nothing in the interaction space can be separated from idiosyncratic noise by estimation. The condition it fails is Chamberlain–Rothschild (1983): factor directions must have population eigenvalues that clear the idiosyncratic bulk, and a direction supported on few securities cannot. Interaction-space content can enter the ledger only by declaration and measurement, never by estimation. With T = 1,5565 — with T ≈ 1,565 — estimation error in the covariance is negligible (order 1/√T), so this is a population gap condition, not a sampling problem; but loadings drift (A5), so the gap is evaluated window by window.

**Layer 3 — epoch-achievable: 3 early, up to 11 late, never 13 at once.** The 13 compose into two blocks sharing one common direction: the between-market block (3 dimensions) and the between-practice block (11 dimensions). The stated mechanics anti-phase them. Early, arrivals are staggered by market, so market contrasts are strong and practice contrasts dormant — the same practice dying at different speeds in three markets is not one common practice factor but three local ones, which are interaction-space and unestimable. Late, arrivals coincide, so the market contrasts collapse toward the common direction and the practice contrasts strengthen.

**The three modes.**

*Cross-sectional within one market (11 securities).* Certifies the existence of comovement, not its nature. It can separate the market direction (support 11) and, conditionally, cluster comovement (support ≥ 3 practices). It cannot tell whether a cluster is cross-market-uniform — a rotation of the practice block, an alternative naming — or market-specific, which is interaction space and unestimable. Any pattern on two or fewer practices fails the gap condition: the Round 3 formulation, a factor on two constituents is a security-specific effect with ambitions, is the N = 11 statement of the Chamberlain–Rothschild bound.

*Cross-market, same practice (3 listings per practice).* The only channel that names factors. It supplies the 13 contrasts, and the practice triplet (N = 3) is where each practice line's separation is adjudicated — support of three clears the two-constituent floor by exactly one, conditional on the practice's common variance dominating the triplet's idiosyncratic bulk. The market side is permanently thin: C − 1 = 2 contrasts. No market-level structure finer than two contrasts is identifiable in this universe, ever.

*Time-series only.* Identifies nothing. Loadings and realizations are recovered only up to nonsingular rotation (classical factor-analysis indeterminacy; Bai–Ng 2002 and Stock–Watson 2002 identify the span and its dimension, not labels), and a slow common series with uniform or proportional loadings is observationally equivalent to the numeraire revision, to a valuation drift, and to every other slow common series at once. T ≈ 1,565 is estimating power, not identifying power. The only rescue for a time-series-only factor is external anchoring — which is exactly what the numeraire and substitution lines are: measured, not estimated.

**Binding constraint.** The within-market cross-section. Reasons: (i) separability is adjudicated on covariances whose idiosyncratic bulk is generated within markets — the 20-dimensional interaction space feeds that bulk and closes everything beyond the 13 contrasts to estimation; (ii) the cross-market capacity (13) exceeds what within-market separation ever certifies (3 early, 11 late); (iii) the Round 3 objection is a within-market separation failure and generalizes to all narrow-support candidates. The cross-market channel sets what can be *named*; the within-market channel sets what can *exist as a separately estimated line*; the question asks about identification, so the latter binds. Judgement, flagged as such: one could instead rank the cross-market contrast counts (2 and 10) as binding; I do not, because those are rotation limits, and the within-market gap is an existence limit.

## Section 2 — What the three-market structure buys, exactly

**When the claim is true.** The two-way layout is complete (A3); the between-market covariance of market-demeaned returns has rank 2 beyond the common direction, with both contrast eigenvalues above the idiosyncratic floor; idiosyncratic directions are not aligned with the contrasts; loadings are window-stable (A5). Under these conditions local and common factors are not merely separable but *named* — the layout pins the rotation, which is more than estimation alone ever delivers.

**When it fails.** Use the given correlation endpoints as structure, not measurement. Under an equicorrelation approximation for the market block, the 3×3 between-market covariance has one eigenvalue proportional to (1+2ρ) along the common direction and two proportional to (1−ρ) along the contrasts. Over the stated path, ρ = 0.2 → 0.9, the contrast variance falls by roughly a factor of eight (proportional to 0.8 → 0.1) while the common direction roughly doubles (1.4 → 2.8 in the same units). Identification of market contrasts fails when (1−ρ)-scaled variance falls below the idiosyncratic floor — detectable by a rank test on the demeaned between-market covariance (at N = 3 with T large, classical Bartlett-type sphericity and rank tests suffice; for the full 33×33 problem, where N/window-length is not negligible, the Onatski 2010 and Ahn–Horenstein 2013 class is the appropriate one, with Bai–Ng 2002 asymptotics only approximate at N = 33 and a margin required accordingly).

Stated plainly: **yes — convergence destroys the identification that early divergence provides.** The destruction is gradual, test-dated per window, and complete only in the limit ρ → 1; at ρ = 0.9 the market contrasts are residual at best, and a defensible ledger demotes them. Partial failure modes: if two markets synchronize with each other before the third, one contrast dies early and the block's rank falls to two; if any listing suspends, two-way completeness breaks and the naming identification breaks with it.

**The mirror.** The same convergence that destroys market-factor identification *creates* practice-factor identification: a practice line is estimable exactly when its three listings comove beyond the common and market directions, which requires synchronization. Early divergence destroys practice identification as thoroughly as late convergence destroys market identification. The design's claim is therefore half-true at every date: the three-market structure buys naming of local versus common at all times, buys estimation of local factors only while markets differ, and buys estimation of practice factors only while markets align. The two purchases trade off along the correlation path and are never simultaneously at full strength. The handover — market lines demoting while practice lines promote — is the structural event of the middle of the history, dated by the Section 4 tests, not by the specification.

**The defensible opening number is eight, nine with the conditional line** — which is what "near nine" admits. If "factor lines" is instead read as *priced* factor lines only, the defensible opening number is three, not nine, and the nine-line opening ledger is not supportable at any date under that reading.

**The defensible late number is sixteen** — the five fixed lines plus eleven practice lines, each +1 on its own three listings, spanning the practice block whose average is the common direction — **up to eighteen** if one or two market contrasts retain enough (1−ρ)-scaled variance to pass the floor test. The change occurs through the handover of Section 2: market lines demote when their contrast eigenvalues fail the floor test; practice lines promote as their triplets synchronize. Both are dated by the tests. The specification bounds but does not date them: the handover must begin after divergence is established and complete before full convergence, and the stagger across practices follows the coincidence of each wave's arrival dates across markets, which the declared schedule fixes. A calendar date is not derivable from what is given.

Nothing in this table is advocated. Each line is conditional on the test named in its last column, and the set is not preferred over its rotations — it is the rotation the listing pins.

## Section 4 — The promotion schedule

Rule, stated as a test. A declared-but-unpriced factor D, with design-implied loading vector ℓ_D drawn from listing membership or the declared schedule, is promoted to a priced line when, over a trailing window W — a declared parameter, set to cover at least one full arrival-to-substitution phase of the waves to which D is exposed; its numeric value is a calibration constant fixed by the Section 7 procedure, not by this text — all of the following hold:

- **T1 — Non-spanning.** The return residual after the existing lines, projected on ℓ_D, retains variance above the idiosyncratic floor. Implementable as a likelihood-ratio test of the restricted model without D against the model with D (confirmatory factor analysis; Jöreskog; the T-large, N-fixed condition holds within any window that satisfies A5).
- **T2 — Rank increment.** Admitting D raises the estimated number of eigenvalues of the adjusted 33×33 covariance that clear the bulk by one (Onatski 2010 difference test or Ahn–Horenstein 2013 ratio; at N = 33 a margin above nominal size is required, since Bai–Ng consistency presumes N → ∞).
- **T3 — Pattern conformity.** The estimated loadings on the new direction match ℓ_D in sign and rank order — the declared restriction is what converts an estimated direction into a named line. Measured by the variance share of estimated loadings explained by ℓ_D, above a calibrated floor.
- **T4 — Orthogonality.** D's realized series is uncorrelated with the realized series of every measured line (numeraire, substitution) below a calibrated bound; otherwise D is a relabeling of a measured channel.
- **T5 — Holdout.** Loadings estimated on W attribute variance in the subsequent holdout window above the floor (the Stock–Watson 2002 diffusion-index predictive check).

Promotion requires T1–T5. Demotion requires T2 (or T1) to fail on two consecutive windows. **The count law:** for a listing of I practices across C markets, named capacity is 1 + (I−1) + (C−1) contrasts, plus whatever lines the declared schedule anchors; the realized line count is the number of those passing T1–T5 at the date in question. Within this round the listing is fixed, so in-span variation in the count comes from promotion and demotion alone.

## Section 5 — What this universe cannot identify

The author's second position, made operational: a factor is estimable only along a contrast the listing draws. The listing here draws practice, market, and their additive combinations. The items below plausibly operate in this world — the stated mechanics imply them — and no contrast exists for any of them. For each: what it is, what it is confounded with, and the listing change that would separate it, answering the round's own question.

1. **The destination of redeemed attention.** The causal engine of this world is the wave stack, and the replacing acts are not listed. The universe observes only the displaced side; the attractiveness of the replacement, and where the redeemed attention goes, never appear as prices. Confounded with: the substitution line's residual and Unexplained — substitution is visible only from the victim's side, and arrival-versus-substitution is carried entirely by the declared schedule rather than by any quotation. Listing change that would separate it: list the replacing acts as securities; arrival and substitution then become a between-listing contrast.

2. **A generational cohort factor.** The stock of attention a birth cohort has placed in these practices moves all of them together, slowly. Confounded with: the common direction and the numeraire (all slow, all common), and with practice lines, since cohort exposure correlates with practice vintage. Listing change: float tranches by cohort within each practice-market — share classes whose only difference is cohort — turning the cohort effect into a between-tranche contrast.

3. **A density or urbanization factor.** Practices decay at different rates by density, and density differs across the three markets. Confounded with: the market contrasts (density loads the between-market block) and with within-market idiosyncratic residuals. Listing change: urban and provincial tranches of each practice-market.

4. **A local price-of-time factor.** The numeraire is one global unit, but the value of spared time differs by market; local wage growth moves local valuations. Confounded with: the market lines and the numeraire line, which are the same capability-and-wage flavor of movement. Listing change: quotation structure — local numeraires, or instruments exposed only to local unit valuation; the local-versus-global spread becomes the contrast.

5. **Cell-level local factors — the interaction space.** The twenty practice×market directions contain real structure: the specific persistence of film photography in Japan, the specific timing of messaging adoption in Korea. Each is confounded, by construction, with that single security's idiosyncratic residual. This is the formal disposition of Round 3's "security-specific effect with ambitions": the ambitions are legitimate exactly when the cell is replicated. Listing change: split each practice-market into listed variants — sub-practices, regional subs — so the within-cell cross-section separates the local factor from local noise.

6. **Style-cluster lines in addition to practice lines.** A style whose loadings are cross-market-uniform — analog media, asynchronous communication — is an exact linear combination of the practice lines: the practice indicators span every cross-market-uniform loading vector. Confounded with: the practice block, not partially but exactly. No listing composed of practices can separate a style from the practice lines; only a rotation choice can, and rotations are not data. This item marks the boundary of the second position itself: a practices-only listing determines that styles do not exist *as separate lines*, only as alternative names.

7. **A common reflexive factor.** Late in the history, feedback amplifies common moves; a reflexive common component loads uniformly. Confounded with: the common direction of whichever block is live. The reflexive line survives only because the feedback rule is declared and measured. Listing change: tracker instruments whose mechanical rules isolate the loop, making the reflexive component a between-instrument contrast.

## Section 6 — Double counting

**The single-driver principle.** The numeraire, the substitution channel, and the float all derive from one underlying capability series. At most two orthogonal attribution components can be drawn from a single driver: the unit-valuation component, measured by the companion index, and the exposure-weighted flow component, residualized against the first. Any third capability-derived line double counts by construction.

The criterion is not correlation but **source overlap**: an attribution is a partition of the weekly return, and double counting occurs when two lines are built from overlapping sources, so that one movement is claimed twice. The sites:

- **(i) Common direction vs numeraire line.** Uniform loadings on both. Prevention: deflate all returns by the companion index before any factor is estimated; the estimated common component then contains no unit revision. Symptom test: realized common series uncorrelated with the revision series.
- **(ii) Common direction vs market lines.** The common direction *is* the average of the three market lines. Carrying a separate world line alongside three unconstrained market lines is linear dependence — the same movement counted twice. Prevention: carry a block's lines or its common line plus contrasts, never both.
- **(iii) Substitution line vs numeraire line.** The same wave that spares time (raising the unit) also substitutes practices. Prevention: residualize the declared wave series against the measured revision before constructing line 2.
- **(iv) Substitution line vs market lines, early.** Market-phased wave timing loads exactly like a market factor. Prevention: estimate priced lines on returns already adjusted for lines 1–2; what remains in a market line is then genuinely non-wave local variance — and may be thin, which is an honest outcome, not a defect of the adjustment.
- **(v) Substitution line vs float.** Redemption is the balance-sheet shadow of substitution; an issuance or redemption line would re-count it. Prevention: float updates the fundamental and never enters the attribution.
- **(vi) Practice lines vs substitution line, late.** Where a practice's entire cross-market comovement is wave-driven, its practice line is collinear with the wave series. Prevention: T4. If a practice never passes T4, it stays inside line 2 — an identifiability boundary, stated as such rather than resolved.
- **(vii) Positioning and reflexive lines vs priced lines.** Crowded flows and common feedback load like factors. Prevention: estimate them last, on residuals.

The general construction: a recursive orthogonalization in a declared order — numeraire, substitution, priced factors, positioning, reflexive, Unexplained — where each stage uses only the residual of the prior stages. The ordering has the identification status of a Cholesky ordering in a recursive structural model (Sims 1980): it determines the split, it is untestable from returns, and it must be declared. Unexplained is the monitor: if its cross-sectional covariance is not near-diagonal, a line is missing, and the Section 4 promotion search is triggered.

## Section 7 — What would settle it

A procedure runnable on data this world generates:

1. **Build the panel.** Returns deflated by the companion index; adjusted for the declared substitution schedule; balanced windows per A5.
2. **Calibrate by Monte Carlo.** Simulate the world under a fully declared generating process that embeds *all* candidate factors from the earlier rounds' sets simultaneously, sweeping each factor's variance share from negligible to dominant, with the stated correlation path and wave-timing structure. Run T1–T5 on the simulated panels; record promotion false-positive and false-negative rates as functions of variance share, N = 33, and window length. This is the Monte Carlo design underlying Bai–Ng (2002) and Onatski (2010), and it fixes the floors and window constants this text deliberately leaves unset. Condition: the generating process must be declared in full, because the tests' size depends on it.
3. **Apply to the actual history, rolling.** Output: the dated sequence of promotions and demotions — the early count through the handover to the late count, with dates.
4. **Evaluate candidate sets.** Each earlier-round set, operationalized as a loading matrix of declared lines, is subjected to T1–T5 line by line. A set is eliminated if any of its lines fails on every window — that line is not separately supported in this universe. Surviving sets are checked by out-of-sample attribution: Unexplained's covariance must be near-diagonal, with no systematic residual.
5. **State the limit plainly.** The procedure discriminates *identifiability classes*, not sets within a class. Two passing sets that are rotations of each other generate the same return distribution; no data this universe can produce distinguishes them, because factor spans are identified only up to rotation. The tie between passing rotations is settled by the listing's exclusion structure, not by the data — the author's second position, reached from the estimation side rather than assumed.
6. **Robustness.** Sweep window lengths; block-bootstrap the rank tests' null distributions to preserve serial correlation (Künsch 1989; Politis–Romano 1994).

What would count as settling: candidate sets are eliminated when their lines fail; survivors are certified only as classes; the class membership is fixed by the listing.

## Section 8 — Refusals

- **Declined to choose among rotations.** No data this universe generates can distinguish a practice-line rotation from a style rotation of the same span. Choosing would be preference, and preference is what the first three rounds produced.
- **Declined to date the handover in calendar time.** Only the correlation endpoints are given; the tests date the rest.
- **Declined to set numeric thresholds** — variance-share floors, window lengths, correlation bounds. They are calibration constants fixed by the Section 7 procedure; inventing them would violate this round's own rule against computing on invented measurements.
- **Declined to evaluate the author's two positions**, per the rules. I interpreted the first — measured lines do not consume covariance rank — which is an interpretation, not a challenge.
- **Flagged, rather than resolved, the ambiguity in "nine factor lines"** (A2), with the alternative reading answered in Section 3.
- **On the framing.** The round asks for scalars — "the maximum number," "the nine lines" — where the objects are indexed by epoch and by rotation. A scalar answer would have to smuggle in exactly the preference this round forbids; I answered with the indexed objects and gave the scalar readings only under stated assumptions. One further note, belonging here and only here: in a world whose generating process is authored, identifiability is a design commitment, and the Section 7 tests audit the design's own commitments rather than discover anything. Read as an audit, the round is well posed, and the audit's result is: the listing commits this universe to thirteen named contrasts — three live at the open, eleven at the close, never both sets at strength — plus the lines the declared schedule anchors, and to nothing else.
