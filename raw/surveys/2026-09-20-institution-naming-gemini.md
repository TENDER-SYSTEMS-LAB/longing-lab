## Section 1 — The Japanese Publisher

### Candidate 1: 産業時事新聞 (*Sangyō Jiji Shimbun*)

* **Literal Gloss:** Industry Current Events Newspaper
* **Plausibility:** Follows the standard 19th/20th-century naming convention combining subject focus (*Sangyō*) with news frequency/journalistic suffix (*Jiji Shimbun*).
* **Collision Check:**
* **Existing Entities:** *Jiji Press* (時事通信社) and *Nihon Keizai Shimbun* (日本経済新聞) exist. The exact combination *Sangyō Jiji Shimbun* is not a major active national daily in Japan.
* **Trademark Risk:** Class 16/36. Low-to-moderate risk of opposition from entities holding "Jiji" or "Sangyo Shimbun" trademarks.
* **Domain Risk:** `sangyojiji.co.jp` / `sangyojiji.com` — Likely available or parked.
* **Confidence:** High confidence regarding no major national newspaper collision; moderate confidence on minor regional registry status.



### Candidate 2: 東亜経済日報 (*Tōa Keizai Nippō*)

* **Literal Gloss:** East Asia Economic Daily
* **Plausibility:** Echoes mid-century regional economic papers (*Tōa* being a very common pre-war and post-war institutional prefix).
* **Collision Check:**
* **Existing Entities:** **COLLISION.** *Tōa Keizai Nippō* (Toa Keizai Nippo Press) exists as a real newspaper entity in Japan focusing on Korean-Japanese economic affairs.
* **Status:** **REJECTED FOR PRIMARY USE** due to real-world publication collision.



### Candidate 3: 帝都新報 (*Teito Shimbun*)

* **Literal Gloss:** Imperial Capital News / Teito News
* **Plausibility:** Uses *Teito* (Imperial Capital / Metropolitan area, historically pre-1945 but maintained in institutional names like Teito Rapid Transit Authority into the late 20th century). Gives an established, conservative tone.
* **Collision Check:**
* **Existing Entities:** Appears in various fictional works as a generic newspaper name; no active national daily newspaper of this exact primary title currently dominates the Japanese market.
* **Trademark Risk:** Low in primary media categories today.
* **Domain Risk:** `teitoshimbun.com` / `teito.co.jp` — `teito.co.jp` is likely registered by historical Teito-suffixed companies (e.g., taxi/transport groups).
* **Confidence:** High regarding financial index publishing collision; uncertain on local corporate trade names in Japan.



### Candidate 4: 毎夕経済新聞 (*Maiyū Keizai Shimbun*)

* **Literal Gloss:** Every Evening Economic Newspaper
* **Plausibility:** Directly mirrors evening paper naming conventions (like the historical *Maiyū Shimbun* which merged into other outlets mid-20th century).
* **Collision Check:**
* **Existing Entities:** Historical *Maiyū Shimbun* existed (defunct). The combination *Maiyū Keizai Shimbun* is not currently an active daily economic paper.
* **Trademark Risk:** Class 16/36. Low risk due to historical abandonment of the *Maiyū* prefix for major media.
* **Domain Risk:** `maiyukeizai.com` — High likelihood of being unregistered/clean.
* **Confidence:** High regarding current market presence; uncertain regarding historical trademark revivals.



### Candidate 5: 日本證券時報 (*Nihon Shōken Jihō*)

* **Literal Gloss:** Japan Securities Times
* **Plausibility:** Employs traditional kanji (證券) and standard industry trade paper framing (*Jihō* / Times/Bulletin).
* **Collision Check:**
* **Existing Entities:** **COLLISION RISK.** Very close to *Nippon Shoken Shimbun* (日本証券新聞). High risk of confusing similarity in trade association directories.
* **Status:** **REJECTED FOR PRIMARY USE**.
