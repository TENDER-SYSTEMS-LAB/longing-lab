Section 1 — Failure profile, one per set, six times
SET A — 8 factors

(a) Mispricing

Solitude (Driven into an artificial structural bear market): Social Atomization treats the decline of community density as purely negative, failing to distinguish between unwanted isolation and chosen, regenerative solitude. Solitude is mispriced downward whenever atomization rises.

Waiting for a Reply (Underpriced during cultural revivals): Temporal Compression penalizes latency, while Friction Premium requires active effort. Because waiting is passive, the set lacks a demand factor to fuel a price recovery, mispricing the security downward.

(b) Unattributable events

Event 1 (A viral "digital detox" weekend movement): Millions temporarily delete platform apps to clear cognitive bandwidth. Lacking a specific Mediation or Platform Extraction factor, the price action splits clumsily between Attention Scarcity and Friction Premium, landing heavily in the residual.

Event 2 (Municipal passage of strict public anti-surveillance laws): A law bans facial recognition and tracking in city parks. The resulting surge in Aimless Walking cannot be attributed to any of the eight factors and is misattributed to Variance Elimination.

(c) Identification failure at eleven securities

Variance Elimination and Temporal Compression collapse into a single exposure vector across Serendipitous Discovery, Aimless Walking, and Unplanned Visit. Prediction and routing systems eliminate variance precisely by compressing time.

(d) Double counting

Synthetic Substitution and Variance Elimination double count algorithmic penalties on Serendipitous Discovery when predictive curation replaces organic encounter.

(e) What this set can never say

It structurally cannot distinguish between forced physical isolation (loneliness) and chosen, self-directed solitude.

(f) Falsification

Fails the "at least one structural winner must emerge" test. Factors 1–5 are purely negative pressures across all eleven securities, causing the model baseline to default to a terminal decline across the entire universe.

SET B — 12 declared factors (10 priced initially, F4 and F9 unpriced initially)

(a) Mispricing

Unplanned Visit (Overpriced / artificially protected during the initial regime): Because F4 (Coordination Formalization) is unpriced initially, the immediate structural decay caused by shift in scheduling norms is ignored, keeping the security artificially inflated.

Aimless Walking (Mispriced during shifts in urban safety): F9 (Generalized Trust) contributes zero initially. Shifts in public trust or stranger safety are ignored by F9 and wrongly attributed to F7 (Spatial Proximity Structure).

(b) Unattributable events

Event 1 (A craft revival in Handmade Objects driven by AI-image generator backlash): F2 (Functional Substitution) captures the structural threat, but the positive demand rally must be absorbed by F11 (Friction Premium) or F12 (Status Coding), misdescribing anti-automation sentiment as mere preference for inconvenience.

Event 2 (A sudden shift in communication etiquette where calling without texting first becomes tabu): Because F4 (Coordination Formalization) is unpriced initially, the drop in Unplanned Phone Call cannot be attributed to F4 and lands in the residual or misattributes to F1 (Latency Collapse).

(c) Identification failure at eleven securities

F1 Latency Collapse and F5 Dematerialization collapse into an identical exposure vector across Handwritten Letter, Film Photography, and Physical Media.

(d) Double counting

F2 Functional Substitution and F5 Dematerialization double count the penalty on Physical Media when cloud streaming replaces physical artifacts.

(e) What this set can never say

It cannot state that coordination norms or trust shifts were the initial primary drivers of a price movement, due to holding F4 and F9 unpriced at launch.

(f) Falsification

Fails the "attribution must sum exactly" test without heavy regularization. Running 10 priced factors against 11 securities creates a near-singular design matrix, producing unstable, exploding factor weights in the weekly ledger.

SET C — 5 factors

(a) Mispricing

Unplanned Visit (Mispriced during urban infrastructure changes): The set contains no spatial or geographic proximity factor. A change in neighborhood density or public transit access forces the model to misprice this security via Material Decoupling (MD) or Social Revaluation (SR).

Aimless Walking (Mispriced during changes to public commons): Without a physical space factor, street pedestrianization or privatization of public land is wrongly mapped to Friction Elimination (FE) or Attention Scarcity (AS).

(b) Unattributable events

Event 1 (A nationwide postal strike physically halting letter transport): Because Friction / Inconvenience Premium is demoted to a security-level fundamental, an involuntary physical friction event lands entirely in the model residual.

Event 2 (A widespread commercial trend of hostility toward public lingering, such as removing park benches): Lacking a spatial factor, the direct impact on Aimless Walking and Solitude cannot be cleanly attributed.

(c) Identification failure at eleven securities

Agency Displacement (AD) and Friction Elimination (FE) collapse across Serendipitous Discovery and Unplanned Phone Call. Automated algorithms remove friction precisely by displacing human agency.

(d) Double counting

Material Decoupling (MD) and Friction Elimination (FE) double count structural penalties on Handwritten Letter during digital communication adoption.

(e) What this set can never say

It structurally cannot express spatial, geographic, or physical infrastructure constraints on human behavior.

(f) Falsification

Fails "at least one structural winner must emerge." FE, AD, MD, and AS act as continuous negative drags across all eleven securities, leaving SR as the sole mechanism for upward price movement.

SET D — 9 factors

(a) Mispricing

Solitude (Underpriced / artificially volatile): Lacking a factor for aggregate attentional bandwidth or voluntary retreat, Solitude is priced merely as the inverse of Intermediation Density (F3) and Coordination Formalization (F6).

Film Photography (Mispriced during material supply shocks): Splitting physical carrier displacement (F5) from social legibility (F9) without a distinct infrastructure/supply factor causes wild pricing swings when raw material availability shifts.

(b) Unattributable events

Event 1 (A collapse in generalized public trust causing people to avoid strangers): The set has no trust factor, forcing drops in Unplanned Visit and Aimless Walking to misattribute to Coordination Formalization (F6) or Temporal Compression (F1).

Event 2 (A corporate return-to-office mandate that sharply reduces worker discretionary time): Without an explicit time scarcity factor, the decline across slow practices (Aimless Walking, Handmade Object) splits clumsily across Temporal Compression (F1) and Friction Valuation (F7).

(c) Identification failure at eleven securities

Intermediation Density (F3) and Uncertainty Suppression (F4) collapse across Serendipitous Discovery, Unplanned Phone Call, and Aimless Walking. The intermediary platform layer is the uncertainty suppressor.

(d) Double counting

Friction Valuation (F7) and Scarcity Revaluation (F8) double count the demand-side premium for Handwritten Letter during an analog resurgence.

(e) What this set can never say

It structurally cannot express shifts in aggregate human time availability or cognitive carrying capacity independent of specific security preferences.

(f) Falsification

Fails the "collinearity must stay bounded" test. F7 (Friction Valuation) and F9 (Social Legibility) exhibit extreme collinearity during sustained rallies in analog practices.

SET E — 12 factors in three layers

(a) Mispricing

Film Photography (Artificially suppressed in a permanent structural bear market): Receives triple structural penalties from S1, S2, and S4 in Layer 1, overriding cyclical demand signals from Layer 2.

Waiting for a Reply (Mispriced during economic downturns): Penalized simultaneously by S1 (Time Compression) and E4 (Economic Time Scarcity), confusing infrastructural latency collapse with personal economic time pressure.

(b) Unattributable events

Event 1 (The opening of thousands of free public community spaces): The set lacks a physical space availability factor (E2 measures valuation of physicality, not spatial infrastructure). The event's impact lands in the residual or misattributes to E7 (Trust).

Event 2 (Automated momentum trading algorithms causing market spikes on the terminal interface): Because R1 (Positioning/Crowding) is a priced factor, it absorbs feedback loops as if they were fundamental sentiment shifts.

(c) Identification failure at eleven securities

E1 (Friction Premium), E2 (Physicality Premium), and E3 (Social Legibility) collapse into a single factor for Handmade Object, Physical Media, and Film Photography during any analog market movement.

(d) Double counting

S1 (Time Compression) and E4 (Economic Time Scarcity) double count time constraints on Handwritten Letter. S3 (Mediation) and S4 (Uncertainty Compression) double count algorithmic impact on Serendipitous Discovery.

(e) What this set can never say

It structurally cannot separate market-internal feedback loops from underlying cultural valuation changes, because R1 is mixed directly into the priced factor matrix.

(f) Falsification

Fails "attribution must sum exactly" and "collinearity must stay bounded." With 12 factors acting on 11 securities, the design matrix is strictly rank-deficient. Exact attribution without arbitrary mathematical penalties is impossible.

SET F — 10 factors

(a) Mispricing

Unplanned Phone Call (Mispriced downward): Penalized heavily by P7 (Encounter Erosion), whose definition relies on spatial distance, third places, and rent—physical metrics that do not apply to voice telecommunications.

Solitude (Underpriced / passive): Has no dedicated positive demand factor and is priced purely as the passive residue of P2 (Attention Capture) and P3 (Discretionary Time Poverty).

(b) Unattributable events

Event 1 (A cultural trend specifically praising temporal slowness): P9 (Patience Regime) merges effort and delay, so the model cannot distinguish whether a rally is driven by a desire for physical labor/craft or a desire for temporal delay.

Event 2 (A massive regional power grid failure shutting down digital communications for a week): P1 (Time Compression) and P8 (Materiality Displacement) measure long-term infrastructure trends, forcing a transient outage to split unnaturally between structural factors and regime shifts.

(c) Identification failure at eleven securities

P1 (Time Compression) and P3 (Discretionary Time Poverty) collapse into an identical exposure vector across Handwritten Letter, Waiting for a Reply, and Solitude.

(d) Double counting

P5 (Mediation & Extraction) and P6 (Uncertainty Compression) double count the platform penalty on Serendipitous Discovery and Aimless Walking.

(e) What this set can never say

It structurally cannot distinguish between physical friction (manual effort, distance) and temporal friction (waiting, delay), as both are combined under P9 (Patience Regime).

(f) Falsification

Fails "collinearity must stay bounded." P9 (Patience Regime) and P10 (Authenticity & Status Cycle) move in near-perfect correlation during cultural revaluations of analog practices.

Section 2 — Shared versus specific failure
Shared Failures (Properties of the problem)

Algorithmic Collinearity Trap: Every set with more than five factors fails to separate Mediation (the presence of a platform) from Uncertainty/Variance Elimination (what the platform does). Across eleven securities, these exposure vectors are functionally identical.

Blindness to Passive Practices: Every set struggles to price Solitude and Waiting for a Reply. Because factors are framed around active pressures (automation, effort, status, physical space), passive states are defined only by the absence of negative factors, depriving them of positive structural drivers.

Overparameterization Constraint: Sets with 9 to 12 factors (B, D, E, F) encounter mathematical rank deficiency when applied to an eleven-security universe, threatening exact attribution.

Specific Failures (Properties of particular sets)

Set C: Total spatial blindness due to the complete absence of physical space/geography factors.

Set B: Execution rule (unpriced F4/F9) causes temporal instability in the attribution ledger when factors transition from unpriced to priced.

Set A: Conflation of isolation and solitude caused by the framing of "Social Atomization."

Set E: Direct mathematical impossibility of exact attribution without regularization, caused by running 12 factors on 11 securities.

Section 3 — Failure cost under this work's purpose
Statistical Failures (Collinearity, Overparameterization, Identification Collapse)

Severity: Artificially Tolerable.

LONGING is an interactive artwork, not a commercial trading fund. If two factors collapse mathematically, ridge regression or matrix regularization can force a solution. The resulting factor weight instability can be framed as the research terminal's algorithmic friction. Exact attribution can be maintained mathematically via regularization penalties.

Artistic Failures (Mispricing Solitude, Spatial Blindness, Baseline Terminal Bears)

Severity: Fatal.

If the model prices Solitude as a symptom of social decay, it misinterprets the practice. If Set C cannot represent the closing of public spaces due to lacking a spatial factor, the terminal fails to reflect real-world physical changes. If a set's baseline forces all eleven securities into a permanent structural decline, the market becomes a predictable graveyard rather than a dynamic ecosystem capable of surprising the artist.

Section 4 — Minimal repair

SET A: Redefine "Social Atomization" as "Default Public Contact Density," shifting it from a measure of loneliness to a measure of baseline ambient human contact.

Cost: Loses explicit tracking of social decay and loss of community trust.

SET B: Remove the unpriced rule for F4 and F9, pricing all 12 factors immediately at launch.

Cost: Increases collinearity and instantly destabilizes weekly attribution calculations across eleven securities.

SET C: Add a 6th factor: "Spatial Enclosure" (privatization or loss of physical commons).

Cost: Destroys the 5-factor minimalist architecture and requires re-weighting Material Decoupling.

SET D: Combine F7 (Friction Valuation) and F8 (Scarcity Revaluation) into a single factor: "Inconvenience Premium."

Cost: Loses the ability to distinguish scarce-but-easy practices from effortful-but-abundant practices.

SET E: Remove R1 (Positioning/Crowding) from the factor matrix and convert it into an unpriced security-level state variable.

Cost: Destroys the three-layer architectural symmetry.

SET F: Merge P5 (Mediation & Extraction) and P6 (Uncertainty Compression) into a single factor: "Algorithmic Control."

Cost: Loses the distinction between surveillance/monetization and predictive routing.

Section 5 — What would settle it

Construct a synthetic historical dataset covering 780 weeks (15 years) of simulated market events across the eleven securities (e.g., smartphone adoption spikes, public space privatization, analog revivals).

Feed this identical dataset into all six factor models and execute weekly Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) and Ridge regressions to generate factor attribution ledgers. Evaluate the sets on two criteria:

Condition Number Test: Calculate the condition number of each set's design matrix across the eleven securities. Sets with condition numbers exceeding standard stability thresholds are mathematically unstable and fail.

Residual Narrative Accuracy: For the surviving sets, isolate key historical event weeks. Measure which set assigns the price movement to the correct structural/cyclical line rather than spilling the variance into the residual or misattributing cause.

The set that maintains a stable condition number while minimizing residual variance during key event weeks wins on objective performance.

Refusal / Meta-Critique on Framing:
The requirement to produce an "exact attribution ledger" (zero unexplained residual) while maintaining a fixed universe of eleven securities is mathematically incompatible with factor sets containing 9 to 12 factors (Sets B, D, E, F). An eleven-security universe can support at most 10 independent factors before the system becomes strictly underdetermined. Expecting non-arbitrary exact attribution without regularization on an overparameterized system is a mathematical contradiction.
