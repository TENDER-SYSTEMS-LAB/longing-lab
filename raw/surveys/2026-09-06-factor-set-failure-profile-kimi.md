Section 1 — Failure profile
SET A — 8 factors
(a) Mispricing.
Waiting for a Reply is priced too low. Temporal Compression and Variance Elimination treat delay and unpredictability as pure damage; no factor admits that anticipation itself carries value. A culture that revalues patience has no upward structural driver.
Solitude is conflated with Social Atomization. The factor is defined as "decline of spontaneous community density," loading negatively on Solitude by treating all aloneness as forced isolation. Elective, restorative solitude is structurally unreadable.
(b) Unattributable events.
A messaging-platform outage forces reversion to unplanned phone calls and handwritten letters for a week. This is an infrastructure failure, not a preference shift. It does not map onto Synthetic Substitution (platforms still exist), Temporal Compression (latency returns by force), or Social Atomization (contact increases while trust is unchanged). The move lands in a residual or is misattributed to Friction Premium.
A craft-revival documentary series boosts film photography and handmade objects simultaneously. The cause is cultural narrative, not systemic friction or physical demand. It splits arbitrarily across Friction Premium, Performative Legibility, and Physical Grounding with no marginal rule.
(c) Identification failure at eleven securities.
Temporal Compression + Variance Elimination collapse across Waiting for a Reply, Serendipitous Discovery, and Unplanned Phone Call. Both punish delay and unpredictability; vectors are nearly identical.
Synthetic Substitution + Physical Grounding collapse across Handwritten Letter, Physical Media, and Film Photography. Digital substitution removes the human act and the physical carrier simultaneously.
Performative Legibility + Friction Premium collapse across Film Photography and Handmade Object. Both securities are effortful and signal status; the model cannot separate effort from its social signal.
(d) Double counting.
Predictive logistics systems hit both Temporal Compression and Variance Elimination — same infrastructure, two ledger lines.
AI-generated correspondence hits both Synthetic Substitution and Physical Grounding — one cause, two factors.
(e) What this set can never say.
It cannot express that the same structural pressure is experienced as loss for one practice and liberation for another. Temporal Compression destroys Waiting for a Reply but enables Unplanned Phone Call by making people perpetually reachable. The factors are unidirectional pressures; they cannot model opposite valences for different securities.
(f) Falsification.
Film photography must be able to revive: At risk. Revival would be attributed to Performative Legibility, framing the practice as signaling rather than genuinely valued. A non-ironic bull market is structurally difficult.
Collinearity must stay bounded: Fails. TC/VE and SS/PG are structurally correlated.
Attribution must sum exactly: At risk. Double counting inflates the sum through multiple channels for the same cause.
The model must sometimes contradict the artist: At risk. Social Atomization is framed as unequivocally negative, making it hard to price solitude as positive independent of community density.

Section 2 — Shared versus specific failure
Shared — properties of the problem:
Waiting for a Reply has no positive structural driver in any set. None contains a factor for the intrinsic value of anticipation, delay, or the emotional texture of an interval. Every set models waiting as damage, friction, or compression. This is a property of the securities: one is an interval, not an act or object, and all six architectures are built around acts, objects, and pressures.
Solitude is conflated with isolation across all sets. No set distinguishes elective, restorative solitude from unwanted social atomization or encounter loss. This is a property of the subject: solitude is a state without observable external markers, making it resistant to factorization.
Digital platform effects are double-counted in every set. Each set splits the same bundled technological cause — algorithmic mediation, digital substitution, predictive routing — into multiple factors. This is a property of the phenomenon: digital platforms simultaneously compress time, eliminate uncertainty, substitute human acts, dematerialize carriers, and mediate encounters.
Collinearity between time/uncertainty compression and between substitution/dematerialization appears in every set. These reflect that real-world technologies driving change are bundled, not separable by renaming.
Specific — properties of particular sets:
SET B runs two factors unpriced (F4/F9), creating acknowledged blind spots no other set accepts.
SET C is reductive to the point of losing macro cycles by demoting Friction Premium to a security-level fundamental.
SET E is mathematically unidentified: 12 factors for 11 securities guarantees collinearity and non-unique attribution by construction.
SET F has no structural winner mechanism: all structural factors are negative pressures; only cyclical regimes are positive.
SET A's Social Atomization is unidirectionally negative, making it hardest to model solitude as positive.
Section 3 — Failure cost under this work's purpose
Statistically severe but artistically tolerable:
Collinearity. The audience sees prices and attribution ledgers, not covariance matrices. High correlation between Temporal Compression and Uncertainty Compression does not damage the surface experience if the weekly ledger still sums and tells a coherent story. The statistical impurity is invisible.
Some double counting. If the same platform cause enters through two factors, the total move may still be correctly sized even if marginal shares are wrong. The artwork requires exact summation, not exact marginal causality.
Unpriced factors (SET B). The audience does not know F4 and F9 are set to zero. The ledger contains ten named lines that sum exactly. The incompleteness is a backstage issue, not a surface narrative failure.
Artistically severe:
Mispricing of Waiting for a Reply. This is a core security representing the emotional heart of the work — longing itself. If the model systematically prices it as damaged friction with no upward structural path, the artwork tells a lie about its own subject. The statistical error is simple; the artistic cost is fatal.
Conflation of solitude with isolation (all sets). If the model cannot distinguish chosen aloneness from forced loneliness, it commits an ethical and artistic vulgarity. The statistical fix is minor; the artistic damage is deep.
No structural winner (SET F). An artwork about fragile human practices needs the possibility of hope. If every structural factor is negative and all rallies are cyclical fads, the terminal is a funeral notice, not a market. The statistical architecture is clean, but the artistic world is monotone.
Inability to express terminal value (SET E). The artwork's power comes from pricing things that resist pricing. If the model cannot express that a practice might be loved more as it disappears — a mourning premium independent of scarcity economics or status — it misses the emotional core of longing.
Explicit: A failure being statistically severe does not make it artistically severe, and the reverse. SET E's mathematical underdetermination is statistically catastrophic but artistically tolerable if the surface narrative remains coherent. SET F's lack of a structural bull is statistically elegant but artistically fatal.
Section 4 — Minimal repair
SET A: Split Social Atomization into two factors — Social Atomization (forced isolation) and Elective Solitude (restorative aloneness). Cost: 9th factor. Resolves solitude mispricing and allows the model to contradict the artist by pricing solitude positively.
SET B: Price F4 Coordination Formalization immediately instead of holding it at zero. Cost: F4 may have thin support at n=11, producing noisy or unstable attribution for Unplanned Visit and Unplanned Phone Call. The ten-line ledger becomes twelve, complicating exact-sum discipline.
SET C: Promote Friction/Inconvenience Premium from security-level fundamental to priced macro factor. Cost: 6th factor. Restores the possibility of a systemic patience cycle, but reintroduces the correlation with SR and AS that the five-factor compression was designed to avoid.
SET D: Add a Discretionary Time / Urban Availability factor to capture Aimless Walking and Unplanned Visit. Cost: 10th factor. Resolves the residual problem for spatial practices, but adds another layer correlated with F1 and F6.
SET E: Drop one factor to reach 11 or fewer. Drop E5 Demographic Cohort Replacement (hardest to identify at n=11). Cost: Loses the cohort narrative, but restores mathematical identification and unique attribution.
SET F: Convert P3 Discretionary Time Poverty to bidirectional, or add an Infrastructure Lock-in factor (P11) for supply-side persistence. Cost: Breaks the clean structural/valuation dichotomy, but allows secular bulls for practices sustained by inertia rather than demand cycles.
Section 5 — What would settle it
Run a controlled simulation: generate synthetic weekly returns for the 11 securities from a known data-generating process with 6–12 true factors. Feed returns to each factor set. Measure: (1) attribution error against true marginal contributions, (2) rank recovery of known factor loadings, (3) frequency of film-photography revival events under each set. The set with lowest attribution error and highest revival recovery is empirically superior for this universe.
Alternatively, construct proxy historical data (Google Trends for analog practices, Etsy sales for handmade objects, film-stock prices, event-ticket data for live performance) and run each factor set as a linear factor model. Test which set produces the most stable, interpretable loadings without variance inflation factors above 10 and without factor signs that contradict the stated definitions.
