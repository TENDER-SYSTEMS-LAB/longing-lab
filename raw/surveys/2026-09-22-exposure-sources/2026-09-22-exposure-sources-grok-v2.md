**Training cut-off.** Pretraining is strongest through 2023–early 2024. Figures and titles after that are attested from live search as of 22 September 2026 and are marked when they sit after the cut-off. Nothing below is a fabricated series.

LETTER is the worked case: a personally composed, physically handwritten message for a named person, released through a delivery route. Adjacent practices (asking after someone, gifting a card, calling, meeting) are noted when a source covers them.

Most sources observe **adoption, volume, or prevalence**, not restoration or substitution. The inference column states the step required.

## Section 3 — Sources that observe both, or the boundary

Same act can be read as restoration or substitution depending on intensity.

**Predictive text / autocomplete / Smart Compose.**  
HCI keystroke logs and the occasional OEM paper record *acceptance rate* and *span* (next word vs next phrase vs whole sentence). A one-word accept is closer to restored motor doing; a whole-clause accept is substituted judgment. No public year-market series for JP/KR/US personal letters. Status: probable as a *kind*, verified only as lab studies.

**Voice banking vs message banking vs voice cloning.**  
ALS Association / Team Gleason / Costello (Boston Children’s) distinguish: message banking stores *the person’s own decided phrases* (restoration of doing, judgment already spent by the person); voice banking / cloning lets *new* text — possibly written by a carer or a model — be spoken in the person’s voice (doing restored, judgment available to be substituted). The published Gleason request counts do not split the three. What would tell them apart: phrase-count vs generative use logs on the SGD. Status: verified distinction, weak measurement.

**Speech neuroprosthesis.**  
Decoded intended speech is restoration of doing if the patient is choosing the words; it becomes substitution if the decoder’s language model is completing or rewriting. Papers report vocabulary size and language-model use. Tiny n. Status: verified.

**Machine translation of personal messages.**  
Papago, Google Translate, LINE translation. If the user writes in L1 and sends L2, doing of the foreign-script letter is restored and judgment over phrasing in L2 is substituted. Product MAUs exist; personal-letter share does not. Status: probable.

**PC-printed “handwriting” fonts and photo nengajo.**  
Japan 1998–2010 home-print boom: doing of ink-on-card is partly restored (a physical card still leaves the house); judgment and graphomotor gesture are substituted by a font and a layout wizard. Japan Post internet-acceptance and printer-software attach would separate them if published together. Status: probable.

**Summarisation and “write a shorter thank-you.”**  
LLM use-case surveys (First Page Sage 2026 lists personal notes at 27% of AI-using adults) sit on the boundary: shortening a draft the user already wrote is light substitution; generating the draft from “mom, birthday” is full substitution. Surveys rarely ask which. Status: verified as a 2023–26 snapshot, not a panel.

**Greeting-card AI attach rate (Moonpig-type).**  
The only commercial source found that *separates who wrote the body from who sent the object*. If the sender edits the AI text, intensity can be scored. Not available for JP nengajo or KR cards in published form. Status: verified for one operator, speculative as a three-market series.

## Section 4 — First sort by quantity and quality

Not a selection. Quantity = how many of the 90 year-market cells (30 × 3) the source can fill. Quality = inference steps from count to exposure, and whether the definition holds 1996–2026.

### Postal traffic
- **USPS First-Class / single-piece letters** — Quantity: US 30/30, others 0. Quality: 2–3 steps (volume ≠ personal LETTER ≠ restored doing). Definition drifts when parcels are pulled out (2008) and when “correspondence” is later estimated separately in household studies.
- **Japan Post standard mail + nengajo issues** — Quantity: JP ~30/30 for total mail; nengajo annual across the span. Quality: nengajo is a better “asking after” series than raw first-class; still mixes corporate stock with handwritten.
- **Korea Post 우편물통계** — Quantity: KR ~20–25/30 (clean from ~2003). Quality: same as other posts.
- **UPU letter-post** — Quantity: all three markets, most years (gaps exist). Quality: best cross-section, worst content mix.

### Assistive / clinical
- **ALS AAC surveys** — Quantity: 2–4 cells. Quality: fewest inference steps for the reference case; not annual, not LETTER-specific.
- **Team Gleason banking counts** — Quantity: US ~8–10 years. Quality: good on restoration of voice-doing; does not split judgment.
- **BCI papers** — Quantity: a handful of person-years in the US. Quality: highest fidelity, unusable as a market index.
- **USPS Free Matter for the Blind** — Quantity: US ~30. Quality: restoration of sending, not of composing a letter.

### Literacy and cost
- **PIAAC / IALS / ALL** — Quantity: 2–4 cells per market. Quality: one step to “can they write unaided,” none to “did they send a letter.” Definition of literacy is held comparable by design.
- **Postage tariffs** — Quantity: 90/90. Quality: measures the cost axis only.

### Enabling tech
- **Internet/smartphone penetration** — Quantity: 90/90. Quality: many steps. Needed as a denominator, not as the exposure.
- **Dictation / eye-gaze product ship dates** — Quantity: era flags in all three markets, not annual intensities. Quality: availability ≠ use.

### Greeting cards and templates
- **GCA / Hallmark occasion units** — Quantity: US ~30; JP nengajo ~30; KR thin. Quality: strong on substituted *wording*, silent on whether the sender still chose the addressee and the occasion.
- **JP PC nengajo software era** — Quantity: JP ~15 years of industry commentary, not a clean unit series. Quality: the main pre-LLM substitution wave for JP LETTER-adjacent practice.

### AI drafting (post-2022)
- **National polls and platform attach rates** — Quantity: US/UK/AU 2023–26 only; JP/KR nearly empty. Quality: fewest steps to “judgment handed over,” but the object is usually a DM or e-card, not a handwritten LETTER.
- **Search for “what to write in a …”** — Quantity: Trends from 2004, all markets with enough volume. Quality: demand for substitution, not substitution itself.

### Boundary instruments
- **Moonpig-type attach rate** — Quantity: 1–2 cells. Quality: best *definitional* quality in the file.
- **SGD stored-phrase vs novel text** — Quantity: 1 US cross-section. Quality: the right distinction; no panel.

## Section 5 — What no source covers

**Years.** 1996–2005 is thin everywhere except postal volumes, postage, and ICT penetration. Assistive-device user counts, voice-banking, and any “who wrote this” flag essentially begin later (SGD reimbursement and AAC surveys in the 2000s–2010s; voice banking ~2015; LLM drafting 2023–).

**Markets.** Korea is the hole. Postal volumes exist from the mid-2000s; PIAAC exists; Papago exists as a product. Missing: greeting-card unit history, nengajo-equivalent (설날/추석 cards) as an official series, AAC user panels, and any AI-draft poll in Korean. Japan has excellent nengajo and mail series, weak AAC panels, and no published AI-body-text attach rate. The US has the best clinical and card-industry series, weakest official “personal letter” split inside First-Class.

**Exposures.**
- *Execution restored for LETTER specifically:* no source counts “handwritten personal letters sent by people who could not have sent them last year.” Postal volume is the doing of the *system*, not the restoration of a person’s capacity. The ALS/AAC material is the right *kind* and the wrong *object* (speech, not a posted manuscript).
- *Judgment substituted for LETTER specifically:* no source before 2023 separates author of the sentences from sender of the object in a physical letter. Greeting-card verse is the best pre-2023 proxy and still leaves the sender’s choice of card intact. JP corporate nengajo is mass substitution of judgment but is not personal LETTER.
- *Distance as a restoration axis:* international letter-post volumes exist (UPU, Japan dispatched international letters, Korea international 일반통상). They do not say whether technology restored a letter that would otherwise not have been written, or replaced a visit.
- *Literacy as restoration:* PIAAC gives the stock of people who cannot write; it does not give the flow of letters written *for* them by software or scribes.
- *A three-market annual series that holds the LETTER definition fixed for 30 years* — does not exist. Building one would require splicing postal single-piece correspondence, a handwriting/authorship survey (never fielded annually), and post-2022 AI-attach data that is so far private.

**Kinds of data that would have covered the holes if anyone had collected them.**  
(1) A postal characteristics study, every year, in all three markets, that flags handwritten vs printed vs hybrid and personal vs transactional (USPS has occasional household correspondence estimates; not annual, not JP/KR).  
(2) AAC device logs: stored-phrase vs newly composed vs model-completed, by year.  
(3) Greeting-card and nengajo printers’ attach rate for stock verse vs customer-authored vs model-authored body text.  
(4) A Korea 연하장/인사 카드 unit series analogous to Japan’s 연賀状発行枚数.  
(5) Adult literacy *and letter-writing incidence* on the same survey (PIAAC background questionnaire does not do this).
