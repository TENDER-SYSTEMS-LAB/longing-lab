Training cut-off for unaided attestation is mid-2025. Rows that depend on 2025–2026 releases were checked against live public pages and are marked as such. Nothing after September 2026 is attested.

The reference practice throughout is LETTER: a personally composed, physically handwritten message for a particular person, released through a delivery route. Related practices (meeting, calling, gifting, asking after someone) are noted only where a source actually covers them.

## Section 3 — Sources that observe both, or the boundary

These are acts that read as restoration or as substitution depending on intensity. What, if anything, in the source would tell them apart.

**Predictive text / Japanese IME / Korean hangul IMEs / T9 / Gboard / iOS QuickType.**  
What is observed: keystrokes, suggestions shown, sometimes suggestions accepted.  
What would separate the two: *acceptance of a whole clause or stored greeting* is substitution; *acceptance of the next character or orthographic conversion* (kana–kanji, hangul jamo assembly) is restoration of doing. Almost no published series records acceptance at that grain. Product histories only clock when the feature existed.

**Autocomplete and Smart Compose / Smart Reply.**  
Company papers sometimes report acceptance rates. A one-word completion after the user has chosen the sentence is doing; a three-line suggested reply is judgment. Public data almost never say which.

**Voice synthesis from a learned or banked voice.**  
Team Gleason request counts and the 2021 ALS AAC survey record banking and device use. Restoration if the person composes the utterance; substitution if they play a stored phrase (“I love you”) they did not form that day. The OHSU survey asked about stored phrases and word prediction — that is the rare instrument that sits on the boundary.

**Speech neuroprostheses (2023–24 clinical papers).**  
Observe decoded words per minute versus the person’s prior AAC rate. Restoration of doing if the decoded string is the intended sentence; substitution if the system maps neural activity onto a small closed set of phrases. Papers usually report open-vocabulary rates, which lean restoration.

**Machine translation of a personal message.**  
Traffic and weekly-use surveys observe the act. Restoration if the user wrote a full text and asked only for lexical transfer they could have checked; substitution if they typed a gist and accepted the system’s greeting. Nothing in the public traffic data records that distinction. DeepL/Google document-translation versus phrase-box would be a weak proxy (document = user-composed).

**Print-and-mail and nengajo printing.**  
Internet 우체국 / インターネット郵便局 / USPS online postage observe a digitally initiated physical object. Restoration of delivery-doing. Substitution if the printed body is a template. Korea’s official split of 인터넷우체국 filings from window filings is the cleanest administrative line; it still does not say who wrote the body.

**Greeting cards with a blank inner leaf.**  
Industry reports sometimes split single cards vs boxed, premium vs standard, not “verse + blank” vs “verse only.” A handwritten addendum on a printed verse is mixed. No source in this round scores that mix by year and market.

**Summarisation and “write this more warmly.”**  
GenAI product telemetry (OpenAI “writing” / “doing” buckets from 2025–26) is the first widely published split of *ask* versus *do*. It still does not say whether the output was then copied onto paper and posted.

## Section 4 — A first sort by quantity and quality

Not a selection. Quantity = year-market cells filled out of 90 (30 × 3). Quality = inference steps from what is counted to the exposure, and whether the definition holds across the span.

### Postal traffic (official totals)
- **USPS First-Class volume** — Quantity: 30 (US only). Quality: 3+ steps (class ≠ personal ≠ handwritten ≠ restored doing). Definition fairly stable after the 2008 parcel/international break.
- **Japan 内国郵便 / 第一種・第二種** — Quantity: 30 (JP). Quality: 3 steps. First/second-class split helps a little. Definition stable enough across corporatisation.
- **Korea 보통우편 / 국내물량** — Quantity: ~20–25 (KR, from mid-2000s). Quality: 3 steps. No personal-letter split. Definition changes around 2006 in some tables.
- **Nengajo issued / New Year delivery** — Quantity: 30 (JP). Quality: 2 steps to “personal greeting sent,” 3+ to LETTER as defined (many are printed templates). Definition of “issued” vs “delivered on 1 Jan” must not be mixed.

### Household / person-level mail diaries and surveys
- **USPS Household Diary Study** — Quantity: ~25 (US; personal-letter line usable ~2001–2025). Quality: 2 steps to personal physical correspondence; 3 to handwritten LETTER; 4 to restored doing. Best quality official series in the set. Definition of “letter from friend or relative” holds across published years.
- **JP nengajo sender surveys (NLI, Pilot, commercial)** — Quantity: 3–15 depending on which survey (JP only; not annual for all). Quality: 2 steps to “I sent a New Year card,” 3 to method of composition. Definitions jump across vendors.
- **KR personal-letter surveys** — Quantity: near 0 as a comparable series. Quality: n/a.

### Greeting-card industry
- **US units and revenue** — Quantity: ~15–30 (US). Quality: 2 steps to physical token sent; 1 step to *substituted judgment* if the verse is pre-written; 3+ to LETTER. Occasion definitions stable.

### Assistive communication
- **SGD/AAC market reports** — Quantity: few reliable year-market cells (US better than JP/KR; often global + regional %). Quality: 2 steps to “device in a home,” 4 to LETTER-doing restored.
- **OHSU ALS AAC survey** — Quantity: 1. Quality: high for the reference case; asks prediction and stored phrases. Not a series.
- **Voice-banking requests (Team Gleason)** — Quantity: 2 cited years, US. Quality: 2 steps to restoration of *voice*, 4 to LETTER.

### Connectivity and cost
- **ITU / WDI / MIC / KOSIS internet and mobile** — Quantity: 90 if one accepts national annual series. Quality: 3–4 steps to LETTER doing (distance collapsed ≠ letter written). Definitions of “internet user” shift in the 1990s–2000s then settle.
- **Postage tariffs** — Quantity: 90. Quality: 2 steps to cost barrier, 3 to doing restored. Definitions (weight steps) change; real-price work is extra.

### Input methods and translation
- **IME / predictive-text product clocks** — Quantity: presence/absence can be coded for ~60–90 cells; intensity almost 0. Quality: 1 step to “suggestion existed,” 3 to substituted judgment of LETTER.
- **MT traffic / weekly-use surveys** — Quantity: a handful of recent years × 3 markets. Quality: 2 steps to wording handed over; 4 to LETTER.
- **GenAI writing use (OpenAI, OECD.AI)** — Quantity: ~3–4 years × 3 markets from 2023. Quality: 2 steps to substituted composition of *some* text; 3–4 to LETTER. Definition of “writing” is internally consistent in OpenAI’s 2025–26 notes but did not exist before.

### Time-use and literacy
- **ATUS / STULA / KTUS** — Quantity: every-year US from 2003 (~23 cells); JP/KR every fifth year (~6–7 each). Quality: 2–4 steps depending on whether a letter code survives in microdata. Activity taxonomies change.
- **PIAAC / literacy** — Quantity: 2–3 cycles × 3. Quality: 3+ steps. Slow.

### Administrative disability mail
- **Free/braille/matter-for-the-blind** — Quantity: partial series in KR official tables; US and JP legal classes exist but published annual personal-letter extracts are thin. Quality: 1–2 steps to doing restored for a named group. Small counts.

## Section 5 — What no source covers

**Years.** 1996–about 2000 are thin everywhere except raw postal totals, postage tariffs, and early internet-user counts. Personal-letter content, AAC intensity, prediction acceptance, and any judgment-substitution measure are essentially empty in those years for all three markets.

**Markets.** Korea has official mail totals, office counts, internet-post-office filings, and connectivity, but no public household diary that isolates personal letters or greeting cards the way the USPS HDS does, and no nengajo-like national census of a handwritten ritual. Japan has the ritual census (nengajo) and rich IME history, but no HDS equivalent. The United States has the diary and the card industry, but no national nengajo analogue and weaker official IME statistics.

**Exposures.**
- *Execution restored, handwriting itself:* no source counts, year by year, handwritten personal letters as a share of personal letters, in any of the three markets. Print-versus-hand surveys exist as scraps in Japan (nengajo method) and nowhere as a 30-year panel.
- *Execution restored, disability:* no annual registry of letters produced on SGDs or by speech-BCI and then mailed, in any market. Clinical papers and one US ALS survey are the ceiling.
- *Execution restored, literacy and cost:* postage and literacy exist; their join to “people who could not send and now can” does not.
- *Judgment substituted, inside LETTER:* no source separates composer from sender for physical personal letters across years. Greeting-card verse is the best proxy and it is not LETTER. GenAI writing series begin only in 2023 and are not postal.
- *The boundary (prediction / stored phrase / translation / template):* feature launch dates are known; acceptance intensity by year and market is not a public series.

**Kinds of data that would have covered the holes.**  
A household diary, in all three markets, that records for each outbound personal piece: handwritten / mixed / printed; composed by sender / jointly / by a template or model; assistive device used or not; language of composition vs language of sending. An AAC registry that follows messages out to the post. An IME/keyboard telemetry series of accepted-suggestion length. A nengajo-style census in Korea and the US for ordinary personal letters, not only New Year cards. Those series were not found in this round; the last of them is marked speculative because it is not known to have been collected.
