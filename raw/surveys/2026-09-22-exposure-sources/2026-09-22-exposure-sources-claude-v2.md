## Section 5 — What no source covers

**Judgment substituted, 1996 to about 2014, all three markets.** Nothing measures content delegation directly. Only proxies exist: greeting-card verse, nengajo templates, letter manuals, card software. What would cover it: a repeated diary or survey item asking who chose the words of a message sent.

**Judgment substituted, Japan and Korea, before 2023.** Only software sales and translation launch dates. No platform disclosures specific to these markets at the decision level. J4 and J5 are global, and their country cuts are thin.

**Decision-level separation in any market-year.** Only J4, J6, B1, B3, B6 and B7 separate doing from deciding. All are after 2015, one-off or platform-controlled, and none form an official annual series. What would cover it: published acceptance rates of full suggested content (versus word completion) by market and year.

**Execution restored for LETTER as defined.** Nothing found in any market or year that measures restoration of physically handwritten composition. Relay, SGDs, dictation and voice banking restore CALLING, typing and speaking. Handwriting robots (B12) appear only commercially and are aimed at senders, not at people who lost the ability to write. What would cover it: supply records for adaptive writing aids, or user-driven pen devices in disability-benefit data.

**Restoration series before programme start dates.** Korean relay before 2005; Japanese relay before 2013; US SGD claims before 2001 (and public files before about 2013); Japanese 意思伝達装置 before 2006 under a different category.

**Letter-level practice data by market.** The US has the Household Diary Study but no national survey of handwriting attitudes. Japan and Korea have attitude surveys (B9) but no mail diary. Korean postal statistics probably do not separate personal correspondence.

**2026 in all markets, and 2025 editions published after late May 2026.** Outside what I can attest. Several annual series (E12, E14, E15, J1–J3) should have 2025 data, but I cannot confirm release.
