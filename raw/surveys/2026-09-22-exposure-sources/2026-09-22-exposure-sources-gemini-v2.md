### Section 3 — Sources that Observe Both, or the Boundary Between Them

The boundary between restoration and substitution hinges on **granularity of agency**: whether technology serves as a mechanical conduit for human intent (restoration) or actively shapes the semantic content and decision (substitution).

#### 1. Mobile Keyboard & Input Method Editor (IME) Keylogging / Diagnostic Datasets

* **Holder/Source:** Mobile OS Vendors (Apple, Google, Samsung) / Academic IME Input Corpora.
* **Observes:** Micro-level keystroke timing, touch coordinates, character insertion, and auto-correct/autocomplete acceptance events.
* **Boundary Marker:**
* *Restoration:* User enters individual keys or uses stroke-recognition/swiping; the technology converts physical touch movements into intended characters (restoring fine motor writing).
* *Substitution:* User types 1–2 characters and selects a multi-word predictive candidate generated by the language model.
* *Differentiation Variable:* **Keystroke Savings Ratio (KSR)** and **Selection Source Flag** (character tap vs. semantic candidate tap).



#### 2. Speech-to-Text & Synthetic Voice AAC Systems

* **Holder/Source:** Clinical Rehab Registries / Speech Recognition Providers (Nuance, Apple, Google).
* **Observes:** Acoustic/text logs comparing spoken audio input to final output text.
* **Boundary Marker:**
* *Restoration:* User vocalizes or uses eye-gaze to spell words phonetically or character-by-character, and the system synthesizes the exact dictated text into speech/letter form.
* *Substitution:* User selects a high-level concept or emotional icon (e.g., "Feeling unwell"), and the system generates and voices a fully formed, polished multi-sentence paragraph.
* *Differentiation Variable:* **Semantic Expansion Factor** (Ratio of input tokens/intent markers to output tokens). A factor close to 1.0 indicates pure execution restoration; >2.0 indicates judgment substitution.



#### 3. Real-Time Machine Translation & Localization Interfaces (e.g., Naver Papago, Google Translate)

* **Holder/Source:** Web and Mobile Translation App Telemetry.
* **Observes:** Source text entered vs. target text output, alongside user post-editing edits.
* **Boundary Marker:**
* *Restoration:* User inputs a completely self-composed letter in Language A to communicate across a language barrier with a recipient in Language B. Technology restores the execution of cross-cultural reach.
* *Substitution:* User inputs a vague prompt or selects "Formality: High" / "Tone: Polite", letting the translation/politeness engine rewrite the interpersonal honorifics and stylistic judgment (especially critical in Japanese *Keigo* or Korean *Jondetmal*).
* *Differentiation Variable:* **Post-Editing Rate (PER)** and **Style-Modifier Toggle Usage**. Zero post-editing with automated style/formality switches indicates full judgment substitution.



#### 4. Generative Draft & Summarization Extensions in Email Clients

* **Holder/Source:** Enterprise/Consumer Email Extensions (Gmail Smart Compose, Outlook Copilot).
* **Observes:** Prompt length vs. output length, edit distance, and time spent on draft interface before hitting send.
* **Boundary Marker:**
* *Restoration:* User writes a full rough draft and uses software for spelling correction, grammar check, or legibility formatting.
* *Substitution:* User types a bullet point ("Say thanks for dinner") and accepts an AI-generated 2-page formal letter without modifying the prose.
* *Differentiation Variable:* **Levenshtein Edit Distance** between generated draft and sent text. High edit distance = user retained judgment; low edit distance = technology took over judgment.

### Section 5 — What No Source Covers

#### 1. Specific Uncovered Cells (Matrix Gaps)

* **Korea & Japan Pre-2005 Granular Disability/Assistive Data:** Precise counts of digital communication aids distributed for speech/motor loss are unaggregated or embedded in broader non-digitized regional welfare categories prior to ~2005.
* **Early-Era Judgment Substitution (1996–2005) Across All Markets:** No systematic source captured the extent to which desktop software templates (e.g., Microsoft Word Letter Wizards, early Japanese postcard printing software) substituted composition judgment versus merely formatting text.
* **Cross-Border Personal Communication Volumes (1996–2010):** Physical international personal letters between US, Korea, and Japan were pooled into general international surface/airmail statistics without separation from commercial or legal documents.

#### 2. The Unmeasured Ideal Data Types

* **Continuous Long-term Micro-Level Composition Logs:** A 30-year longitudinal panel logging individual keystrokes, voice dictations, and accepted predictive suggestions across both analog-to-digital shifts and AI shifts does not exist.
* *What would cover it:* A unified, privacy-compliant longitudinal panel tracking the ratio of **manually typed/written characters to algorithmically generated characters** across all personal messaging media from 1996 to 2026.


* **True Intent-to-Output Ratio in Assistive Communication:** No source systematically records whether a non-verbal/disabled user dictated an exact word-for-word sentiment or relied on an assistant/algorithm to construct the phrasing.
* *What would cover it:* Clinical device telemetry logging **input effort (gaze-dwells, taps) vs. output token length** in speech-generating devices across multi-decade cohorts.


* **Qualitative "Deliberation Time" Measurements:** No public or private statistical series measures the time a human spends deliberating over *what to say* in a personal letter versus time spent physically executing the writing.
* *What would cover it:* Longitudinal active-screen/pen-on-paper pause-time telemetry during personal letter drafting.
