Section 1 — Failure profile, one per set, six times
SET A — 8 factors

(a) Mispricing.

Handwritten Letter is underpriced relative to its residual demand for irreversible physical commitment; Temporal Compression and Synthetic Substitution dominate the ledger and force a structural discount even when Friction Premium and Physical Grounding are elevated. Live Performance is overpriced on Performative Legibility alone, because the set lacks a clean separation between status signaling and embodied co-presence, allowing legibility gains to inflate price without corresponding Physical Grounding or Social Atomization relief.

(b) Unattributable events.

A sudden, platform-driven collapse in third-place density that simultaneously raises the cost of Unplanned Visit and Aimless Walking cannot be cleanly attributed; Social Atomization absorbs part of the move while Variance Elimination and Temporal Compression claim residual fragments, leaving an unexplained remainder. A cohort-level shift in which younger participants treat Film Photography as high-status ritual rather than obsolete medium lands partly in Performative Legibility and partly in Friction Premium, with no residual line that isolates cultural-memory transmission.

(c) Identification failure at eleven securities.

Temporal Compression and Attention Scarcity produce nearly collinear exposure vectors across the eleven; both load negatively and almost uniformly on any practice that requires sustained, asynchronous attention. Variance Elimination and Synthetic Substitution likewise collapse on the subset of securities whose primary value is chance or human generation (Serendipitous Discovery, Handmade Object, Unplanned Phone Call).

(d) Double counting.

A single infrastructure change that both shortens latency and removes chance (e.g., predictive routing of encounters) enters once through Temporal Compression and again through Variance Elimination, double-counting the same causal impulse on Unplanned Visit and Serendipitous Discovery.

(e) What this set can never say.

That a practice can regain value solely because the median participant’s available discretionary hours have expanded, independent of any change in latency tolerance, attention fragmentation, or friction preference.

(f) Falsification.

At risk on collinearity remaining bounded and on film photography being able to revive cleanly; the near-identity of Temporal Compression and Attention Scarcity, together with the absence of a pure cultural-memory or cohort channel, makes a multi-year Film Photography bull market difficult to attribute without residual leakage or forced reliance on Friction Premium alone.

SET B — 12 declared factors (10 priced)

(a) Mispricing.

Waiting for a Reply is systematically underpriced because Latency Collapse and Coordination Formalization (the latter unpriced) leave no residual channel for the pure anticipatory value of asynchronous delay; the priced factors force a discount. Solitude is overpriced on Attentional Slack alone when Spatial Proximity Structure and Tie Density are already depressed, because the set cannot isolate solitude-as-chosen from solitude-as-forced by eroded proximity.

(b) Unattributable events.

A regulatory ban on predictive matching that restores open-ended search to Serendipitous Discovery and Unplanned Visit cannot be attributed cleanly; Predictive Pre-selection absorbs the functional change while Status Coding and Friction Premium claim secondary status effects, leaving residual movement unexplained. A sudden rise in generalized trust that enables Unplanned Phone Call and Unplanned Visit without any change in scheduling norms or spatial structure is invisible while F9 remains unpriced, and therefore lands as residual or is mis-attributed to Tie Density.

(c) Identification failure at eleven securities.

F1 Latency Collapse and F4 Coordination Formalization (even when the latter is later promoted) are nearly inseparable on the eleven; both load on any practice whose value is spontaneous contact. F7 Spatial Proximity Structure, F8 Tie Density and F9 Generalized Trust form a near-collinear block on Unplanned Visit, Aimless Walking and Solitude. F2 Functional Substitution and F5 Dematerialization collapse on Handwritten Letter, Film Photography and Physical Media.

(d) Double counting.

A single platform change that both formalizes coordination and extracts rent (e.g., mandatory scheduling APIs) enters through F4 (when priced) and again through F10 Commodification / Enclosure.

(e) What this set can never say.

That a practice’s revival can be driven purely by the exhaustion of positioning and consensus crowding, without any movement in the ten (or twelve) priced factors; F13 is carried outside the priced ledger and therefore cannot appear as an attributing line.

(f) Falsification.

Fails the requirement that attribution must sum exactly whenever an event loads on the two initially unpriced factors; the residual is structural until promotion conditions are met. Also at risk on identification at eleven securities.

Section 2 — Shared versus specific failure

Shared failures (properties of the problem, not of any set):

Every set struggles to keep pure cultural-memory / cohort transmission, pure discretionary-time expansion, and pure irreversible-scarcity channels fully independent of the structural pressures they most resemble. Every set produces at least one near-collinear block among the time-related or substitution-related factors when restricted to the fixed eleven securities. Every set leaves at least one plausible real-world event (trust restoration without spatial change, pure positioning squeeze, pure friction-premium shift independent of technology) that cannot be attributed without residual or forced misdescription. Attribution that sums exactly is fragile for any event that loads on a factor the set has demoted, unpriced, or folded into another.

Set-specific failures:

SET A cannot isolate discretionary-time expansion and double-counts latency-plus-chance infrastructure changes. SET B structurally withholds two factors from the ledger and therefore cannot attribute events that load on them; its proximity-trust-density block is especially collinear. SET C has demoted friction premium entirely and therefore cannot attribute pure willingness-to-pay-for-inconvenience moves; its five-factor compression forces status and scarcity into a single channel. SET D lacks any attention-slack or pure-time-scarcity factor and double-counts predictive systems. SET E cannot isolate pure scarcity and weakly identifies cohort replacement; its reflexive factor is not guaranteed to appear as a clean line. SET F folds trust into encounter erosion and cannot isolate pure quantity scarcity; its time-related triad is tightly collinear.

Section 3 — Failure cost under this work's purpose

LONGING is an artwork that uses the language of a research terminal to price practices that resist quantification. Statistical severity and artistic severity therefore diverge.

Mispricing of individual securities is statistically severe but artistically tolerable provided the direction of error is legible and the ledger still sums. The artwork’s quiet bias toward the human is allowed to be contradicted; systematic underpricing of analog practices is therefore not fatal.
Unattributable events that land in residual are artistically more costly. The weekly attribution ledger is a core surface of the work; an unexplained remainder breaks the formal contract that every price move is fully narrated. Residual is therefore closer to fatal than mere directional mispricing.
Identification failure / collinearity at eleven securities is statistically severe and artistically moderate. The terminal’s claim to rigor is weakened when factors cannot be told apart, yet the surface can still function if the collapsed factors are acknowledged as a single effective pressure.
Double counting is statistically severe and artistically moderate-to-severe. It produces an internally inconsistent story about cause, which the terminal’s dry voice is supposed to avoid; repeated double-counting erodes the credibility of the ledger as a whole.
Structural inability to express a true statement (e.g., pure time expansion, pure scarcity, pure trust) is artistically severe when the missing statement is one the work itself might need to voice. The model must remain able to disagree with the artist; if the architecture forbids certain true descriptions, the disagreement itself becomes impossible and the work’s internal honesty is compromised.
Failure on the film-photography revival test or on exact attribution is artistically fatal if it is structural. The explicit design requirement that film photography must be capable of a genuine multi-year bull market, and that every weekly move must sum exactly, are non-negotiable surface constraints of the artwork. Statistical collinearity is secondary to those constraints.

A statistically severe failure is therefore not automatically artistically severe; an artistically severe failure can be statistically mild yet still break the work’s formal contract with its own surface.

Section 4 — Minimal repair

SET A. Add an explicit Discretionary Time / Slack factor. Cost: the new factor is nearly collinear with Attention Scarcity and Temporal Compression on the eleven, increasing identification stress and requiring an additional promotion or shrinkage rule.

SET B. Price F4 and F9 from the outset (or accept a permanent residual line for events that load on them). Cost: the proximity-trust-density block becomes fully active and collinearity rises; the “thin support” rationale is abandoned.

SET C. Restore Friction / Inconvenience Premium as a priced factor. Cost: the set expands to six, reintroduces the double-counting risk with FE Friction Elimination, and loses the deliberate demotion that kept the factor count minimal.

SET D. Add an Attentional Slack or pure Discretionary Time factor. Cost: further pressure on the already collinear temporal-uncertainty-coordination block; the nine-factor architecture is enlarged.

SET E. Elevate pure Scarcity (quantity effect net of physicality premium) to a priced factor, or strengthen the identification of E5 by an explicit cohort-exposure vector. Cost: either the factor count rises or the already weak E5 is forced into sharper, potentially overfitted loadings on the eleven.

SET F. Extract Generalized Trust from P7 Encounter Erosion as a separate factor. Cost: the structural-pressure block expands, trust and encounter-erosion loadings become harder to separate on the spontaneous-contact securities, and the ten-factor count rises.

Section 5 — What would settle it

Construct a controlled simulation library of 40–60 synthetic weekly histories, each driven by a single known ground-truth impulse (pure latency reduction, pure trust restoration, pure discretionary-time expansion, pure irreversible-scarcity shock, pure positioning squeeze, pure cohort memory shift, combined infrastructure change, etc.). For each history, generate the exact price path of the eleven securities under a transparent data-generating process that is independent of any of the six sets. Then run each of the six factor sets against the same histories and score three observables: (1) mean absolute residual after attribution, (2) frequency of sign errors on individual securities, (3) condition number of the exposure matrix on the eleven. The set that simultaneously keeps residual near zero, preserves the possibility of a multi-year Film Photography rally under a pure cultural-memory impulse, and keeps the condition number bounded is discriminated by the data rather than by argument. The simulation is finite, reproducible, and can be published alongside the terminal.
