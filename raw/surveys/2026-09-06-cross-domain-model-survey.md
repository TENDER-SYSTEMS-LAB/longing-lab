# Cross-domain Mathematical Models and Designed Systems

Date: 2026-09-06
Attribution: llm-synthesis

This is an original research catalog of 120 distinct works or project lineages, compiled in response to the separately registered user request. It is not an archive or verbatim reproduction of the linked papers. External links identify the primary work, author-hosted copy, publisher record, institutional research record, or official project description.

## Scope and reading limits

The catalog contains 117 substantive model/system entries, one bibliographic lead (Leontief), and two adjacent data-mapping artworks. It deliberately includes descriptive models, generative simulations, institutional mechanisms, engineered systems, and algorithmic art. These are different kinds of formalization, not 120 equivalent or validated scientific explanations. Reviews, books, working papers, and projects are labeled instead of being called journal papers.

Each entry states only the modeling or design target. This pass does not reproduce equations, assess model validity, compare predictive accuracy, or recommend a LONGING implementation. Author/site claims about a system's aims are not evidence that it accurately models reality. Models concerning emotions, cultural change, violence, and ecological collapse retain this distinction.

Discovery used targeted web searches followed by publisher, author, institutional, and official project results across the fourteen categories below. Screening relied on accessible abstracts, indexed primary-source excerpts, and project documentation; selected pages and originals were opened for clarification. This is a broad discovery survey, not a systematic review or a full-text review of all entries. The literature cannot be exhaustively collected in one pass. Candidate families were retained for distinct modeling targets, not split into every derivative paper.

Access limits: some publisher routes returned access errors or metadata only. Latané and Ratcliff were checked through APA-provided publisher previews on ResearchGate. Richardson was checked through limited original-book preview and bibliographic information, not the full monograph. Leontief is explicitly a title/bibliography-level lead and is not counted among the 117 substantive entries. For sources located in indexed author/publisher excerpts, that is not a claim of direct full-PDF retrieval. Kermack–McKendrick links to a later republication of the 1927 work; the Turing link is a later collected edition of the 1952 work. Dates identify the original work or explicitly named reference edition, not search-engine crawl dates.

Five papers already covered in the initial survey are retained for navigation across this broader map: Bass, Abrams–Strogatz, Candia, Loewenstein, and Rohlfs. The Petrarch–Laura case is a different Rinaldi paper from the earlier linear love-dynamics card. The three previously mentioned market artworks are included with refreshed official-page checks. No prior-project decision has been changed.
