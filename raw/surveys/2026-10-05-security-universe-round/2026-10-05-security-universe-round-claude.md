# A. Critique of the thirty

**Fails or barely passes a test or rule**

- **1**: *issue*, barely passes. The bond is settled at the moment it is issued. Nothing remains outstanding, so it feeds belief as an event, not as stock.
- **5**: *issue*, ambiguous. An apology repairs an existing bond rather than issuing a new one. It passes only if the ontology treats repair as reissue, and that should be decided explicitly.
- **7**: *act, not form*, weak. The practice is defined by the absence of a device ("unprompted"). *Loss record* is also weak: a remembered birthday and a reminded one leave the same trace.
- **8**: *act, not form*. "Calling" names a medium. The act is reaching out to someone for no reason.
- **10**: *loss record*, weak. This is private and leaves little difference between records. "Until late" is a form attribute.
- **14**: *act, not form*. "By showing" names a medium (body). The act is teaching someone.
- **15**: *exposure*, weak. Feeds act on this practice only indirectly. A stronger channel is the device that makes exit cheap: block, mute, unfollow.
- **17**: *act, not form*. Like 7, it is defined by a device being absent. The act is keeping an appointment on trust, or waiting for someone who said they would come.
- **18**: *outside the market*, edge case. Obligatory, unpaid family care can drift into the exploitative relations the ontology excludes. The channel is also two-sided: remote monitoring and telemedicine restore as well as take.
- **22**: *act, not form*. "At a counter" names a place. *Issue* also barely passes, as with 1.
- **25**: *exposure*. "Individual schedules" is a change in how work is organised, not a technology channel. Delivery does not take over the shared meal; it takes over cooking.
- **26**: *act, not form*. "By hand" is a body attribute. The act is making something for someone, which overlaps with 6.
- **28**: *act, not form*, mild. "Public forum" names a venue. The act is asking a question of people you don't know.
- **29**: *act, not form*. Distance is a place attribute. It is still the list's main arrival and restoration case, so removing it would break coverage rule 3.
- **30**: *issue*, indirect. This passes only through the two-person bonds a gathering produces, and the practice as worded doesn't show that.

**Overlaps that would move together**

- **3 / 19** are the same exchange seen from the two sides, and both are exposed to the same AI counsel and companion channel.
- **13 / 14** are the apprentice and master sides of one exchange.
- **12 / 24 / 27** are favours among people who know each other, all exposed to on-demand services. 27 is an instance of 12.
- **23 / 24** are both open accounts between friends, taken by exact settlement.
- **4 / 5** share the AI-drafting channel and would track each other closely.
- **2 / 6 / 11** are all driven by recommendation and matching systems.
- **1 / 22 / 28** are all one-off appeals to strangers feeding belief.
- **6 / 26** are both making or choosing something for someone.
- **8 / 10** are both unstructured talk.

**Hard to understand at a glance**

- **7**: "unprompted" needs explaining.
- **16**: "optimisers" is jargon, and "deciding together" is abstract.
- **17**: the phrasing is awkward.
- **21**: the channel "restoration of voice" is unclear.
- **25**: "arranged together" is muddy; "eating together" is what an audience would recognise.

**Little meaning inside the work**

- **27** is a mundane instance of 12.
- **24** adds little beside 23, which carries the same meaning with more charm.
- **21** is pleasant but narrow next to 20.

# C. Coverage check

**Thinnest balance.** By count, belief is now well fed. Structurally it is thin, though. Many belief practices are one-off encounters with strangers that barely pass *issue*: 1, 22, the lift, giving to a stranger. Trust has the opposite problem. It is fed almost entirely by declining practices. Its only strong risers are secondhand selling, ride-sharing and remittances, so trust is where the decline looks most selected.

**Thinnest channel.** Restoration is thinnest: 21, 29, and the two restoration rows added above. Among arrivals, 19th- and early 20th-century arrivals touch very few practices. Post reaches only 4 and transport only 9, while railways, cheap print and the telegraph reach nothing. Without them, the period from 1811 to 1990 has almost no gives.

**A structural gap.** The ontology has no channel for rises that are not technological. Longer lives, smaller families, shorter working hours and love marriage have all raised practices (playing with a child, asking someone out). These rises either get credited to no channel or get credited to the wrong one.

**Kinds of life still missing:**

- Childhood: friendship between children.
- Old age and dying: staying with someone as they die appears only as a variant of 18.
- Conflict and justice: forgiveness, reconciliation, settling a dispute without institutions.
- Civic life: deliberation, juries, petitioning. These are hard to fit because the two-person rule excludes much of public life by design.
- Physical intimacy, perhaps deliberately.
- Migration, beyond remittances.
- Rural and seasonal life, beyond mutual labour.

**What could go wrong:** a state priced through practices moves only when its carriers move, so it adds a ticker without adding information, and whoever picks the carriers has quietly decided the state's trajectory.
