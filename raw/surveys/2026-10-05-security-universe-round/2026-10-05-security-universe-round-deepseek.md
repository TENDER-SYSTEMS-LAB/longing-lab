## A. Critique of the thirty

**Fails or barely passes**

| # | Test or rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | issue | The bond to a stranger is momentary and may not keep issuing coupons; it can pass only as a very thin two-person ask/response. |
| 7 | practice by act | “Remembering” is a capacity or state, not an act with steps; recast as “wishing someone on their day without being reminded.” |
| 17 | act, not form | Defined by the absence of tracking, so it is negative form; recast as “keeping an agreed appointment on trust.” |
| 22 | act, not form | “Counter” is a setting; the act is small talk. If kept, place must be an attribute, not the definition. |
| 26 | act, not form | “By hand” is a mode/medium; recast as “making a gift oneself for someone.” |
| 28 | act, not form | “Public forum” risks becoming medium-defined; recast as “asking a question where many strangers can answer.” |
| 29 | practice by act | “Keeping a friendship at a distance” is a bundle of acts, not one practice with steps; split into writing, calling, visiting, checking in. |

**Overlaps that would move together**

- 3, 10, 15, 19: talk-based understanding; all exposed to AI counsel, feeds, or AI companions.
- 4, 5: writing/apologising in one’s own words; both exposed to AI drafting.
- 6, 26: gift-love; both exposed to recommendation and generated content.
- 9, 18, 25: presence/care/meal; all decline with delivery, video calls, and individual schedules.
- 12, 27: neighbourly help; both exposed to on-demand services.
- 13, 14: craft teaching/learning; both exposed to tutorials and AI.
- 23, 24: informal exchange; both exposed to exact settlement and credit services.
- 28, 30: strangers/gathering; both exposed to social web and personalised feeds.
- 8, 29: calling/distance friendship; both exposed to asynchronous messaging and social web.

**Audience/meaning problems**

- 7 is clear but not an act; it would read as a mood, not a practice.
- 17 is understandable but may read as a nostalgic prohibition rather than a practice.
- 22 and 28 may read as atmosphere or medium nostalgia unless the act is sharpened.
- 29 is too broad for an audience to see what is being priced.
- 23 is clear only if “treating” is understood as paying and carrying the debt forward.

## C. Coverage check

After these additions, the thinnest balance is **belief** in practices that are not also gatherings or rituals; it is fed, but many belief practices cluster around public/ritual settings. The thinnest channel is **restoration**: it still has few practices compared with device, substitution, and arrival. Restoration is carried mainly by 21, 29, 66, 68, and 69, while device and substitution touch most of the list.

Missing kinds of human life: courtship and sexual intimacy; parenting very young children; civic and political action (voting together, protesting, jury duty, petitioning); manual and agricultural work; daily care for disabled and elderly people beyond illness; neighbourly governance and mutual aid; friendship across deep difference; and practices of public order that are not commercial.
