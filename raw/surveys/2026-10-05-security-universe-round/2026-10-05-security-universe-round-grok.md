C. Coverage check

Balances: belief and trust are well supplied; understanding is adequate; love is present but thinner once overlapping care practices are noted.  
Channels: device and substitution dominate the takes; arrival is present but thinner; restoration is the thinnest (only a few candidates show clear restoration).  
Missing kinds of human life: formal worship and ritual beyond counsel; sustained play among adults that is not interest-based gathering; long-term care of the old that is not illness; commercial apprenticeship that is not craft-learning; political or civic speech that issues bonds rather than platforms.
