## C. Coverage check

- **Thinnest balance: understanding.** Its count is adequate, but its bearers are near-variants of one behaviour — talking, teaching, confiding (3, 5, 10, 14, 15, 16, 19, 20, plus the language and rare-condition additions). The balance has many tickers and one act; they would correlate.
- **Thinnest channel: restoration.** Only 21 (mis-signed), 29, and two additions above carry it. The ontology holds that every technology gives before it takes; the list barely shows the giving, which weakens the credibility of every take.
- **Missing kinds of human life:** work and livelihood beyond the two added here (the job as a place of bonds is nearly absent); civic and public life (only "taking your turn"; voting, assembly, local office have no bearer); rites of passage (weddings, births, funerals — occasions where many listed practices concentrate, but with no bearer of their own); the receiving side of care (18 is giving care; being cared for by one's own children has none). Courtship — strikingly absent from the original thirty — is now proposed above.
