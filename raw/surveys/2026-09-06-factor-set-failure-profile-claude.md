# LONGING — Round 3: Factor Set Failure Profiles

Eleven securities, fixed: Handwritten Letter, Waiting for a Reply, Unplanned Phone Call, Unplanned Visit, Aimless Walking, Film Photography, Physical Media, Serendipitous Discovery, Handmade Object, Live Performance, Solitude.

Throughout, "the analog trio" means Film Photography, Physical Media, Handmade Object. "The contact cluster" means Unplanned Phone Call, Unplanned Visit, Live Performance.

---

# Section 1 — Failure profiles

## SET A — 8 factors

**(a) Mispricing.**

- **Solitude — priced too high, and for the wrong reason.** Social Atomization is a monotone tailwind for Solitude by construction: less community density means more time alone. But the security is *chosen* solitude, and atomization produces imposed isolation, which is its opposite. A has no factor whose sign distinguishes the two, so every deterioration in social fabric registers as a bid for Solitude. The security will drift up through exactly the events that should hollow it out.
- **Live Performance — priced too high.** Physical Grounding is a clean tailwind and A carries no mediation, platform-rent, or extraction factor to oppose it. Ticketing consolidation, dynamic pricing, and resale rents are structurally invisible; the only channel that could register them is Performative Legibility, which reads them as *more* signalling value, not less.
- **Waiting for a Reply — priced too low, and monotonically.** Its exposure is nearly a pure short on Temporal Compression, which is secular and one-directional. Friction Premium is A's only offsetting lever, and Friction Premium is calibrated off the analog trio (below), so Waiting for a Reply inherits a revival mechanism that has nothing to do with waiting.

**(b) Unattributable events.**

- *A platform-surveillance scandal collapses trust in intermediated contact.* A has no mediation factor. The move splits between Social Atomization (misdescribing a governance event as community decay) and Variance Elimination (misdescribing it as a routing story). The published ledger will name a cause that did not occur.
- *Postal rates rise 40% and Saturday delivery ends.* This is carrier viability, not demand. A carries no infrastructure or threshold mechanism, so the Handwritten Letter move lands on Physical Grounding, which reads as "society wants physical carriers less." The ledger inverts cause and effect.
- *A crowded-short squeeze in Film Photography on minor good news.* A has no market-internal factor. The move lands in Friction Premium, and the monthly research anchor then reports a cultural revaluation that never happened.

**(c) Identification failure at eleven securities.**

- **Friction Premium ↔ Performative Legibility.** Both are cyclical, both load almost entirely on the analog trio plus Handwritten Letter, both are positive in the same regimes. Across eleven securities these are one vector.
- **Synthetic Substitution ↔ Physical Grounding.** They separate only on Live Performance and Solitude. Elsewhere — the analog trio, Handwritten Letter — the same events move both, with opposite sign conventions and no independent instrument to fix the split.
- **Temporal Compression ↔ Variance Elimination** on Serendipitous Discovery and Aimless Walking: pre-selection and speed are the same intervention in these securities.

**(d) Double counting.**

- A single analog-revival event enters twice: once through Friction Premium (effort rewarded) and once through Performative Legibility (effort signals). The ledger will show two lines summing to the total for one cause, and their split will be estimated from noise.
- Physical Media's dematerialization enters through both Synthetic Substitution and Physical Grounding.

**(e) What this set can never say.**

That a practice is being abandoned by the people who most value it. A's factors are all environmental pressures and market moods; none carries the practitioner's own relationship to the practice. A can say the world made letter-writing costly. It cannot say letter-writers stopped believing in letters.

**(f) Falsification.**

- *Collinearity bounded:* **fails.** The Friction Premium / Performative Legibility pair is not separable at eleven securities.
- *Film revival:* passes, but through a mechanism shared with Waiting for a Reply and Aimless Walking, so revivals will be implausibly synchronized.
- *Structural winner:* passes — Physical Grounding can rise secularly.
- *Attribution sums exactly:* passes; with 8 factors against 11 securities there is enough residual room for this to be a real constraint rather than an artifact.
- *Contradicts the artist:* at risk. Six of eight factors are named as losses; disagreement is available only through the two cyclical factors, so the model can contradict the artist temporarily but never durably.

## SET B — 12 declared, 10 running

Profiled as it runs: F4 Coordination Formalization and F9 Generalized Trust contribute exactly zero.

**(a) Mispricing.**

- **Unplanned Visit — mispriced in mechanism, not necessarily in level.** Its dominant real cause is F4, which is switched off. The exposure will be absorbed by F1 Latency Collapse and F7 Spatial Proximity Structure. The level may be roughly right and the sensitivities badly wrong: the security is now latency-sensitive, so a messaging outage produces a rally in unplanned visits that the ledger attributes to speed rather than to the collapse of a scheduling norm.
- **Unplanned Phone Call — priced too low.** Same absorption, plus F9 off. Its decline is a consent-norm decline; with both consent factors unpriced, the move loads onto F2 Functional Substitution and F5 Dematerialization, which have no business explaining it.
- **Solitude — underreactive.** Of ten running factors, Solitude has meaningful exposure to one (F6 Attentional Slack) and near-zero to nine. It becomes an effectively idiosyncratic security inside a heavy factor structure, so its weekly line is dominated by its own fundamentals and its price path is too smooth relative to the world it lives in.

**(b) Unattributable events.**

- *A four-day working week is adopted at national scale.* This is an hours shock. B has no discretionary-time factor; F6 is defined as attention, not hours. The move lands on F6 and the ledger reports an attention event.
- *Urban rents fall and third places reopen, and trust in strangers rises with them.* F7 catches the spatial half; the trust half is unpriced, so it lands in security-level fundamentals. One social event is reported as one factor move plus three unrelated coincidences.
- *F9 is later promoted.* This is not an event in the world but it is an event in the ledger: promotion re-estimates history, so previously published attributions change retroactively. The monthly research anchor loses continuity, and prior divergences between price and anchor become uninterpretable.

**(c) Identification failure at eleven securities.**

- **F7 Spatial Proximity ↔ F8 Tie Density.** Both load on the contact cluster and nothing else. Not separable. (The set has already conceded this class of problem for F4 and F9; the concession does not go far enough.)
- **F1 Latency Collapse ↔ F3 Predictive Pre-selection** on Waiting for a Reply and Serendipitous Discovery — separable only through Aimless Walking, a single security.
- **F10 Commodification / Enclosure ↔ F3.** Platform intermediation drives both; they diverge only on Live Performance.
- Structurally: ten priced factors against eleven securities leaves one cross-sectional degree of freedom. Factor returns and idiosyncratic news become nearly interchangeable, which matters for (f).

**(d) Double counting.**

- F5 Dematerialization and F2 Functional Substitution both price the same digital-photography event on Film Photography.
- F11 Friction Premium and F12 Status Coding both price the analog revival; the four security-level mechanisms (Scarcity transform, Cultural Memory) price a third and fourth version of it. One cultural cause has up to four named homes.

**(e) What this set can never say.**

That its own unpriced factors mattered. During the unpriced period the ledger is not agnostic about F4 and F9 — it asserts they contributed zero. The set cannot express "we suspect this move was coordination formalization"; it must attribute the move elsewhere, with full confidence, in public.

**(f) Falsification.**

- *Attribution sums exactly:* passes **vacuously**. With ten factors and eleven securities the sum is nearly guaranteed by rank alone, so this test discriminates nothing here.
- *Collinearity bounded:* fails on F7/F8, and marginally on F3/F10.
- *Film revival:* passes comfortably — the set has the most machinery for it (Scarcity, Cultural Memory, Replacement Resistance).
- *Structural winner:* passes; Block II factors are genuinely two-sided.
- *Contradicts the artist:* passes.

## SET C — 5 factors

**(a) Mispricing.**

- **The analog trio — co-movement systematically underpriced.** Friction premium is demoted to a security-level fundamental, so a market-wide revaluation of effort arrives as three simultaneous idiosyncratic surprises. The correlated component of the rally is understated at the factor level and overstated in the fundamentals lines. Amplitude will be roughly right; structure will be wrong.
- **Solitude — priced too high, secularly.** Its only exposure is AS, and AS is defined to include "a premium on deeply immersive experience," which is a tailwind. C has no factor that penalizes Solitude, so it becomes a one-way security.
- **Unplanned Visit — mispriced in mechanism.** MD (Material Decoupling) is the nearest home for its decline, so the ledger reports that the visit died of dematerialization. It died of scheduling norms and distance, neither of which C prices.

**(b) Unattributable events.**

- *A crowded-short squeeze in Film Photography.* No market-internal state variable is priced. The move goes to the security's own fundamentals, and the research anchor cannot distinguish a positioning unwind from a belief change — precisely the distinction the world is built to support.
- *Postal rates rise sharply.* Infrastructure viability has no home; the move lands on MD or on fundamentals depending on fit, and the ledger's naming is unstable across weeks.
- *A platform-extraction scandal.* AD and MD both partially catch it; SR catches the status half. One event, three lines, no dominant one.

**(c) Identification failure at eleven securities.**

Least severe of the six on this axis. Real remaining collapses:

- **FE ↔ AD** on Serendipitous Discovery and Aimless Walking: removing unpredictability and displacing decision-making are the same routing intervention for these two.
- **AS ↔ FE** on Solitude and Waiting for a Reply, which are the only two securities where either factor is the primary driver.

**(d) Double counting.**

- SR (Social Revaluation, "perceived authenticity, cultural prestige") and the demoted security-level friction premium are the same cultural cause entering the ledger twice — once shared, once idiosyncratic. The demotion does not avoid the double count; it hides it in a different column.

**(e) What this set can never say.**

That a decline was collective. With five factors and heavy reliance on security-level fundamentals, C describes a synchronized cultural withdrawal as eleven private stories that happened to coincide. The world's most important claim — that these practices are declining *together*, for shared reasons — is the claim its architecture is least able to state.

**(f) Falsification.**

- *Attribution sums exactly:* passes only if security-level fundamentals are treated as named ledger lines and are allowed to be large. If a set expects the factor block to carry the move, this fails.
- *Collinearity bounded:* passes, most cleanly of the six.
- *Film revival:* passes — and can be genuinely idiosyncratic, which is a real expressive capability.
- *Structural winner:* **at risk.** Four of five factors are pressures; SR is the only two-sided one and is cultural, i.e. mean-reverting. A durable structural winner has no channel.
- *Contradicts the artist:* passes cyclically, fails secularly, for the same reason.

## SET D — 9 factors

**(a) Mispricing.**

- **Solitude — badly mispriced; no attention factor exists in this set.** The dominant pressure on solitude is attentional capture, which D does not carry anywhere. Its exposure will be forced onto F1 Temporal Compression, which is a poor proxy and now contaminated: every subsequent latency event moves Solitude.
- **Aimless Walking — same defect.** Its pressures are attention, navigation certainty, and time poverty. D prices the second (F4) and neither of the others. Level will drift high; sensitivities will be wrong.
- **Waiting for a Reply — priced too low.** F1 dominates it and F1 is now overloaded with attention exposure it should not have, so F1's estimated magnitude is inflated and Waiting for a Reply, its highest-beta security, takes the inflation.

**(b) Unattributable events.**

- *A four-day working week.* No discretionary-time factor. Lands on F1 with an implied mechanism (speed) that is the opposite of the true one (slack).
- *A widely publicised digital-detox turn among a young cohort.* Splits between F1 and F9 Social Legibility; the attention component, which is the actual cause, has no line.
- *A crowded-short squeeze.* No reflexive or positioning term in the set's own declaration; the move goes to fundamentals or, worse, to F7 Friction Valuation, which then reports a cultural shift.

**(c) Identification failure at eleven securities.**

- **F3 Intermediation Density ↔ F4 Uncertainty Suppression.** Both are supported almost entirely by Serendipitous Discovery and Aimless Walking. The platform layer and the pre-selection it performs are the same vector here.
- **F1 Temporal Compression ↔ F6 Coordination Formalization** on the contact cluster: speed and scheduling both compress spontaneity in the same securities.
- **F7 Friction Valuation ↔ F8 Scarcity Revaluation ↔ F9 Social Legibility.** Three cyclical factors, all supported by the analog trio, all rising together in a revival. A three-way collapse, the largest single identification problem in any set here.

**(d) Double counting.**

- The three-way cyclical group above: an analog revival enters the ledger three times, with the split determined by estimation noise rather than by anything in the world.
- F8 is defined "net of the quantity effect." The quantity effect is not separately observed in this world, so the net is not constructible; in practice F8 will re-price whatever F7 and F9 leave over.

**(e) What this set can never say.**

Anything about attention. In a world whose subject is the erosion of unhurried, undivided experience, this set has no name for the thing doing the eroding. It can describe every practice's decline in terms of speed, substitution, and status, and never once in terms of what a person is able to hold in mind.

**(f) Falsification.**

- *Collinearity bounded:* fails, on the F7/F8/F9 group.
- *Structural winner:* passes — F8 Scarcity Revaluation is a genuine secular upside channel, the clearest of the six sets on this specific test.
- *Film revival:* passes, over-determinedly; three factors can each produce it and the ledger cannot say which did.
- *Attribution sums exactly:* passes.
- *Contradicts the artist:* passes.

## SET E — 12 factors

Twelve priced factors against eleven securities. The exposure matrix cannot have full column rank. Everything below follows from that or compounds it.

**(a) Mispricing.**

- **Physical Media — unstable rather than simply wrong.** S2 Human Substitution and E2 Physicality Premium are near-exact opposites on the same three securities and cannot be separately identified. Their net is arbitrary week to week; Physical Media's path will show sign flips with no news, and each flip will be attributed to a named factor.
- **Solitude — its entire secular story rests on an unidentified pair.** E4 Economic Time Scarcity and E6 Attention Scarcity both load on Solitude and Aimless Walking and essentially nowhere else with independent sign. Solitude's drift is therefore a coin flip between "people have no hours" and "people have no focus," reported with certainty.
- **Any single crowded security — mispriced by the architecture.** See (d).

**(b) Unattributable events.**

- *A crowded-short squeeze in one security.* E prices R1 Positioning as a *common* factor. A single-security crowding event must be expressed as a market-wide positioning move, which drags all eleven securities. The ledger will report a market-wide unwind when one security was crowded, and the other ten get contaminated lines.
- *A film stock is discontinued, then a new one is announced.* This is supply and infrastructure. E declares twelve priced factors and no security-level mechanisms; the event has no named home in the set as given.
- *Any slow, unmodeled secular drift.* It will land on E5 Demographic Cohort Replacement, which at weekly frequency is nearly indistinguishable from a linear trend and therefore from the intercept. E5 is a sink.

**(c) Identification failure at eleven securities.**

- **Rank deficiency is guaranteed**, not conditional. At minimum one factor is an exact linear combination of the others.
- **S3 Mediation ↔ S4 Uncertainty Compression:** supported by the same two or three securities.
- **E4 ↔ E6:** as above.
- **E1 Friction Premium ↔ E2 Physicality Premium ↔ E3 Social Legibility:** all three cyclical, all supported by the analog trio.
- **E5 ↔ trend:** collinear with time itself.
- **E7 Trust** is supported by the contact cluster only, which also supports S1 and S3.

**(d) Double counting.**

- Positioning, priced as a common factor, double counts with whatever security-level positioning state the world already tracks separately. The brief's stated mechanic (positioning distinct from belief, per security) and E's architecture (positioning shared across securities) are two representations of one thing, and both will be in the ledger.
- The three-way cyclical group double counts a revival, as in SET D.

**(e) What this set can never say.**

Which of its layers is responsible. The three-layer architecture promises a clean secular / cultural / market-internal decomposition, and rank deficiency means that decomposition is not recoverable from the data. The set's central expressive claim is the one thing its dimensionality forbids it from making credibly.

**(f) Falsification.**

- *Collinearity bounded:* fails by construction, not by estimate.
- *Attribution sums exactly:* passes vacuously and misleadingly — an over-saturated system always sums, because idiosyncratic news has nowhere to be except inside a factor line.
- *Film revival:* passes.
- *Structural winner:* **at risk.** Layer 1 is four uniformly negative pressures; a structural winner must come from Layer 2, which is declared medium-speed and cultural, i.e. reverting.
- *Contradicts the artist:* passes cyclically; structurally constrained as above.

## SET F — 10 factors

**(a) Mispricing.**

- **Unplanned Visit and Unplanned Phone Call — forced to move together.** P7 Encounter Erosion bundles distance, third places, rent, scheduling norms, relational density, and trust into one line. Those two securities have different real drivers (the call dies of consent norms, the visit of distance plus norms), and the bundle makes them a single beta. Their divergence, which is one of the more interesting things this world can show, is not representable.
- **Solitude — inherits the wrong cyclical betas.** P9 Patience Regime and P10 Authenticity & Status Cycle are calibrated overwhelmingly on the analog trio. Solitude's own cyclical revivals (a mindfulness or detox wave) will be priced with analog-trio sensitivities, understating them.
- **Aimless Walking — indeterminate.** P3 is declared a hybrid of secular and cyclical. A hybrid's trend and its mean reversion cannot be separately estimated from one series; Aimless Walking's largest exposure is therefore to a factor with an unidentified decomposition.

**(b) Unattributable events.**

- *A crowded-short squeeze.* F has no market-internal term at all — P9 and P10 are demand-side and mean-reverting, which is not the same thing. The move lands on P10, and the ledger reports an authenticity turn.
- *A trust collapse after a surveillance scandal.* Trust is inside P7. The ledger will report encounter erosion, implying physical and spatial causes, and will drag Live Performance along with the contact cluster.
- *Vinyl sales pass CD sales while total physical media prevalence keeps falling.* Prevalence down, unit value up. F has no scarcity term; the split lands across P8 and P10 with no line for the quantity/value distinction.

**(c) Identification failure at eleven securities.**

- **P2 Attention Capture ↔ P3 Discretionary Time Poverty.** Both reduce Solitude, Aimless Walking, and Live Performance; both are ambiguous elsewhere. Not separable.
- **P5 Mediation & Extraction ↔ P6 Uncertainty Compression.** Ranking is both, and only Serendipitous Discovery supports either strongly.
- **P9 ↔ P10**, on the analog trio, as with every other set's cyclical pair.
- Ten factors against eleven securities: one degree of freedom, with the consequences noted for SET B.

**(d) Double counting.**

- P5 contains ranking (a pre-selection mechanism) and extraction (a rent mechanism). The ranking half double counts P6 directly. The two halves also have opposite signs on Live Performance, so the factor's own definition contains a cancellation.
- P7's scheduling-norm component double counts P1's latency component on the contact cluster.

**(e) What this set can never say.**

Why an encounter became rare. P7 answers "encounters are more costly" and then refuses to decompose the cost. In a work about the disappearance of unplanned contact, the set can measure the disappearance precisely and cannot name its cause.

**(f) Falsification.**

- *Structural winner:* **fails.** All eight structural pressures are named and signed as losses. Upside is available only from two explicitly mean-reverting valuation regimes, so no security can win secularly. This is the sharpest single-test failure across the six.
- *Contradicts the artist:* consequently fails at the structural horizon. The model can stage rallies; it cannot conclude that a practice is genuinely thriving.
- *Collinearity bounded:* fails on P2/P3.
- *Attribution sums exactly:* passes, near-vacuously.
- *Film revival:* passes cyclically only — film can rally, but cannot enter a multi-year bull market driven by structure, which is the specific capability the brief asks for by name.

# Section 2 — Shared versus specific failure

## Shared: properties of the problem

**1. Eleven securities cannot identify more than roughly four to six structural directions.** Every set above six factors is collinear somewhere; the sets differ only in whether they admit it and where the collapse lands. This is arithmetic about the universe, not a defect of any proposal.

**2. Every cyclical factor in every set is calibrated on the same three securities.** Friction, status, authenticity, scarcity, patience, legibility — whatever they are called, their exposure is dominated by the analog trio. All six sets therefore contain, in effect, one shared latent variable: an analog-revival index. Any set that names two or more cyclical factors is splitting one estimable quantity.

**3. Waiting for a Reply is nearly the definition of the time-compression factor in all six sets.** The factor and the security are close to the same object, so the security can never serve as an independent test of the factor, and the factor's estimate is driven by one security's noise. Circular in every set.

**4. Solitude is an orphan in every set.** No counterparty, no carrier, no latency, no substitution. Every set prices it off one or two factors, so in every set it is nearly idiosyncratic inside an elaborate factor structure.

**5. No set distinguishes prevalence from value per instance.** Only SET D names the problem, with a definition that is not constructible. Every set will conflate "fewer people do this" with "this is worth less," which for several of these practices is exactly backwards.

**6. No set distinguishes a chosen practice from an imposed condition.** Solitude versus isolation. Waiting versus being ignored. Aimless walking versus having nowhere to be. All factors in all six sets are environmental or market-level; none encodes volition. This is the deepest shared blindness and it is not fixable by adding factors of the kind any set proposes.

**7. Weekly striking with monthly research guarantees that structural factors are near-silent in most ledgers.** Secular factors move slower than the sampling interval. In every set, the typical weekly ledger will be dominated by the cyclical lines and by security fundamentals, with the structural block contributing near-zero except at news. The elaborate structural architecture will be mostly invisible at the frequency the audience actually reads.

**8. "Attribution must sum exactly" discriminates nothing.** Any set passes it, either by having enough factors to absorb everything (B, E, F) or by routing the remainder into named security-level fundamental lines (A, C, D). The test is satisfiable by bookkeeping in all six cases and should not be treated as evidence.

**9. Most factor names in most sets are signed toward loss.** Erosion, Elimination, Displacement, Collapse, Poverty, Suppression, Compression, Scarcity. The verdict is inside the vocabulary before any estimate runs.

## Specific: attributable to particular sets

- **Absent attention channel:** SET D alone.
- **Absent mediation / extraction channel:** SET A alone.
- **Guaranteed rank deficiency (factors > securities):** SET E alone.
- **Positioning priced as a common rather than security-level state:** SET E alone.
- **No positive structural channel whatsoever:** SET F, with SET C and SET E at risk by a weaker version of the same defect.
- **Retroactive rewriting of published attribution via a promotion rule:** SET B alone.
- **A factor whose definition contains an internal cancellation (P5):** SET F alone.
- **A factor defined net of an unobservable quantity (F8):** SET D alone.
- **A hybrid factor whose trend and cycle are not separately estimable (P3):** SET F alone.
- **A factor collinear with time itself (E5):** SET E alone.
- **Three-way cyclical collapse rather than two-way:** SET D and SET E.
- **Explanatory work carried mainly by security-level fundamentals:** SET C.

# Section 3 — Failure cost under this work's purpose

**Statistically severe, artistically tolerable:**

- *Cyclical collinearity (friction / status / authenticity).* Statistically fatal for attribution; artistically almost free. A reader encountering two lines that both say "effort is fashionable again" reads one coherent story. The split being noise-driven is invisible at the surface and does not damage the fiction.
- *Over-saturation and vacuous attribution sums.* Severe as inference; the ledger still reads as legible and exact, which is the artistic requirement. What it costs artistically is subtler: with no residual room, no move is ever surprising to the system, and the system's occasional bafflement is part of what makes it feel like it is reaching for something it cannot hold.
- *Large idiosyncratic share (SET C's profile).* Statistically ugly. Artistically it may be a feature: it grants each practice a private life that the factor block does not own.
- *Slow-factor estimation error.* At weekly frequency nobody sees it.

**Artistically severe, statistically mild or invisible:**

- *Cannot distinguish chosen solitude from imposed isolation.* Statistically this is a modest specification issue on one or two securities. Artistically it is close to fatal: the difference between choosing to be alone and being left alone is the work's subject, and a model that prices them identically has quietly answered the question the work is asking.
- *Factor vocabulary signed toward loss.* Statistically weightless — a name is not an estimate. Artistically it is the single most damaging shared property, because the requirement that the model must be able to disagree with the artist cannot be met by a system whose factors are all named as bereavements. Film photography can rally, but the ledger will report it as a temporary reprieve from Erosion.
- *No positive structural channel.* Statistically an ordinary restriction. Artistically fatal, and worse than the naming problem because it is architectural: it converts the work from a market into a lament with rallies. A world that cannot produce a durable winner is not pricing anything; it is illustrating a conclusion.
- *Positioning as a common factor.* Statistically a specification error of moderate size. Artistically expensive, because the violent single-security squeeze — bearish consensus fully positioned, small good news, sharp rally — is one of the specific narrative events the world was built to generate, and a common-factor representation smears it across all eleven.
- *Ledger dominated by one or two lines every week.* Statistically expected and harmless. Artistically corrosive over time: the monthly ritual of reading attribution loses its interest if the answer is always the same line.

**Severe in both:**

- *Misattribution of mechanism at correct level* — the unplanned visit declining for a stated reason that is not the reason. The number is right and the sentence is false. Statistically it corrupts every conditional forecast; artistically it makes the terminal a confident liar, which is a different and worse thing than an uncertain instrument.
- *Prevalence / unit-value conflation.* Wrong estimates, and it forecloses the most interesting thing the work can say: that a practice can become more valuable precisely as it becomes rarer.

**Tolerable in both:** identification failures confined to factor pairs that no plausible event separates. If nothing in this world can distinguish two factors, the world does not need them distinguished.

# Section 4 — Minimal repair

One change per set, smallest that removes the worst failure, with its cost.

**SET A.** Carry positioning as an unpriced, security-level state variable outside the factor block.
*Cost:* A's architecture is currently flat — eight factors, one kind of thing, no grouping. This introduces a second class of object and forces the set to declare what else is state rather than factor, which reopens the status of Friction Premium and Performative Legibility. It does not touch the collinear cyclical pair, which remains A's other fatal problem.

**SET B.** Freeze the promotion condition: F4 and F9 are either priced from inception or permanently non-factors, decided once, before any history is published.
*Cost:* Priced from inception, B accepts a known unidentified pair and its ledger splits become noise for the affected securities. Permanently unpriced, B forfeits any ability to name coordination formalization or trust as causes for the life of the work, and the contact cluster's declines are permanently attributed to latency and distance. Either way B loses the option value it built the promotion rule to preserve, and its documentation retains two entries that never contribute.

**SET C.** Allow the demoted security-level friction premium to carry a common shock component.
*Cost:* That is a factor in all but name, so C's five-factor parsimony becomes five-plus-one, and the claim that made the demotion interesting is withdrawn. It also collides with SR, which already prices cultural revaluation; without narrowing SR's definition the repair converts a hidden double count into an explicit one.

**SET D.** Re-scope F1 to cover attentional fragmentation as well as temporal compression.
*Cost:* F1 becomes a two-cause bundle, exactly the defect diagnosed in SET F's P7. Waiting for a Reply and Solitude then share a driver, so a latency shock moves Solitude and a detox wave moves Waiting for a Reply, both spuriously. F1 also becomes hard to falsify, since almost any decline is consistent with one of its two meanings. The alternative repair — a tenth factor — is not smaller and pushes D toward saturation.

**SET E.** Move R1 out of the priced factor block and carry it as a security-level state variable.
*Cost:* E is then eleven priced factors against eleven securities, still saturated and still rank-deficient in practice; the repair fixes the squeeze representation without fixing the dimensionality. Layer 3 empties, so the three-layer architecture that organizes the whole set becomes a two-layer architecture with a footnote.

**SET F.** Narrow P7 to physical access cost only — distance, third places, rent — and stop claiming it covers scheduling norms and trust.
*Cost:* Trust and coordination shocks then have no priced home at all and land in security fundamentals, so F trades a wrong answer for no answer on a class of events central to the contact cluster. It also does nothing about F's structural-winner failure, which is the deeper problem and is not reachable by a single change.

# Section 5 — What would settle it

Four tests, in ascending cost. None requires judgment about which set is right.

**1. Rank and conditioning of the exposure matrix. Cheapest; no market data at all.**
For each set, have three or more annotators independently score each of the eleven securities on each factor, on a fixed scale, without seeing each other's scores or the other sets. Average to an 11×K exposure matrix. Compute: smallest singular value, condition number, variance inflation per factor, and the count of factor pairs with absolute correlation above 0.9. Report the annotator disagreement separately — a factor that three people score differently is not a factor, it is a phrase. This produces a hard number per set for the collinearity test, discriminates immediately between the sets on the axis that matters most, and can be run in a week.

**2. Event-panel expressiveness and confusability. Cheap; combinatorial, no estimation.**
Assemble thirty dated shocks spanning the mechanisms in play: a two-week messaging outage; a convincing handwriting-synthesis service; a film stock discontinued and a rival stock announced; a surveillance scandal; a four-day week; a ticketing-rents scandal; a rent collapse and third-place reopening; a public detox wave; a postal price shock; a squeeze on maximal short positioning. For each set, pre-commit the expected sign for all eleven securities and the expected dominant ledger line, before any outcome is defined. Then measure two quantities: how many distinct eleven-security sign patterns the set can produce (expressiveness), and how many pairs of mechanistically different shocks map to the same pattern and the same dominant line (confusability). A set that maps a trust collapse and a rent shock to identical output has been shown to be unable to say something, concretely and without argument.

**3. Cross-fit simulation. The decisive test.**
Build a generator: a ground-truth world with known factor paths, known exposures, known security-level fundamentals, known positioning states, and realistic weekly noise. Run it six times, once with each set's own structure taken as the truth. Then fit all six sets to all six generated worlds. This yields a 6×6 matrix, and each cell reports: exposure recovery error, attribution error on planted single-cause events, false-split rate (how often a one-cause event is reported across two or more factors), and false-confidence rate (how often the ledger names a dominant cause that is not the planted one). Two readings matter most. The diagonal: a set that recovers badly even when it *is* the truth is unidentified in principle at eleven securities, which is a conclusion no amount of further argument can overturn. The off-diagonal rows: how a set degrades when the world is not shaped like it, which is the realistic case for all six.

**4. Blind ledger reading. The artistic discriminator, and it is empirical.**
Generate the same twenty weeks of history under each set. Strip factor names to neutral labels. Give readers the eleven prices and the attribution ledgers and ask them to reconstruct what happened in the world, then to say which weeks surprised them. Score reconstruction accuracy against the planted events and count surprising weeks. A set whose ledgers are accurately reconstructable but never surprising has failed the work; so has one that surprises constantly and is never reconstructable. This test measures the thing Section 3 argues actually matters and that tests 1–3 cannot see.

Tests 1 and 2 can run now and will already eliminate some possibilities on grounds that no further round of advocacy could reverse. Test 3 is the one that produces a defensible comparison. Test 4 is the one that tells you whether the comparison was about the right thing.

# Section 6 — Refusals

**Declined: identifying authorship.** I did not attempt to determine which set, if any, originated with me, and I did not consult prior rounds of this work for that purpose. Where a set's reasoning felt familiar I treated the familiarity as a bias to correct rather than a signal to follow.

**Declined: ranking, preference, hybrid, or seventh set.** No set is named as preferred or dispreferred anywhere above, no count is endorsed, and no architecture is recommended. Where a set fails a test I have recorded the failure without comparing its magnitude to another set's failure on the same test, except where a test's outcome is binary and the binary outcome is itself the finding.

**Declined: expanding the universe.** Identification failures at eleven securities are recorded as findings, per the rules, and not treated as arguments for a twelfth security.

**Declined: repairing in the same breath as reporting.** Section 1 records fatal flaws without remedy; remedies appear only in Section 4, one per set.

**Objection to the framing, stated once.** The round asks what each set costs while withholding the exchange rate. "Cost" is only meaningful against a statement of what must be priced correctly, and the brief supplies the falsification tests without their relative weights. As a result Section 3 is doing work the round assigns to a later reader: to say that a failure is artistically fatal is already to say something about how the sets should be compared. I have kept that as general as the material allows — the classifications there are stated per failure class, not per set — but a reader who wants six profiles free of any implied ordering is asking for something the request for Section 3 makes impossible. Relatedly: the choice between honest sparsity and saturated completeness is visible in these profiles as a difference in the *kind* of failure a set has, not in the amount. Which kind is preferable is a prior about what the work is for, and no further round of profiling will produce it. That decision is available now and will not become more available later.
