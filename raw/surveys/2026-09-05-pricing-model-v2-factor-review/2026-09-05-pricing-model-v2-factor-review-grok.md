**1. Executive verdict**

The core principle—retain every factor that carries a genuinely distinct causal story, and merge only those whose separation is mostly semantic—is correct and stronger than any a-priori factor count. Collinearity in the real world is not, by itself, a reason to collapse mechanisms that can still diverge under plausible scenarios. The earlier seven-variable set failed because the variables were near-synonyms of the same modernization vector; the new candidate list partially escapes that trap but still contains several pairs that are more terminological than causal.

What remains conceptually wrong is residual technology-centrism and insufficient attention to the demand-side, demographic, and social-psychological channels that actually determine whether a practice retains or gains value. Time compression, human substitution, mediation, uncertainty compression, physicality displacement, and coordination compression are all supply-side or technological forces. Friction/inconvenience premium, scarcity revaluation, and social legibility are closer to the right territory, yet they are still under-specified and risk becoming residual catch-alls. Loneliness, attention scarcity, bodily effort, demographic structure, and cultural memory are either missing or only weakly present.

The candidate set is neither too many nor too few; it is simply misclassified. Several items belong as observable indicators or security-level fundamentals rather than independent latent pricing factors. Others can be kept once their definitions are sharpened so that independent movement becomes possible. The architecture should therefore be hierarchical: a larger set of observables feeding a smaller but still multi-dimensional set of latent factors, each of which can move on its own and produce different cross-sectional price effects. Complexity is welcome provided every retained factor buys a behavior that cannot be recovered from the others.

Subjectivity is not a defect; it is required. Cultural attachment, replacement resistance, and status readings must remain editorial or hybrid, tagged as such, and never presented as pure observables. The model can still generate secular decline in the aggregate LONGING index without hard-coded drift, because the bulk of exposures to technological compression factors are negative, while a minority of securities can carry positive exposures to scarcity, friction, or social-legibility factors that allow multi-year bull markets.

**2. Recommended factor architecture**

**F1. Temporal Compression**  
Definition: the systematic reduction in the interval between desire, initiation, and completion of an action or interaction.  
Causal mechanism: compresses the temporal space in which practices that require waiting, anticipation, or extended presence can generate value; simultaneously raises the opportunity cost of any residual delay.  
Behavior: secular with mild cyclical overlay (e.g., fatigue with speed).  
Observables: reply latency distributions, same-day delivery penetration, on-demand content share, scheduling density (observed + modeled).  
Correlations: high with coordination compression and mediation; separable because pure speed gains can occur without new intermediaries.  
Why separate: produces distinct effects on “waiting” securities versus pure physicality securities.

**F2. Human Act Substitution**  
Definition: the degree to which the human performance of an act can be replaced by non-human agents while preserving functional output.  
Causal mechanism: lowers the scarcity and authenticity premium attached to the human performance itself.  
Behavior: secular, accelerating in AI-capable domains.  
Observables: AI-generated text/image/audio share, automated scheduling uptake, synthetic media penetration (observed + modeled).  
Correlations: moderate with temporal compression and mediation; independent because substitution can advance without further time savings (e.g., slower but fully automated letter generation).  
Why separate: directly prices the human versus machine distinction that many LONGING securities embody.

**F3. Intermediation Density**  
Definition: the fraction of human-to-human or human-to-world contact that passes through an algorithmic or platform layer rather than occurring directly.  
Causal mechanism: inserts selection, ranking, and filtering that alter both the incidence and the qualitative character of contact.  
Behavior: secular, with regime shifts at major platform or regulatory changes.  
Observables: platform-mediated interaction share, algorithmic recommendation penetration, remote-versus-in-person ratios (observed).  
Correlations: high with temporal and coordination compression; separable because mediation can thicken without further speed gains.  
Why separate: explains why some practices survive in mediated form while others require direct presence.

**F4. Uncertainty Suppression**  
Definition: the reduction in residual chance, ambiguity, failure probability, and open-endedness engineered by systems of prediction, reservation, and pre-selection.  
Causal mechanism: erodes the option value and experiential value of practices whose payoff depends on unpredictability.  
Behavior: secular with possible cyclical backlash.  
Observables: reservation penetration, ETA accuracy, recommendation coverage, route-optimization share (observed + modeled).  
Correlations: moderate with temporal and coordination compression; independent because uncertainty can be suppressed without compressing calendar time (e.g., perfect long-horizon forecasts).  
Why separate: uniquely relevant to aimless walking, serendipitous discovery, and unplanned contact.

**F5. Physical Carrier Displacement**  
Definition: the substitution of dematerialized or remote equivalents for physical carriers and embodied co-presence.  
Causal mechanism: removes the scarcity, sensory, and ritual properties attached to the physical object or body.  
Behavior: secular, domain-specific plateaus.  
Observables: digital-versus-physical media ratios, remote-work / remote-meeting shares, streaming penetration (observed).  
Correlations: moderate with intermediation; separable because pure digitization of an existing physical channel can occur without new platforms.  
Why separate: isolates the material substrate that film, vinyl, letters, and live performance still depend on.

**F6. Coordination Formalization**  
Definition: the replacement of spontaneous or loosely synchronized contact by explicit scheduling, availability signaling, and optimized matching.  
Causal mechanism: raises the fixed cost of unplanned interaction and reduces its residual occurrence.  
Behavior: secular, reinforced by dual-income and urban time-pressure regimes.  
Observables: calendar-driven contact share, location-sharing intensity, reservation density for social encounters (observed + modeled).  
Correlations: high with temporal compression; independent because formalization can intensify even when absolute time available is stable.  
Why separate: cleanly targets unplanned calls and visits.

**F7. Friction Valuation**  
Definition: the market’s current willingness to pay a premium (or extract a discount) for practices that impose effort, patience, skill, or inconvenience.  
Causal mechanism: cyclical taste and status dynamics can invert the usual efficiency preference, producing multi-year rallies in high-friction practices.  
Behavior: cyclical / mean-reverting around a mild secular decline.  
Observables: analog-sales growth, craft-premium indices, survey measures of “desire for analog,” editorial status readings (hybrid).  
Correlations: low-to-moderate negative with technological factors; deliberately allowed to diverge.  
Why separate: the primary channel for analog and craft revivals inside a secular bear market.

**F8. Scarcity Revaluation**  
Definition: the change in unit value attached to remaining instances of a practice as its prevalence declines, net of any pure quantity effect.  
Causal mechanism: rarity itself becomes a valued attribute once a threshold is crossed.  
Behavior: regime-dependent, often non-linear.  
Observables: prevalence time series, secondary-market premia for scarce instances, editorial scarcity scores (hybrid).  
Correlations: moderate with physical displacement; independent because scarcity can rise from demand collapse without further technological displacement.  
Why separate: prevents double-counting prevalence decline with price response to rarity.

**F9. Social Legibility**  
Definition: the net social interpretation of a practice as authentic, intimate, elite, nostalgic, performative, or obsolete.  
Causal mechanism: status and identity motives shift demand independently of technological or scarcity fundamentals.  
Behavior: mixed—secular drifts plus fashion cycles.  
Observables: cultural discourse measures, status surveys, media framing indices, editorial judgment (primarily editorial + modeled).  
Correlations: low with pure technology factors; moderate with friction valuation.  
Why separate: the explicit narrative and status channel that real markets also price.

**3. Factors to merge, demote, or delete**

- Efficiency / Automation / Optimization / Digitization / Predictability / Immediacy (original set): demote entirely to observable indicators feeding F1–F6. They are manifestations, not independent latent factors.  
- Mediation (candidate C): retained and sharpened as F3; the original label was too broad.  
- Coordination Compression (F): retained as F6 with clearer definition.  
- Friction / Inconvenience Premium (G): retained as F7.  
- Scarcity Revaluation (H): retained as F8, with explicit net-of-prevalence definition.  
- Social Legibility / Status (I): retained as F9.  
- Pure “digitization” or “physicality” as standalone without the displacement language: fold into F5.  
- Loneliness or social fragmentation as a macro factor: demote to security-level fundamental or residual demand driver; too close to the definition of several securities themselves.  
- Attention scarcity: demote to an observable feeding uncertainty suppression and intermediation; not independently causal enough once those two are present.  
- Commodification: demote to a security-level fundamental (degree of marketization of the practice).  
- Reversibility, bodily effort, geographic distance, synchronization vs asynchrony: useful observables or exposure modifiers, not separate latent factors; they are already partially captured by F1, F5, F6.

**4. Missing factors**

- Demographic time pressure (distinct from technological temporal compression): dual-income households, aging populations, and urban density alter the residual time available for high-friction practices even when technology is held constant.  
- Attention allocation regime: not pure scarcity, but the systemic shift toward continuous partial attention that degrades practices requiring sustained presence.  
- Cultural memory / nostalgia intensity: a slow-moving factor that can amplify scarcity and friction valuation without being identical to either.  
- Trust in mediated systems: a regime variable that can reverse the usual substitution and intermediation effects when it collapses.  
These four are candidates for later addition once the core nine are stable; none is required for the first implementable version.

**6. Independence tests**

- Temporal Compression moves while Human Act Substitution stays flat: widespread 5G and same-day logistics cut reply and delivery times, but no new generative AI capability is released.  
- Human Act Substitution moves while Temporal Compression stays flat: a new LLM generates high-quality personal letters at human speed; latency distributions are unchanged.  
- Intermediation Density rises while Uncertainty Suppression is stable: a new social platform intermediates more contact but does not improve prediction or ranking quality.  
- Uncertainty Suppression rises while Coordination Formalization is stable: better long-horizon weather and traffic models reduce residual risk of outdoor unplanned activity without requiring more calendar bookings.  
- Physical Carrier Displacement advances while Friction Valuation is unchanged: streaming share rises further, yet survey measures of “willingness to pay for inconvenience” remain constant.  
- Friction Valuation rises while Scarcity Revaluation is stable: a fashion cycle elevates the status of high-effort practices even though prevalence of those practices has not declined further.  
- Scarcity Revaluation rises while Physical Carrier Displacement is stable: a sharp drop in demand for film cameras (fashion-driven) reduces the stock of remaining users without any new digital substitute appearing.  
- Social Legibility shifts while all technological factors are flat: a high-profile cultural essay re-frames solitude as elite rather than pathological; no technology changes.

**7. Event classification examples**

1. Major LLM releases letter-writing mode → F2 strongly negative; minor F1 and F3 spill-over.  
2. National same-day delivery mandate → F1 primary; F6 secondary.  
3. New dating app with perfect availability matching → F6 and F3; F4 secondary.  
4. City-wide reservation system for all parks and cafés → F4 and F6.  
5. Vinyl sales double for three consecutive years while streaming share is flat → F7 and F8; F5 neutral.  
6. Regulatory ban on algorithmic ranking of personal messages → F3 positive for direct practices.  
7. Large-scale remote-work permanentization → F5 and F3.  
8. Cultural campaign declaring “waiting” a form of resistance → F9 and F7.  
9. Sudden collapse in trust in AI-generated media → F2 and F9 reverse.  
10. Demographic report showing sharp rise in dual-income households with children → demographic time-pressure channel (future F10) and F6.  

Double-counting risk is highest for events that simultaneously advance F1, F3, and F6; attribution must allocate via a hierarchy or orthogonalized shocks.

**8. Attribution design**

Weekly return decomposition:

```text
Security XXX — WEEKLY RETURN                     +x.xx%

Factor contributions (orthogonalized)
  Temporal Compression                           a.aa%
  Human Act Substitution                         b.bb%
  Intermediation Density                         c.cc%
  Uncertainty Suppression                        d.dd%
  Physical Carrier Displacement                  e.ee%
  Coordination Formalization                     f.ff%
  Friction Valuation                             g.gg%
  Scarcity Revaluation                           h.hh%
  Social Legibility                              i.ii%

Interaction / second-order                       j.jj%
Positioning / short-covering                     k.kk%
Narrative residual                               l.ll%
Delayed pricing adjustment                       m.mm%
Idiosyncratic residual                           n.nn%
                                                -------
TOTAL                                            +x.xx%
```

Orthogonalization via sequential residualization or a constrained factor model keeps the sum exact. Interaction terms are estimated from historical co-movement or scenario analysis. Confidence intervals or explicit “low/medium/high confidence” tags accompany each line. Delayed pricing is a separate lag structure so that an event’s full effect can appear over several weeks without breaking the weekly ledger.

**9. Complexity risks**

Earned complexity ends and pseudo-quantitative theater begins when:

- factors are added whose only justification is thematic resonance rather than an independent causal story;  
- more than two layers of interaction terms are estimated from sparse data;  
- editorial scores are given four-decimal precision without documented scoring rules;  
- attribution residuals are routinely larger than the sum of named factors;  
- the same observable is allowed to load on three or more latent factors without clear allocation rules;  
- the model produces neat multi-year bull markets for every security the artist privately likes, regardless of factor realizations.

**10. Falsification tests**

1. Film-photography revival test: raise F7 and F8 sharply for three modeled years while holding F1–F6 constant; film photography must be capable of a multi-year positive cumulative return that exceeds the LONGING index. Failure if it cannot.  
2. Structural-winner test: identify at least one security whose average absolute exposure to the technological factors is low and whose F7/F8/F9 exposures can be positive; that security must be able to trend upward for five years while the equal-weighted index declines.  
3. Collinearity test: compute the correlation matrix of factor realizations over long simulated histories; any pair with |ρ| > 0.85 for more than 70 % of rolling windows must be re-examined for merger.  
4. Attribution integrity test: every weekly return must be exactly decomposable; the residual line may not systematically absorb more than 25 % of absolute return variance.  
5. Model-versus-artist disagreement test: construct a scenario in which all technological factors advance and friction/scarcity/legibility factors do not; the model must be able to price several “beloved” securities into multi-year bear markets even if the artist’s personal valuation remains high.

**11. Proposed v2 specification**

```text
OBSERVABLE INDICATORS (continuously or weekly updated)
  reply latency, delivery speed, AI-content share,
  platform-mediation ratio, reservation penetration,
  recommendation coverage, digital-vs-physical ratios,
  calendar density, analog-sales growth, prevalence series,
  cultural-discourse indices, survey friction-willingness,
  demographic time-pressure metrics
        ↓
LATENT FACTORS (monthly or weekly state vector)
  F1 Temporal Compression
  F2 Human Act Substitution
  F3 Intermediation Density
  F4 Uncertainty Suppression
  F5 Physical Carrier Displacement
  F6 Coordination Formalization
  F7 Friction Valuation
  F8 Scarcity Revaluation
  F9 Social Legibility
        ↓
SECURITY-SPECIFIC EXPOSURES (β matrix, revised quarterly)
  + security-level fundamentals
    (Cultural Attachment, Replacement Resistance,
     Expected Persistence, Baseline Scarcity)
        ↓
FUNDAMENTAL / NARRATIVE / POSITIONING LAYERS
  Research Fair Value = f(exposures × factor states + fundamentals)
  Narrative residual and status adjustment (editorial)
  Positioning / short-interest proxy (market microstructure)
  Analyst Target = function of expected factor paths
        ↓
WEEKLY PRICE
  Market Price updates via
  orthogonalized factor shocks
  + positioning adjustment
  + residual
  with full attribution ledger published each close
```

Implementation notes: factor states are updated from a mix of observed time series, simple diffusion models, and explicit editorial interventions (tagged). Exposures are sparse and signed as in the matrix above. No hard-coded drift term exists; aggregate decline emerges from the dominance of negative technological exposures. Multi-year rallies emerge endogenously when F7–F9 realizations turn sufficiently positive for high-friction, scarce, or high-legibility securities. All subjective inputs carry provenance tags (Observed / Modeled / Editorial) and versioned scoring rules.
