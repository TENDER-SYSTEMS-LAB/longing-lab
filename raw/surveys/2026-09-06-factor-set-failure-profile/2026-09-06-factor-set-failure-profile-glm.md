Thought Process
Section 1 — Failure profile, one per set, six times

Convention used throughout: a factor "rising" means the named process intensifying; practices negatively exposed to that process fall. "Priced too high / too low" describes the set's error relative to what the world described in Section 3 of the brief would warrant. Mispricing directions are conditional on natural calibrations; identification, double-counting, and inexpressibility findings are not calibration-dependent. No set is ranked or preferred anywhere below.

SET A — 8 factors

(a) Mispricing.

Unplanned Phone Call — priced too high. A's only time factor is Temporal Compression, and a phone call is a low-latency, synchronous practice; the set's time-axis therefore marks UPC a compression-winner. The axis conflates speed with spontaneity: the call is fast but unscheduled, and it is the unscheduled half the world is killing. That cause — the norm that contact must now be pre-arranged — has no factor. Attention Scarcity books only the interruption cost. The set is structurally bullish on a security whose substrate is dissolving, and books what decline occurs under Attention Scarcity, over-weighting distraction and missing the norm.
Solitude — priced too high in taste-factor rallies. Physical Grounding reads solitude as spatial (offline, in place); Performative Legibility reads it as off-grid prestige. The actual erosion — perpetual availability and ambient mediation — has no factor. Rallies in the set's supportive factors lift a security whose substrate keeps thinning: cyclical amplitude over-booked, secular decline under-booked.
Handwritten Letter — priced too high on Physical Grounding. Paper is carried as a spatial carrier in demand. HL's binding constraint is the shrinking stock of people to write to, which must ride Social Atomization — a factor shared with UV, LP, and SD — so addressee decay is smeared across the cluster and under-booked on the letter itself.

(b) Unattributable events.

A postal strike, or a collapse of mail reliability. HL and WR fall together on a supply shock. A's only latency line is Temporal Compression, defined as cultural intolerance for delay; the ledger books a logistics failure as a change in collective temperament — attributed exactly, to the wrong kind of cause.
A platform retreat (dating-app exodus, a "touch grass" wave). SD, UV, UPC, AW rise on disintermediation. A's nearest lines are reversals in Social Atomization and Variance Elimination: the ledger narrates "community density returned and chance returned" when people merely left apps. One cause, two misdescribing lines.
A recession that trades income for time. No economic line exists; the move lands in Friction Premium — a budget constraint narrated as an aesthetic shift.

(c) Identification failure at eleven securities.

Performative Legibility / Friction Premium / Physical Grounding collapse on {Film Photography, Physical Media, Handmade Object, Live Performance}: three factors, four securities, similar positive loadings. Any three-way split is imposed, not estimated.
Variance Elimination / Temporal Compression separate only through WR (pure latency) and AW (pure navigational chance); on {UPC, UV, SD} they move together — partial collapse.
Attention Scarcity carries a same-signed loading across effectively all eleven: indistinguishable from a market-level factor. Its separate existence cannot be established from these prices.

(d) Double counting.
The death of third places: one cause enters as Social Atomization (proximity, density, trust) and again as Physical Grounding (demand for spatial carriers) — Unplanned Visit and Live Performance are each hit twice. (Secondarily: the calendar enters as both Temporal Compression and Variance Elimination on the unplanned cluster.)

(e) What this set can never say.
It contains no economic or labor-market line of any kind. It can never say that a practice declined because hours got worse — that the letter died of the wage rather than of the phone. Every decline must be narrated as substitution, culture, or taste.

(f) Falsification.

Collinearity: fails. The analog triad plus a near-uniform attention factor; the published split among the triad is arbitrary week to week although the sum holds.
Film revival: passes on price, at risk on attribution. Friction Premium and Performative Legibility can both turn; the revival's cause is not separable among three collinear lines, so the bull market's story is not reproducible.
Structural winner: capable — LP can run a structural bull on Physical Grounding.
Attribution exactness: passes arithmetically; the same event can carry a different line split the following week under equivalent data.
Contradicting the artist: capable — nothing prevents Synthetic Substitution from crushing a beloved practice.

(d) Double counting.
Nostalgia: one cause enters as Cultural Memory (security-level mechanism) and again as F12 Status Coding (priced factor). A broadcast analog wave is booked twice on Film Photography and Physical Media — once as memory, once as signal.

(e) What this set can never say.
The block architecture hard-codes which causes may be secular and which cyclical. B can never say that a valuation regime ratcheted — that the authenticity cycle stepped up and never reverted. A permanent revaluation of the human must be expressed as an unending sequence of cyclical swings while Block I grinds; the set's own taxonomy forbids the sentence "this was not a cycle."

(f) Falsification.

Collinearity: fails / at grave risk — ten priced factors, the F11–F12 collapse, F7–F8 hanging on one security, and a promotion rule that can end at twelve on eleven.
Attribution exactness: passes arithmetically by construction (ten lines, two contributing zero); fails in meaning whenever the true cause is F4- or F9-shaped — exactness is achieved by misbooking.
Film revival: capable — over-capable. F11, F12, the Scarcity transform, Cultural Memory, Replacement Resistance, and Infrastructure thresholds can each carry the bull; the revival is explainable under every engine and therefore under none specifically. The risk is unfalsifiability, not incapacity.
Structural winner: capable (LP via F7/F10/F11).
Contradicting the artist: capable in principle; at risk in practice — twelve factors plus four mechanisms can rationalize any trajectory, including the artist's prior, without ever being cornered into disagreement.

(f) Falsification.

Film revival: at risk. The price path is achievable through SR plus security-level friction, but the market's story of the revival is split between one revaluation line and five idiosyncratic lines: the bull is producible, its market-level cause is not.
Structural winner: at risk. SR is the only positive factor; every winner is by construction a status story. A winner driven by structural demand is unpriceable as structural.

Section 2 — Shared versus specific failure

Shared failures — no choice of set fixes these:

The patience/status pair is unidentified everywhere. Every set has a cyclical-taste line (A6, B F11, C's demoted premium, D F7, E E1, F P9) and a legibility/status line (A7, B F12, C SR, D F9, E E3, F P10), and both load the analog cluster {FP, PM, HO, HL, ±LP} with the same sign. Whether the count is five or twelve, the split between loving the slowness and loving the look of slowness is imposed, never measured. In C the pair survives across a factor and a demoted fundamental instead of two factors — same failure, different layer.
Market-level causes have no home. A cause that hits all eleven same-signed is either fused into a near-uniform factor (indistinguishable from the market level: A5, B F6, C AS, E E6, F P2/P3) or smeared into eleven coincident idiosyncratic lines (D, which lacks such a factor). Both forms fail the same event — "everything human declined together this quarter" — one by non-identification, one by non-attribution.
The substrate is homeless. No set prices supply-side viability at the factor level. B's per-security Infrastructure mechanism handles single-practice thresholds, but a shared materials shock still smears. All six describe infrastructure as either culture or coincidence; "the practice died because its stuff ended" is available to none.
Trust has two faces, and every set fuses them. Stranger-trust (UV, SD) and institutional-trust (HL, in the opposite direction) respond differently to the same event. A fuses both inside Atomization; B freezes F9; C and D omit trust entirely; E fuses both in E7; F buries both in P7. The discriminating event — a distrust wave that lifts paper and suppresses unplanned contact — is unattributable in all six, differently in each.
The ledger is parallel; nothing constitutes anything. All six architectures are additive: lines that sum, none that act on another. "Substitution starved the labs, and the labs killed the practice" must appear as small substitution lines plus one large idiosyncratic event. Causal chains and constitutive causation are inexpressible in every set.
Exactness is arithmetic, not truth. All six satisfy "sums with no unexplained remainder" by construction; in all six, that exactness is achieved by forcing real causes into available lines or frozen zeros. The test as written discriminates nothing; the discriminating quantity is split-stability, which each set fails in its own places.
The revival is producible and unwitnessed. Every set can generate film's bull market through cyclical channels (A: 6+7; B: F11/F12 plus mechanisms; C: SR plus security-level premium; D: F7/F8/F9; E: E1/E2/E3; F: P9/P10). No set can produce a stable cause for it — over-determined in the rich sets, split across layers in the poor ones. The bull passes the price test in all six and the attribution test in none.
A pattern across the six: the sets that organize (B, E, F) pay in frozen dynamics — taxonomy forbids regime change (B's secular/cyclical walls, E's layer speeds, F's mean reversion); the sets that reduce (A, C, D) pay in blind spots — omitted economics, counterparty, capacity. The failure moves; it does not disappear.

Specific failures — where the comparison is actually available:

Positioning: carried by B (price-only state) and E (priced factor); absent in A, C, D, F. The violent crowded-short rally is unattributable in four of six.
Cohort: carried by E only, and weakly signed. Five of six must describe a generational influx as fashion.
Economic causes: carried by E (E4) and F (P3); absent in A, B, C, D — the wage cannot move anything in four of six.
Enclosure/rent: carried explicitly by B (F10) and F (P5); visible nowhere else. Capture-as-love is invisible in four of six.
Practitioner capacity: carried in some form by A, B, E, F; inexpressible in C and D.
Set-unique structural items: A's three-line analog collapse; B's frozen-factor execution rule and its promotion discontinuity; C's demoted market cycle; D's commodity-scarcity logic at factor level and its winners-only-by-dying structure; E's arithmetic over-count (12 > 11); F's fused authenticity/status at the top of a bull and its internally six-caused P7.
Section 3 — Failure cost under this work's purpose

Principle first. Statistical severity is about being wrong about the world — identification, bias, forecast loss. Artistic severity is about three other things: (i) presenting an adequacy the machine lacks (covert precision), (ii) committing a category error about what these practices are, (iii) violating the work's own constitutive requirements — two-way disagreement and a genuine possible revival. A failure that leaves the machine visibly wrong is on-subject: the piece is an extremely calculated system attempting to price what resists calculation. A failure that leaves the machine invisibly adequate is off-subject. Statistical severity neither implies nor precludes artistic severity, in either direction.

Class by class:

Identification collapse and split jitter (shared #1, plus per-set collapses). Statistically severe. Artistically tolerable to central — conditional on disclosure. A terminal that visibly cannot decide whether film is patience or status is stating something true about the practice. The fatal variant is silent normalization: an arbitrary split presented as a measurement falsifies the rigor the surface stakes itself on. Same failure, two artistic outcomes, decided entirely by whether the jitter is legible in the published ledger.
Market-level homelessness (shared #2). Statistically moderate. Artistically moderate: a market-wide human decline smeared into eleven private coincidences quietly denies the commonality that is the subject's emotional core — longing is communal. Not fatal; a real cost.
Substrate homelessness (shared #3). Statistically moderate. Artistically on-theme: the machine's blindness to matter — that things end because their stuff ends — is precisely the calculative blind spot the piece exhibits. Its real artistic cost is realized jointly with class 5.
Fused trust (shared #4). Statistically severe — sign errors on a discriminating event. Artistically tolerable with a caveat: describing fear as preference is what markets actually do; the voice is market-plausible. The misdescription must remain legible as misdescription for the poignancy to survive.
Additive parallelism (shared #5). Statistically negligible — a fund would not object. Artistically the clearest inversion in this whole section: the lab-death spiral — the most poignant true story available in this universe — cannot be told by any set. The terminal's world is one of pure coincidence. Statistical nil; artistic real; non-fatal.
Exact-sum semantics (shared #6). Statistically vacuous — all pass. Artistically decisive in both directions: exactness is the surface's claim to rigor, and jitter beneath an exact sum is either the piece's central irony (if the reader can see it) or its central lie (if not). Disclosure is the whole of the artistic question; the statistics are silent on it.
The unwitnessed revival (shared #7). Statistically moderate. Artistically high: the work requires a genuine bull. A bull whose cause the ledger cannot state is real in price and unaccounted in meaning — the piece's central event happens off-book, in every set.
One-signed structural layers (E, F; D's winner-by-dying; C's SR-only optimism). Statistically mild — a sign constraint, a cap. Artistically the most severe specific class in this round: the model must be able to disagree with the artist in both directions, and a layer that can only kill, or can only revive as labeled fashion, is an elegy machine, not a market. The sharpest inversion: least statistical severity, highest artistic severity.
Over-parameterization (B, E). Statistically severe. Artistically fatal by stealth: a model that can explain anything never has to disagree with anyone and never visibly fails; the tension between calculation and the resistant subject collapses into competence, and the longing leaves the machine. Both axes severe — one for reasons of identification, the other for reasons of honesty.
Blind spots (A: economics; C: counterparty; D: capacity; five of six: cohort). Statistically moderate — omitted variables absorbed by wrong lines. Artistically mostly on-theme: each blindness (the wage, the rent-taker, exhaustion, the young) is a true sentence about calculative systems. One dangerous exception: enclosure-blindness is systematically directional. Without a rent line, every price rise reads as love; the machine becomes an accidental propagandist for capture. That specific blindness is artistically more dangerous than its statistical weight.
Frozen taxonomies (B, E, F). Statistically mild — constraints on dynamics. Artistically moderate: the barred sentences — "this was not a cycle," "the culture lurched," "this appetite is durable" — are among the most important things this terminal could ever say, and the sets that organize give up the ability to change their minds about their own categories.
Positioning absence (A, C, D, F). Statistically mild. Artistically a lost scene rather than a false one: the crowded-despair-then-spike is among the most human behaviors in markets and thematically perfect for longing. Nothing untrue is shown; a true thing never occurs. Mild on both axes, non-zero artistically — statistical nil does not imply artistic nil.
Composite factors (A's Atomization, C's FE, E's E7, F's P7, B's F10 sign ambiguity). Statistically severe where components diverge. Artistically: confident vagueness — a line named for six things describes nothing while appearing to describe everything. Covert adequacy in miniature: more dangerous than a simple omission, less than full over-parameterization.
B's frozen factors. Statistically the set's worst feature — the attribution promise broken in meaning. Artistically, its most truthful: a set that declares its own thin support and refuses to bluff a line is doing the one thing this artwork most needs its machine to do — admit a limit. The artistic cost is the smear (systemic events as coincidence), not the freeze. The statistical and artistic verdicts on the same feature point in opposite directions.
Section 4 — Minimal repair

SET A. Worst failure: the scheduling norm — the principal cause across the unplanned cluster — has no line, while the analog cluster carries three collinear lines. Single smallest change: merge Performative Legibility into Friction Premium, leaving one cyclical-taste line, and stand up a coordination-formalization factor in the freed slot. Count stays at eight. Costs: the merged line can no longer distinguish loving slowness from being seen to love slowness — one of A's sharpest distinctions disappears; Solitude's off-grid prestige and HL's signal value now ride the general taste line, changing the shape of their mispricing rather than removing it; and the set's widest blind spot is untouched — there is still no economic line, so the letter still cannot die of the wage.

SET B. Worst failure: F4 and F9 frozen at zero while the world moves through them; systemic events narrated as coincident idiosyncratic news. Single smallest change: run F4 as a priced factor with a fixed prior loading and a pre-committed slow drift path, rather than a hard zero; leave F9 frozen. Costs: the ledger gains a line whose content is assumption rather than measurement — exactness is now partly achieved by fiat, a different breach of the same promise; the promotion condition loses its function, removing the set's built-in device for admitting thin support; and the F3/F4 collinearity arrives on day one instead of at promotion — unattributed smearing is traded for identified-but-partially-arbitrary splitting.

SET C. Worst failure: the demoted friction premium makes the market's principal cycle invisible as a market event. Single smallest change: re-price the friction premium as a sixth factor. Costs: C ceases to be the five-factor reduction — the identity of the set is itself the cost; the re-priced patience factor and SR become the same inseparable pair every other set carries, spending part of C's cross-sectional slack; and the demotion's original wager — that patience is a property of practices rather than of the market — is conceded rather than tested.

SET D. Worst failure: the valuation triad on the analog cluster. Single smallest change: demote F8 Scarcity Revaluation to a security-level mechanism — rarity is per-practice and prevalence is security-level, so the factor was carrying security-specific content cross-sectionally. Costs: the market-wide "everything remaining is precious" moment loses its factor line and becomes coincident security lines — the shared-cause smear arrives; the "net of quantity effect" definition must now be enforced where prevalence is endogenous, so the security line can quietly absorb the quantity effect it was defined to exclude; and, most consequentially, the repair removes D's only structural positive channel — the structural layer becomes fully one-signed, and winners-by-dying become cyclical too. The collinearity is repaired by worsening the winner problem.

SET E. Worst failure: twelve factors on eleven securities; at least one unidentified by arithmetic. Single smallest change: demote R1 Positioning from a priced factor to a price-only security-level state. Costs: the reflexive layer empties of priced content — the market's self-regard leaves the factor ledger, and market-wide positioning events become many coincident lines; R1's endogenous loadings were the circular identification problem, which the demotion relocates rather than solves; and the set remains at exactly eleven priced factors on eleven securities — the cross-section is saturated with no residual dimension, so idiosyncratic cross-sectional dispersion must be forced into factor lines. The repair reaches the boundary and stops there.

SET F. Worst failure: P9/P10, a same-signed cyclical pair fused on the analog cluster, and behind them a structural layer with no positive channel. Single smallest change: merge P9 and P10 into one valuation regime and re-point the freed slot to a structural demand-side line (a durable co-presence/physicality premium) — one substitution, count unchanged at ten. Costs: the merge permanently fuses patience and status, so the divergence at the top of a bull market — status rising as authenticity falls — becomes invisible by construction rather than by weakness, and the bull's ending is uniformly arbitrary; the new structural line breaks the set's rhetoric, since "structural pressures" were adversarial by design and a positive line leaves the layer's language incoherent (a pressure for what?); and the new factor is identifiable only to the exact extent that patience and status truly were one — the repair's validity is hostage to the collapse it repairs.

Section 5 — What would settle it

A runnable discrimination, not an appeal to judgment:

A ground-truth generator. Build a synthetic world whose causal engine contains, by construction, all candidate processes at controllable strengths — the shared displacement, condition, and valuation processes, plus supply events, cohort dynamics, enclosure events, both faces of trust, and positioning with crowding. Because the engine is code, the true cause of every weekly move is known exactly.

A pre-registered injection battery of roughly thirty single-cause events, each with a known sign pattern across the eleven: postal strike; film-chemicals restart; a young cohort's film influx; Etsy-ification of handmade; a stranger-trust scandal; a platform-distrust scandal; a platform exodus; an urban rent shock; a recession trading income for time; a scheduling-norm wave in each direction; a patience turn; a status turn; a materials shortage; a lab-closure threshold; a short squeeze on a hated security; and so on.

Run all six sets as ledger-estimators on the generated tape and score, per set: (i) attribution hit-rate — does the largest line in the weekly ledger match the true injected cause; (ii) sign accuracy on known-shock weeks; (iii) split-stability — bootstrap the exposure matrix, recompute attribution splits for fixed price moves, and report the fraction of attribution variance carried by collinear pairs; (iv) revival capability — force film bulls under four distinct engines (taste, cohort, supply, scarcity) and test whether each set produces both the price path and a stable attribution; (v) contradiction frequency — plant an artist-style prior in calibration and measure how often run outcomes deviate from it in each direction.

The discrimination is the failure fingerprint, not a total score. The predictions are falsifiable in advance: A fails the economic and mediation injections; B fails the norm and trust injections while frozen and shows the promotion as a discontinuity in ledger composition; C fails the market-cycle and supply injections and cannot attribute a taste-led film bull at market level; D fails the trust and capacity injections and cannot host a growing structural winner; E fails everything routed through its collinear pairs and shows the largest split-jitter; F fails the cohort, supply, and patience-versus-status injections and fades a cohort-led revival. If two sets share a fingerprint, run the two world variants that locate the residual dispute: generate one tape with few true causes and one with many, and compare out-of-sample forecast scores. The sparse tape and the dense tape settle the disagreement between reduction and identification, because that disagreement was never about the sets — it was about the world.

A static pre-test is also available before any simulation: the condition number of each set's 11×k exposure matrix.
