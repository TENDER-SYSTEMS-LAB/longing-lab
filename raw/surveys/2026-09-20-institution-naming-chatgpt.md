# LONGING — Naming and Collision Screen

**Screen date: 20 September 2026**

**The strongest names to take into a proper clearance search are *首都経済新聞 / Shuto Keizai Shimbun* and *KOPCI*. None of the US surname pairs below earns an unqualified recommendation.** Several otherwise convincing newspaper names are already historical publications or fictional institutions.

This is an open-web knockout screen, not a professional trademark clearance search. I have not queried live trademark registers or domain-registration records. “No exact entity verified” means precisely that—not “unused.” Registration is also not the only source of rights; a comprehensive clearance search includes similar names and unregistered commercial use. ([미국 특허청][1])

I use three dispositions:

| Disposition              | Meaning                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Advance to clearance** | No sufficiently established collision found in this screen. Not approved for public use.                                                             |
| **Hold**                 | A related name, financial-sector association, or unresolved match needs investigation.                                                               |
| **Reject**               | An exact use or sufficiently strong association makes further investment unattractive under your brief. This is not necessarily a legal prohibition. |

**Trademark-class shorthand used throughout:** the **publisher profile** covers Classes **16**—printed matter; **41**—publishing; and **9**—downloadable publications or data. Computing and publishing financial indices also calls for checking **35**—business/statistical information—and **36**—financial information and analysis. The **finance profile** primarily covers **36**, with **35, 9, 16 and 41** according to the services and publications offered; **42** becomes relevant for hosted software. These are search scopes, not claims that any candidate is registered in those classes. The actual public-facing work may require publishing, software, or entertainment clearance even though its fictional institution provides financial services. ([NCLpub][2])

For every entry below, **“trademark unknown” applies separately to Japan, Korea, the United States and the EU**.

# 1 — The Japanese publisher

Eight candidates, including names rejected during screening. Plausibility comments are naming judgments, not invented institutional histories.

### 1. 首都経済新聞 — Advance to clearance

**Romanisation:** *Shuto Keizai Shimbun*
**Literal gloss:** “Capital Economic Newspaper.”
**Form:** A geographical designation followed by an economic remit and the ordinary newspaper suffix; credible without requiring an explanatory backstory.

**Entity collision:** I did not verify an exact publication, company, index, product, or established fictional institution under either the Japanese title or this romanisation. Historical occupation remains unknown. Search results containing the individual words were not treated as exact masthead matches.

**Trademark:** Unknown in all four territories; publisher profile. Its descriptive components do not eliminate the need to search similar titles.

**Domains:** `shuto-keizai.jp` and `shutokeizai.com`—registration status unknown.

**Confidence and basis:** Medium on linguistic plausibility; **low on non-occupation**, based on limited exact-name web searches. This is the first Japanese candidate I would spend clearance effort on.

### 2. 京浜経済新聞 — Hold

**Romanisation:** *Keihin Keizai Shimbun*
**Literal gloss:** “Tokyo–Yokohama Economic Newspaper.”
**Form:** A regional economic newspaper whose masthead need not change when its index acquires national importance.

**Entity collision:** No exact newspaper was verified. **Keihin**, however, is an established Japanese automotive-company and brand name with international industrial history. The geographical spelling 京浜 and the automotive branding are not identical in every script, but the romanised and spoken overlap is real. This particularly weakens a shortened **Keihin Average**. ([위키백과][3])

**Trademark:** Exact title unknown in all four territories; publisher profile. Rights in the automotive name are plausible and require investigation, although an automotive mark does not automatically block publishing.

**Domains:** `keihin-keizai.jp`, `keihinkeizai.com`—unknown.

**Confidence and basis:** High that the related automotive name exists, using secondary company-history evidence; low that the complete newspaper title is unoccupied.

### 3. 夕刊京浜 — Hold

**Romanisation:** *Yūkan Keihin*
**Literal gloss:** “Tokyo–Yokohama Evening [Paper].”
**Form:** The edition designation *yūkan*, “evening edition,” followed by a geographical masthead.

**Entity collision:** No exact masthead was verified. Results describing an evening or regional edition are not sufficient evidence of a newspaper actually named 夕刊京浜. The **Keihin** component nevertheless inherits the automotive-name issue above. ([위키백과][3])

**Trademark:** Exact title unknown in all four territories; publisher profile. Both the complete title and shortened publisher attribution need searching.

**Domains:** `yukan-keihin.jp`, `yukankeihin.com`—unknown.

**Confidence and basis:** Medium on form; low on historical non-occupation. Exact-title searches plus a confirmed related-name concern—not clearance.

### 4. 京葉経済新聞 — Hold

**Romanisation:** *Keiyō Keizai Shimbun*
**Literal gloss:** “Tokyo–Chiba Economic Newspaper.”
**Form:** Another geographically ordinary regional economic masthead.

**Entity collision:** No exact publication was verified. **Keiyo Bank** is a real Japanese financial institution. That is a more relevant association for an index publisher than an unrelated consumer brand, particularly if the index is shortened to **Keiyo Average**. It is not evidence that the complete newspaper title belongs to the bank. ([京葉銀行][4])

**Trademark:** Exact title unknown in all four territories; publisher profile. Protection of the banking name in Japan is plausible; Class 36 similarity deserves particular attention. Territorial registrations were not checked.

**Domains:** `keiyo-keizai.jp`, `keiyokeizai.com`—unknown.

**Confidence and basis:** High on the bank’s existence, from its official website; low on the full title’s non-occupation.

### 5. 関東経済新聞 — Reject

**Romanisation:** *Kantō Keizai Shimbun*
**Literal gloss:** “Kantō Economic Newspaper”; *Kantō* literally means “east of the barrier.”
**Form:** Entirely plausible—which is precisely why it is already occupied historically.

**Entity collision:** An actual historical newspaper used this title. Japan Search records an issue dated **23 August 1950**, and Saitama’s library holdings identify the newspaper in Urawa. This is a historical regional-publication collision, not an assertion that a national paper currently operates under the name. ([ジャパンサーチ][5])

**Trademark:** Current registrations and surviving rights unknown in all four territories; publisher profile. Historical publication alone does not establish an active trademark.

**Domains:** `kanto-keizai.jp`, `kantokeizai.com`—unknown.

**Confidence and basis:** **High on exact historical occupation**, based on institutional archival records.

### 6. 東都経済新聞 — Reject

**Form:** An economical metropolitan masthead with an older institutional register.

**Domains:** `toyokeizai.net` is demonstrably occupied by the publisher; other extensions were not checked. ([東洋経済新報社][8])

**Confidence and basis:** **High on occupation and rejection**, from the publisher’s own site.

**Korean:** 한국행위총합지수
**Romanisation:** *Hanguk Haengwi Chonghap Jisu*
**Literal gloss:** “Korea Practice Aggregate Index.”

**Why this form:** Administrative and impersonal, though **총합** is less natural as a flagship-index term than **종합**.

**Collision:** No exact full index was verified, but **Kopai** is the name of a restaurant at ATELIER Playa Mujeres in Mexico. That is a real hospitality-brand use, not a financial-index assignment. Under your stricter naming rule, it is enough to keep KOPAI out. ([Atelier de Hoteles][15])

**Domains:** `kopai.com`, `kopai.kr`—unknown.
**Disposition / confidence:** **Reject; high confidence on the restaurant use, low on registration status.**

**Korean:** 한국행위가치지수
**Romanisation:** *Hanguk Haengwi Gachi Jisu*
**Literal gloss:** “Korea Practice Value Index.”

**Why this form:** Suitable only if the series measures value rather than participation, prevalence, or an undefined mixture.

**Collision:** **KOPVI** appears as a women’s sweatshirt model name in a Czech retail listing. Separately, **Kopvi.com** is offered for sale by a domain marketplace. The first is a product-name collision; the second is evidence against assuming the obvious domain is freely registrable. Neither establishes a financial index. ([Sasoo][16])

**Domains:** `kopvi.com`—**likely already held**, based on the sale listing, not registration records; `kopvi.kr`—unknown.
**Disposition / confidence:** **Reject; high on the product listing, medium on the domain inference.**
