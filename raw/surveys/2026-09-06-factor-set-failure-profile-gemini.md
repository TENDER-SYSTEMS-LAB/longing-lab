Here is the failure profile review for the six factor sets, executed according to your rules of engagement.

Section 1 — Failure profile, one per set, six times
SET A — 8 factors

(a) Mispricing: This set misprices Solitude and Waiting for a Reply. By framing factors as "Social Atomization" (decline of community), the model will likely price Solitude as structurally benefiting from atomization, failing to distinguish between imposed isolation (loneliness) and chosen, regenerative aloneness. Waiting for a Reply is heavily penalized by Temporal Compression, but lacks a cyclical demand factor to fuel a revival, as Friction Premium implies active effort rather than passive patience.
(b) Unattributable events: A sudden mass migration away from a specific algorithm (a "digital detox" movement). The set lacks a direct "Mediation" or "Intermediation" factor. The move would have to split clumsily between Friction Premium and Variance Elimination, failing to attribute the specific rejection of third-party routing.
(c) Identification failure at eleven securities: Variance Elimination and Temporal Compression will collapse into a single vector across these eleven securities. For Serendipitous Discovery, Aimless Walking, and Unplanned Visit, prediction systems eliminate variance precisely by compressing time.
(d) Double counting: Synthetic Substitution and Variance Elimination will double count the penalty on Serendipitous Discovery when an algorithm is introduced to curate content or matches.
(e) What this set can never say: It structurally cannot express the difference between physical distance and social distance.
(f) Falsification: Fails the "structural winner must emerge" test. All five structural factors (1-5) are heavily negative for almost all 11 securities. The baseline model state is a terminal downtrend for everything.

SET B — 12 factors

(a) Mispricing: Misprices Unplanned Visit during the initial run. Because F4 (Coordination Formalization) is unpriced initially, the immediate normative barrier to dropping in on someone is ignored. It misprices Serendipitous Discovery because F3 (Predictive Pre-selection) and F1 (Latency Collapse) are active, but the psychological slack required to enjoy discovery (F6 Attentional Slack) is treated as a separate regime, desynchronizing the cause from the condition.
(b) Unattributable events: A renaissance in Handmade Objects driven specifically by a rejection of AI-generated art. F2 (Functional Substitution) captures the structural threat, but the set relies on F11 (Friction Premium) or F12 (Status Coding) for the rally, leaving the specific anti-automation sentiment unattributable as a distinct demand driver.
(c) Identification failure at eleven securities: F1 Latency Collapse and F5 Dematerialization. Across Film Photography, Handwritten Letter, and Physical Media, these two factors move in historical lockstep.
(d) Double counting: F2 Functional Substitution and F5 Dematerialization double count the penalty on Physical Media. The media is substituted by a non-human process (streaming) and dematerialized simultaneously.
(e) What this set can never say: Because of the execution rule holding F4 and F9 unpriced until promoted, it cannot say that coordination norms and trust are the initial drivers of a security's collapse.
(f) Falsification: Exact attribution will fail. With 10 initially priced factors, 11 securities, and unpriced state variables acting as threshold triggers, the attribution ledger will either require heavy regularization (destroying exactness) or produce wild, unstable week-to-week swings in factor weights.

SET C — 5 factors

(a) Mispricing: Misprices Unplanned Visit and Aimless Walking. This set contains no spatial, geographic, or physical proximity factor. A change in urban design or physical community density will force the model to misprice these as changes in MD (Material Decoupling) or SR (Social Revaluation).
(b) Unattributable events: A postal strike or supply chain breakdown that physically halts letters and film. With Friction Premium demoted to a fundamental, and Friction Elimination functioning only as a systemic design factor, a sudden involuntary physical friction event lands entirely in the residual.
(c) Identification failure at eleven securities: Agency Displacement (AD) and Friction Elimination (FE) collapse for Serendipitous Discovery. Algorithms eliminate friction exactly by displacing human agency.
(d) Double counting: Material Decoupling (MD) and Friction Elimination (FE) double count the structural penalty on Handwritten Letters during a transition to digital messaging.
(e) What this set can never say: It structurally cannot express spatial or geographic constraints on human behavior.
(f) Falsification: Fails "at least one structural winner must emerge." FE, AD, and MD are strictly negative across the entire 11-security universe. The universe will inevitably trend to zero unless SR (Social Revaluation) runs permanently hot.

SET D — 9 factors

(a) Mispricing: Misprices Solitude. As defined, none of the 9 factors inherently support Solitude as a positive practice; it registers only as the absence of Intermediation Density (F3) and Coordination Formalization (F6). Misprices Film Photography by splitting its physical nature (F5) from its social signal (F9), making it highly sensitive to collinearity.
(b) Unattributable events: A collapse in generalized public trust causing people to retreat indoors. The set has no trust, safety, or community factor. The drop in Unplanned Visits and Aimless Walking would be misattributed to Coordination Formalization (F6) or Temporal Compression (F1).
(c) Identification failure at eleven securities: Intermediation Density (F3) and Uncertainty Suppression (F4) collapse for Serendipitous Discovery and Unplanned Phone Call. The intermediary layer is the uncertainty suppressor.
(d) Double counting: Friction Valuation (F7) and Scarcity Revaluation (F8) will double count the cyclical premium of Handwritten Letters in a bull market, as both factors bid up the asset for highly correlated reasons.
(e) What this set can never say: It cannot express changes in the baseline attentional or time capacity of the population, as it lacks a discretionary time or attention factor.
(f) Falsification: Collinearity must stay bounded is at severe risk. F7 (Friction Valuation) and F9 (Social Legibility) will demonstrate runaway collinearity during any sustained rally for analog practices.

SET E — 12 factors

(a) Mispricing: Misprices Film Photography. It is punished heavily and redundantly by S1, S2, and S4 in the structural layer, making it artificially suppressed. Misprices Waiting for a Reply; it is penalized by S1 (Time Compression) but also by E4 (Economic Time Scarcity), muddying the difference between system speed and human exhaustion.
(b) Unattributable events: The introduction of a new physical community space (e.g., a massive expansion of public parks). The set lacks a spatial/geography factor; E2 (Physicality Premium) measures valuation, not structural physical layout.
(c) Identification failure at eleven securities: E1 (Friction Premium), E2 (Physicality Premium), and E3 (Social Legibility) will collapse together for Handmade Object and Physical Media during any cyclical uptrend.

(e) What this set can never say: It cannot cleanly separate the fundamental cause of a cyclical rally from market mechanics, because R1 (Positioning) is treated as a factor alongside experiential variables, mixing state with cause.
(f) Falsification: Exact attribution is mathematically guaranteed to fail. 12 active factors applied to 11 securities results in an underdetermined system. The design matrix is singular. Attribution will require arbitrary penalties.

SET F — 10 factors

(a) Mispricing: Misprices Unplanned Phone Call. It is penalized by P7 (Encounter Erosion), but the definition of P7 relies heavily on physical constraints (distance, third places, rent), which do not apply to telephony. Misprices Solitude by lacking a factor that actively rewards it, relying instead on the absence of P2 (Attention Capture).
(b) Unattributable events: A cultural movement embracing "waiting" and delayed gratification. P9 (Patience Regime) captures the discount rate, but because Friction is not its own factor, the model cannot distinguish between a willingness to wait and a willingness to exert effort.
(c) Identification failure at eleven securities: P1 (Time Compression) and P3 (Discretionary Time Poverty) will collapse for Handwritten Letter and Waiting for a Reply.
(d) Double counting: P5 (Mediation & Extraction) and P6 (Uncertainty Compression) double count the structural penalty on Aimless Walking and Serendipitous Discovery.
(e) What this set can never say: It cannot distinguish between physical friction (sweat, distance) and temporal friction (waiting, delay), as both are rolled into P9 (Patience Regime).
(f) Falsification: Collinearity risk is high between P9 (Patience Regime) and P10 (Authenticity & Status Cycle), as periods of high patience for analog practices will almost perfectly correlate with their perceived authenticity.

Section 2 — Shared versus specific failure

Shared Failures (Properties of the problem):

The Algorithmic Collinearity Trap: Every set with more than 5 factors fails to separate Mediation (the presence of a platform) from Uncertainty/Variance Suppression (what the platform does). Across 11 securities, these vectors are practically identical.

The "Solitude" and "Waiting" Problem: Every set struggles to price passive states. Because factors are designed around active pressures (automation, effort, status, space), passive practices are usually defined only by the absence of a negative factor, leaving them without a structural demand driver.

Overparameterization: Any set with 9 or more factors (B, D, E, F) structurally threatens exact attribution in an 11-security universe.

Specific Failures (Properties of the sets):

Set C's blindness to physical space and geography.

Set B's execution rule (unpriced factors), which creates temporal instability in the ledger.

Set A's conflation of isolation and solitude via the "Social Atomization" framing.

Set E's mathematical impossibility (12 factors for 11 securities without a stated regularization mechanism).

Section 3 — Failure cost under this work's purpose

Statistical Failures (Collinearity, Overparameterization, Identification Collapse)
Severity: Artificially Tolerable.
LONGING is an artwork. If two factors collapse (e.g., Mediation and Uncertainty), the math can be forced to resolve via ridge regression, and the resulting instability can be displayed on the terminal as a feature—a machine struggling to parse human nuance. Exact attribution can be "faked" via matrix penalties so long as the lines sum to the total.

Artistic Failures (Mispricing Solitude, Double Counting, Cannot Say Spatial/Normative truths)
Severity: Fatal.
If the model prices Solitude as the byproduct of a lonely, atomized society, it contradicts the soul of the practice. If Set C cannot "see" public spaces being closed down because it lacks a spatial factor, the terminal fails to reflect the physical reality of the viewer. If the baseline state of a model (like Set A and C) forces all 11 securities into a permanent secular downtrend without structural winners, the market becomes a predictable graveyard rather than a dynamic ecosystem.

Section 4 — Minimal repair

SET A: Rename and redefine "Social Atomization" to "Default Public Exposure," shifting it from a measure of loneliness to a measure of baseline ambient human contact. Cost: Loses the ability to track the decay of deep trust.

SET B: Remove the unpriced execution rule; F4 and F9 price immediately. Cost: Increases collinearity and adds noise to the weekly ledger.

SET C: Add a 6th factor: "Spatial Enclosure" (privatization or loss of physical commons). Cost: Breaks the strict 5-factor minimalist aesthetic and requires re-weighting Material Decoupling.

SET D: Merge "Friction Valuation" (F7) and "Scarcity Revaluation" (F8) into a single "Inconvenience Premium." Cost: Loses the ability to price things that are scarce but easy to do.

SET E: Demote "R1 Positioning / Crowding" from a priced Layer 3 factor to an unpriced, security-level state variable. Cost: Breaks the symmetrical three-layer architecture of the set.

SET F: Merge "P5 Mediation" and "P6 Uncertainty Compression" into "Algorithmic Routing." Cost: Loses the distinction between data surveillance (extraction) and prediction (uncertainty).

Section 5 — What would settle it

Generate a synthetic historical dataset spanning 2010–2025 (the smartphone/social media adoption era). Assign weekly, directional "ground truth" price moves to the 11 securities based on real-world events (e.g., the launch of algorithmic feeds, COVID-19 lockdowns, the 2023 film camera resurgence).

Feed this identical price history into all six sets and force them to generate their weekly exact attribution ledgers using standard ordinary least squares or ridge regression.

Evaluate the results on two metrics:

Condition Number: Measure the condition number of each set's design matrix. Sets that exceed a critical threshold (indicating severe collinearity and mathematical instability) are disqualified.

Residual Narrative Test: For the models that survive, isolate the weeks of known idiosyncratic cultural shifts (e.g., the sudden spike in Film Photography). Check which set's attribution ledger most accurately assigns the rally to a cultural/cyclical factor versus bleeding the move into the mathematical residual.

The set with the lowest condition number that still correctly categorizes the idiosyncratic events settles the debate.

Section 6 — Refusals

I declined to recommend a preferred set, a preferred factor count, or an architecture. I declined to suggest expanding the universe to solve the identification failures. I declined to rename factors to fix them, except as explicitly requested in Section 4.

Meta-critique regarding framing:
The framing of this round demands "exact attribution" (a ledger of lines that sum perfectly to the total return with no residual) while maintaining a fixed universe of exactly 11 securities. Mathematically, any factor set approaching or exceeding the number of securities (Sets B, D, E, F) cannot produce exact, non-arbitrary attribution without generating infinite solutions or requiring heavy regularization (which produces a residual). The premise that a 12-factor set can provide "exact attribution" on an 11-asset universe without a mathematical contradiction is structurally false.
