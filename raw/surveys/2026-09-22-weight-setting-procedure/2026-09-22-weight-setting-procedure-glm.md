What follows is a procedure, not a valuation. No security's weights are set here; every number is marked *illustrative*. Nothing below touches exposure measurement, price formation, or the five fixed points, which are treated as given.

---

## Section 1 — The unit of classification

The unit is the **step**, and steps are not chosen freely: they are induced by the security's own definition. A definition is a finite sentence; at listing, its distinct claims about what a performance must be are marked as **registered requirements**, each with its **anchor phrase** quoted verbatim. The rules:

1. Each registered requirement induces exactly one step; each step must cite the phrase that anchors it. No step without an anchor; no anchor without a step.
2. A step is performed by the practitioner, or commissioned as one undivided act — where the definition has the practitioner commission an act (a delivery route), *the commission is the step*; the delegate's internal method is not a step. (**The commission rule.**)
3. A step is counted once — not per occasion, per draft, per revision, per minute.
4. Steps inherit the order in which a canonical performance satisfies the requirements (not the order of clauses in the definition).
5. The requirements fix the boundary and the stance: steps belonging to the counterpart side of the practice (the recipient's reading of a letter) are not steps of the security.

Two conventions the definition alone does not settle are fixed here once, before any listing, so they are never re-argued per security:

- **Completion attaches upstream.** The decision that a step is finished belongs to the step it completes, not the one it authorizes. The judgment "this letter is done, it goes" lives in composing, not in releasing. This prevents the same judgment being counted twice.
- The commission rule (rule 2) as stated, with its alternative priced in Section 3.

**LETTER** — *a personally composed, physically handwritten message intended for a particular person and released to that person through a delivery route* — has four registered requirements and therefore four steps:

| # | Step | Anchor phrase | What the step is | Agent |
|---|------|---------------|------------------|-------|
| 1 | Select | "intended for a particular person" | fixing the recipient and the occasion; the decision that a letter will go to them | practitioner |
| 2 | Compose | "personally composed" | fixing the text — matter, wording, order, what is withheld — including the decision that it is finished | practitioner |
| 3 | Form | "physically handwritten" | forming the marks of the fixed text by the writer's own hand | practitioner |
| 4 | Release | "released to that person through a delivery route" | addressing and entrusting the formed letter to a route | practitioner; carriage commissioned |

The carriage itself is not a fifth step, by the commission rule. The requirement-marking is fixed at listing and changes only under revision condition (a). Everything downstream inherits it.

## Section 2 — The sorting rule

The candidate — *whether the step handed over contained more than one live option* — is **adopted with three refinements**. As stated it covers only handed-over steps, leaves "live" undefined, and says nothing about who holds the options. Refined:

**The test**, applied to one step:

1. Fix the **upstream record**: the definition's registered requirements plus the outputs of every step upstream of the one under test. Nothing else is in the record.
2. Ask whether a **competent delegate** — one holding whatever skill the definition demands of the step, and nothing beyond the upstream record — would face more than one admissible performance of the step.
3. Two admissible performances are **distinct options** if they differ in the practice's **product** — what the practice exists to produce or convey — or in any manner of performance the definition explicitly names. Differences in unnamed manner (route, paper, pitch, hour, pen) are not options.
4. **Judgment** if more than one option; **execution** if one.

Two **defaults** make the test decidable: the definition's silence defeats a claimed option (unregistered differences never count); product-content always registers. The gloss this yields: a step is judgment exactly when its upstream record specifies the matter but not the form the product takes; execution exactly when the upstream outputs fix everything that remains.

The test is **era-independent and performer-independent by construction**: it asks about a hypothetical delegate against the definition, not about what any technology in any year can do. Restorability is invisible to it on purpose — restoration is an arrival-side property measured as exposure, not a label.

Applied to the record's hard cases:

| Case | Step under test | Options the delegate faces | Becomes | Where the rest of the effect goes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Voicing a sentence the person chose; reading a text aloud in a learned voice | voice the fixed sentence | none registered — the sentence is fixed upstream; manner of voicing is not product-content and is unnamed | **Execution** | the restoring side is arrival-side; exposure |
| Sending *how are you* through a system translation | render the greeting in the recipient's language | many renderings; each is a different product the recipient receives | **Judgment** — the deliberation is handed over whole | substitution of judgment; exposure |
| A system's interpretation read before the original, changing the person's decision | — | — | **Nothing to classify**: no step of the practitioner's practice is handed over | flagged to exposure as *influence-without-handover* |

Case three shows where the line really sits: had the relevant definition registered *interpretation* as a requirement, handing it to a system would have been a judgment handover. Whether it does is a definitional fact, not a classification judgment.

**Partly-both units.** Definition-anchored steps are pure by construction: a step boundary falls wherever the anchor phrase does, and the test returns one label. The residual case is a step whose product and manner are **inseparable** — no decision point at which the upstream output fixes what remains (improvised forms are the paradigm). The classification panel then declares the step **mixed**; the default split is one-half to each share; the user ratifies; the declaration is recorded as a named ambiguity and enters the band (Section 3). Any split other than one-half requires a definitional amendment showing where the definition separates the act — a mixed label chosen for arithmetic convenience rather than inseparability is invalid.

## Section 3 — The counting rule

**Base rule: equal count.** With *n* steps, *e* execution and *j* judgment, the pair is *e/n* and *j/n*; a mixed step contributes one-half to each share. The pair sums to one by construction. The number of steps is small (LETTER: four), so the arithmetic is trivial and checkable by hand.

| Rule | Assumes | Distorts | Era-independent? | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Equal count over registered steps | registered requirements weigh alike | blind to a step's depth; the pair swings by 1/*n* per relabeled step | yes | **adopted** |
| Weighted by time | duration measures share | tool- and skill-relative; the same practice takes different time in different eras | no | rejected — inadmissible under fixed point 3 |
| Weighted by consequence | the practice's point is rankable across steps | imports a hidden valuation; invites outcome-tuning | only if frozen at listing, which re-imports discretion | rejected for the base pair |
| Weighted by failure-frequency | failure records reveal anatomy | causes of failure are era-bound | no | rejected for weights; admissible as an exposure diagnostic |

What equal counting assumes: that each registered requirement is a like unit of the practice. What it distorts: magnitude. Composing outweighs releasing in any honest reckoning of depth; the pair cannot see this. The remedy is not a weighting but a definition that registers more — if the institution believes depth matters, it must say so in the definition, and the pair moves mechanically.

**Sensitivity, to show what the conventions buy** (*illustrative throughout*): LETTER as decomposed, *n* = 4 → 0.5 execution / 0.5 judgment. If carriage were admitted as a fifth step → 0.6 / 0.4. If the completion decision were detached into its own step → 0.4 / 0.6. None of these is "wrong"; that is precisely why the conventions are fixed once, before listing, and never varied per security.

**Tolerance.** Given the same definition text with the same requirement-marking and the same classification record, two independent appliers must reproduce the step-set **exactly**, the labels **exactly** after the Section 4 protocol, and the pair **exactly**. The only admitted uncertainty is printed, not tacit: if *k* steps are named-ambiguous, the pair is published as a band of half-width *k*/(2*n*) around the value computed with ambiguous steps at one-half (*illustrative*: *n* = 4, *k* = 1 → ±0.125). A tolerance stated on the pair alone is meaningless at small *n*; the binding tolerances are at the step level.

**What this procedure cannot decide.** (i) Unregistered judgment is invisible by design — if the definition is silent on a difference, the procedure returns execution and cannot be argued out of it; the fallback is a definitional amendment. (ii) Step depth is not represented; fallback as above. (iii) Inseparable steps get a band, not a truth; the one-half default is a convention, flagged as such. (iv) Whether a definition *should* register more (is the hand a bearer of the person, or only an instrument?) is a definitional act for the user, not a finding of the classification.

## Section 4 — Who sets it, and how disagreement is resolved

What is put before the user is a **dossier and one computed pair**, not a menu:

1. The definition text with requirements marked and anchor phrases quoted.
2. The induced step-set: per step, its anchor, its upstream record, the options enumerated (or "none registered"), the label, any named ambiguity.
3. The pair, with the arithmetic shown, and the band if ambiguities exist.
4. A sensitivity table over the alternative step-set readings that were rejected during reconciliation, so the user can see what a different definition would have bought.
5. An attestation that no exposure, price, or fit data entered the derivation.

**The user's levers are definitional only.** They ratify the dossier, amend the definition (triggering recomputation, and condition (a) if post-listing), fix a convention where the procedure offers defaults, or resolve a named ambiguity by choosing among pre-stated defaults. They may not edit a label or a number directly. If they want a different pair, they change what the security *is*, publicly; the pair follows. This is the structural guarantee behind fixed point 5.

**Disagreement protocol.** Two classifiers who disagree on one step each file an option-test record: the options they claim, each tied to its anchor phrase, or a certification that none exist. Three outcomes only: (i) the dispute is about whether a difference is registered — the definition text decides, and where the text is silent, the silence default applies; (ii) the dispute is about the meaning of the text itself — this becomes a clarification proposal to the user, with the silence default holding in the meantime; (iii) both readings are admissible under the text — the step is declared named-ambiguous, the default split applies, and the band is published. **No votes on labels.** A majority shortcut manufactures confidence the record cannot support. At listing, three independent derivations (one per market desk, blind to each other) are run and reconciled under this protocol before anything is published.

## Section 5 — Cross-market consistency

The procedure yields **one pair per security**, not one per market. The canonical derivation is made once from the single definition; all three listings carry it.

Condition (b) is operationalized as **derivation divergence**: the same definition text, independently applied, producing different step-sets or labels. Detection runs two ways:

- **At listing:** the three blind per-market derivations described above. Any difference in the induced pairs that survives the Section 4 protocol is a condition (b) event *at birth* and is resolved before listing — by amending the definition until all three markets read it the same way, or by splitting the security into separately defined listings.
- **After listing:** a standing challenge right — any of the three desks may file a re-derivation with a step-level trace showing a different label under the shared definition — plus a scheduled blind re-derivation (proposed: every five years, and on any definitional event; the cadence is a dial for the user).

What fires (b): a sustained, step-traceable pair difference under the same definition version. What does not: band differences that overlap; and era effects of any kind — exposure readings differing across markets never fire (b), because the weights are era-invariant by construction. A sustained (b) finding is evidence that the definition under-specifies the practice across the markets' understandings of it; the remedy is textual (harmonize) or a split, never per-market weight-setting.

## Section 6 — The revision procedure

**A revision record contains:**

- Security identifier; definition version; the current pair and a reference to its derivation record.
- The condition invoked, (a) or (b); the initiator; the date.
- For (a): a **requirement-level diff** of the definition (anchor phrases added, removed, altered); the newly induced step-set; the recomputed pair; a **firewall attestation** signed by the revision team that no exposure measurement, price, or fit-of-generated-record data was consulted in preparing it.
- For (b): the conflicting derivation records; the surviving locus of disagreement (step and anchor phrase); the reconciliation minutes; the resolution chosen — harmonized definition or split listing — and the resulting pair(s).
- Named ambiguities carried over or resolved; the effective date; publication links.

**Initiators:** any market desk, any member of the classification panel, or the user. **Ratification:** the user alone; the panel executes the recomputation.

**Evidence standards.** For (a): the user-approved instrument of amendment is itself the evidence, and the pair change must be mechanically traceable to the diff — *a revision whose pair changes without any requirement changing is invalid.* Era drift is expressly not condition (a): that letters are now often typed does not touch a definition that says "physically handwritten"; drift belongs to exposure. For (b): two independent derivation records under the same definition version whose divergence survives the Section 4 protocol, with the disagreement localized to a step.

**Published readings.** Readings already published under the old pair **stand as published and are never overwritten**. Each reading remains linked to the pair in force at its publication; a restated series under the new pair may be computed mechanically and published alongside, clearly labeled as restated. The rationale: a past reading is a record of the security as it was then defined; silent retroactive substitution would misdescribe the record and would be indistinguishable from tuning.

## Section 7 — Failure modes

Ranked hardest to detect afterwards first. With small *n* (three to six steps), one relabeled step moves the pair by roughly 0.17–0.33 (*illustrative*), so each mode below is amplified; the only structural damping is a definition that registers more, which is a definitional act.

| Rank | Failure | Why it is hard or easy to detect afterwards |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Under-registration at listing** — the definition fails to name differences that carry judgment | leaves no trace; the derivation runs clean and nothing in the record is wrong; surfaces only by a fresh stricter reading or a cross-market divergence, both contingent |
| 2 | **Requirement bundling or splitting** — a defensible marking of requirements whose *n* was never argued | each marking looks anchored; no internal wrongness to flag; detectable only by a re-anchoring exercise with fresh classifiers |
| 3 | **Option-scope drift** — the enumeration quietly cites what delegates in the era can do rather than what the definition registers, era-tuning the weights while looking diligent | partially detectable by auditing the option-test records for citations that appeal to the record instead of the definition; requires a standing audit habit |
| 4 | **Ritual consensus on (b)** — per-market derivations become copied rather than blind; the condition never fires | the published record shows unanimity either way; distinguishable only by injecting genuinely blind re-derivations |
| 5 | **False unanimity in reconciliation** — a majority label stands without refuting the minority's enumeration | visible only if minority records are preserved; nothing otherwise |
| 6 | **Mixed-label gaming** — "mixed" declared to move a pair | each declaration must argue inseparability against the definition; checkable, moderate |
| 7 | **Revision-path tuning** — condition (a) used to move pairs after seeing fit | the most visible mode — amendments are public and dated and attestations exist — but visible only after the pair has carried the error |

Modes 1 and 2 are the dangerous ones because they are indistinguishable from correct work inside the record; the entire defense is front-loading diligence at listing and keeping the derivation dossiers comparable across time.

**Note on training cut-off.** My reliable knowledge of real practices runs to early 2025. Nothing in this procedure depends on later practice — it is built to be era-independent — and the technologies the record names as reference cases (speech decoders for people who have lost speech, consumer machine translation, voices learned from samples) were established before that date.

## Objections

The procedure makes the definition text carry everything: the steps, the labels, and therefore the pair. It thus measures the rigor of definition-drafting rather than the practice directly, and an under-drafted definition bakes its looseness into every era's reading with no internal signal — the failure ranked first above. I design for fixed points 3 and 5 as given because this carriage is what makes era-invariance enforceable and outcome-tuning structurally impossible: the only lever that moves a pair is a public, dated, requirement-level amendment, and the pair always follows mechanically. The accepted cost is that judgment the definition fails to register is invisible to the weights, and the burden of noticing that silence falls on the listing stage and the cross-market derivations, not on any later check.
