## A. Definitions

- **CARE** — "their body, their days or their voice": "days" and "voice" are not parseable by an audience, and nursing a body, managing days and speaking for someone have different step shapes, so the split moves with whichever is meant.
- **ETRS** — "a key, a secret, a child, one's name, a time": "one's name" and "a time" are opaque, and a key has handover and return steps a secret does not, so the spread of objects moves the split.
- **GTHR** — "civic acts enter here": no boundary is given (voting? protest? petition?); what is admitted changes which steps exist and therefore the split.
- **VIST** — "a visit, a meal arranged together, walking them home": three acts of different shape (co-hosting a meal is co-decision-heavy; walking home has no "choose what to do together"), so the split depends on which exemplar is priced.

## B. Steps

- **STRG** — there is no content step; "say it" must carry the choice of opening words, which elsewhere (MEND 3, TELL 3, WRIT 2–3) is its own judgment step.
- **INTR 2** — "choose whom or what to recommend" either repeats step 1's finding of the pair or silently means choosing the intermediary; as worded the same seeing is counted twice.
- **RECH 1** — an unprompted thought is an event, not a choice; the live option (act on it or let it pass) is absorbed into step 2 and never counted as its own judgment.
- **MEND 5** — "offer or accept forgiveness" bundles the repairer's act and the harmed party's act into one step; elsewhere each security stays on one side of the bond.
- **MAKE** — under the definition's second branch (having its maker make it), the choice of maker is a judgment step the list omits; it is exactly the hand-over-the-doing-keep-the-choice case the model is about.
- **GTHR 3** — "take part" is far coarser than any step in the other securities; it spans the whole life of membership and holds the judgment choices that PLAY carves out as its step 4.
- **ETRS 5** — "keep it or give it back" is the trustee's act; the only security whose steps span both sides, though its kin (TELL/LSTN, ASK/HELP) were split into legs.

## C. Classes

- **CHSE 1** — classed execution while the same unprompted mental event is judgment in RECH 1 and CARE 1; if acting-on-it-or-letting-it-pass is a live option there, it is one here — the three should take one class.
- **DEC 2** — "gather the options": which options are gathered changes the choice-set the other decider faces, a live option differing in meaning; under the stated text this is judgment unless the step is reworded as assembling what the framing already yields.
- **ASK 4** — accepting contains a live meaning-laden variant: take the help as a standing gift or cancel the debt on the spot; these differ in the practice's product, so judgment, not execution.
- **GTHR 3** — as drawn it cannot be execution: it holds the meaning-laden choices of participation (see B), and a mixed step is forbidden.
- **VIST 3** — travel is route and mode, i.e. manner, hence execution, unless the one visited reads the mode as message, which the step does not claim; but for the walking-home exemplar the going is the whole act, so the class depends on which exemplar is meant (see A).
- **ETRS 5** — keep-or-return holds live variants (return at once, keep longer, keep) that differ in the practice's meaning — judgment; or drop the step from the entruster's side entirely (see B).
