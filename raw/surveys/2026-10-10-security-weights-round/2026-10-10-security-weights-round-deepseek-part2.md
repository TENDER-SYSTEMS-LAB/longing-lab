## A. Definitions

- INTR: “or asking someone who knows both to do it” leaves open a delegated act whose steps and split may differ from doing the introduction oneself.
- WRIT: “one’s own words are words one stands behind” is tautological and does not clarify what standing behind adds.
- RECH: “or on their day” is ambiguous and leaves open a choice (birthday, difficult day, ordinary day) that could move the split.
- MAKE: “or having its maker make it for them” introduces an unstated choice of maker that could be judgment or execution.
- CARE: “their voice” is unclear and an audience may not understand whether it means speech, representation, or advocacy.
- GTHR: “civic acts enter here” is vague and does not define what counts as a civic act.

## B. Steps

- INTR: Steps 1 and 2 overlap: seeing that two would suit each other and choosing whom or what to recommend may be one judgment.
- RECH: Steps 1 and 2 overlap: thinking of the person unprompted and choosing the moment may be one impulse.
- TELL: Step 5 “Weigh the view received” is outside the definition, which says only “asking for their view.”
- LSTN: Step 5 “Keep it to oneself” is not in the definition “listening and answering”; it is a separate confidentiality act.
- MEND: Step 5 “Offer or accept forgiveness” combines two different acts that could have different splits.
- VGIL: Steps 2 and 4 both describe presence (“Be there”, “Stay through”) and may be one step cut in two.
- VIST: Steps 5 and 6 overlap: spending the time and seeing them back may be one continuous act.
- GTHR: Steps 2, 3, and 4 are all attendance (“Go and join”, “Take part”, “Keep coming”) cut finer than similar securities.
- ASK: Steps 4 and 5 “Accept the help” and “Keep the debt in mind” could be one post-ask step.
- ETRS: Step 5 “Keep it or give it back” combines two opposite choices in one step, unlike other securities’ single-class steps.

## C. Classes

- WRIT step 4 “Put it into a medium”: medium is execution by default; author marks judgment without showing the recipient reads the medium as part of the message.
- RECH step 3 “Choose the means”: same default-execution issue; author marks judgment.
- LSTN step 5 “Keep it to oneself”: whether to keep confidence has live options differing in meaning; should be judgment, not execution.
- VIST step 3 “Travel”: route/travel is execution unless the effort is read as part of the message; author marks judgment.
- ASK step 5 “Keep the debt in mind”: remembering or repaying the debt is a live choice differing in meaning; should be judgment, not execution.
- ETRS step 5 “Keep it or give it back”: the step states two live options that differ in meaning; should be judgment, not execution.
