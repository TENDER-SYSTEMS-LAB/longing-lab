## A. Definitions

* **WRIT**: Defines "one's own words" circularly, leaving open whether AI-drafted text subsequently approved constitutes personal words, which directly shifts step 3 judgment.
* **VIST**: Includes "travel" (step 3) as judgment without specifying if the route or medium carries communicative meaning, which an audience will find confusing.
* **TRET**: Leaves open whether "leaving the turn open" is an intentional action or passive neglect, altering whether step 3 is active judgment or default state.
* **ETRS**: Bundles distinct asset types (a secret vs. a physical key vs. a child) whose governance rules differ so drastically that audience comprehension of a single security risk profile fails.

## B. Steps

* **STRG**: Missing step for deciding the topic or framing the initial query before addressing.
* **INTR**: Steps 1 ("See that two would suit") and 2 ("Choose whom to recommend") redundancy-count the same match-making decision twice.
* **WRIT**: Steps 2 ("Decide what to say") and 3 ("Choose the words") cut the semantic decision process significantly finer than other securities.
* **LSTN**: Missing step for evaluating the truth or validity of what is heard prior to formulating a response.
* **MEND**: Step 4 ("Say it") is execution, but step 3 ("Choose what to say") already covers the choice; step 4 doubles the utterance compared to `TELL`.
* **VIST**: Step 3 ("Travel") is categorized as judgment while physical conveyance in `GTHR` (step 2) and `CARE` is coarse execution.
* **ASK**: Step 4 ("Accept the help") and Step 5 ("Keep the debt in mind") split the post-request receipt far more finely than `HELP`.
* **HELP**: Missing step for evaluating whether the requested favor is appropriate or safe to grant before deciding to help.

## C. Classes

* **VIST Step 3 ("Travel")**: Assigned as judgment, but standard physical transit contains no communicative choice read by the other person, so under Rule 3 it must be execution.
* **CHSE Step 5 ("Give it")**: Assigned as judgment, but the physical act of handing over a purchased item contains no live option unless the manner itself communicates a message; otherwise it is execution.
* **MAKE Step 4 ("Give it")**: Assigned as judgment, but delivering the created object is an execution step following the design and fabrication choices.
* **LSTN Step 1 ("Make time")**: Assigned as execution, but clearing schedule space among competing personal priorities involves choosing between live options with distinct structural meanings, making it judgment.
* **ASK Step 1 ("Decide not to do it alone or buy it")**: Assigned as judgment, but evaluating personal capacity vs. external acquisition is an internal operational choice; under the exception, if uncommunicated to the recipient, it lacks a message read by the other.
