## Section 1 — The Japanese publisher

**1. 全国経済新聞 — Zenkoku Keizai Shimbun — "Nationwide Economic Newspaper."**
Form follows the real field-daily pattern of scope word + subject + 新聞 (全国農業新聞 [R]).
- *Exists:* exact name not recalled as ever used [?]. Near-neighbour: the みんなの経済新聞 network has occupied the "◯◯経済新聞" pattern at city scale (藤沢経済新聞, 川崎経済新聞 etc.) [R]. A Korean local paper 전국경제신문 may exist [?, low confidence].
- *Trademark:* 全国 + 経済 are common morphemes; composite marks in JP classes 16/36/41 unverified [?].
- *Domain:* zenkoku-keizai.co.jp plausibly open [?]. **Confidence: medium-low. Recommended primary.**

**2. 東西経済新聞 — Tōzai Keizai Shimbun — "East-and-West Economic Newspaper."**
Compass-scope masthead in the 東洋 lineage without the imperial freight [C].
- *Exists:* not recalled [?]. *Trademark:* [?]. *Domain:* [?]. **Confidence: medium-low. Recommended alternate.**

**3. 商工日報 — Shōkō Nippō — "Commerce-and-Industry Daily."**
商工 is the state's own SME vocabulary (商工中金, 商工組合 [R]) — a paper taking the government's category word.
- *Exists:* not recalled [?]; 商工-prefixed trade papers are plausible [?]. *Trademark/Domain:* [?]. **Confidence: medium-low. Recommended alternate.**

**4. 中央経済新聞 — Chūō Keizai Shimbun — "Central Economic Newspaper."**
中央 is standard postwar institutional register (中央公論 [R]).
- *Exists:* exact paper not recalled [?]; **near-miss: 中央経済社, a real business-book publisher, sits in the adjacent class** [R].
- *Confidence: medium risk. Usable only after clearance.*

**5. 帝国経済新聞 — Teikoku Keizai Shimbun — "Imperial Economic Newspaper."**
A pre-war masthead preserved into the 1990s, the way 帝国データバンク kept its name [R].
- *Exists:* 帝国新聞 recalled as a real Meiji-era daily [R, medium]; exact combination not recalled [?]. Homophone hazard: short form 帝経 (Teikei) sits one vowel from 帝京大学 (Teikyō University) [R].
- *Trademark:* 帝国- marks live in JP class 36 (Teikoku Databank) [R]. **Confidence: strong flavour, real clearance burden.**

**6. 経済タイムス — Keizai Taimusu — "Economic Times."**
タイムス is an established paper suffix (ジャパンタイムズ [R]; 東京タイムズ recalled as a defunct postwar evening paper [R, medium]).
- *Exists:* exact not recalled [?]; the English "Economic Times" family is large internationally (the Indian paper [R]) — evocation, not identity. **Confidence: low-medium.**

**7. 夕刊東京 — Yūkan Tōkyō — "Evening Edition: Tokyo."**
Dedicated evening mastheads exist (夕刊フジ [R]); an evening paper has first claim on closing prices, which is the operational ground for its right to measure. 
- *Exists:* not recalled [?]; the evening-tabloid shelf is occupied by 夕刊フジ and 日刊ゲンダイ [R].
- *Weakness:* no short house word, and the `<publisher> Average` form needs one. **Confidence: low-medium; form-weak for the index role.**

## Section 3 — The three index names

**Principle.** The form records the measurer: Korea's authority word is the country itself in a state acronym; Japan's is the newspaper's own abbreviation (日経 was built from 日本経済新聞); America's is the house plus a count of issues *the house itself graded*. The number follows the literal-count tradition (Dow 30, Wilshire 5000 — named for roughly the number of issues it covered at launch [R, medium]), not S&P 500's breadth aspiration. Count = f(the measurer's own operation), never f(the market's size). The Korean form carries no count — KOSPI carries none [R].

## Section 6 — Refusals and residual risk

**What I could not check:** live trademark registers (J-PlatPat, KIPRIS, TESS, EUIPO); corporate registries; domain availability; current exchange symbology files; exhaustive historical periodical records — especially pre-1945 Japanese dailies, regional Korean press, and US city dailies; and the full membership of the local 経済新聞 network.

**Professional clearance required before use:** 帝国経済新聞 (JP classes 16/36/41; dense 帝国- landscape plus the Teikyō homophone); 中央経済新聞 (中央経済社 adjacency); KOSCI and KOPIX (KIPRIS plus KRX); CWL, ZKA, KSCI, LGCP (symbology files — three-letter strings are heavily harvested by funds and crypto assets). All US pairs need only nominal clearance: surnames are weak marks; the exposure is evocation, not ownership.

**Low enough risk to proceed on, pending the checks above:** Zenkei (全国経済新聞 / 全経平均), Shōkō (商工日報 / 商工平均 96), Thayer & Biddle 250, Winslow & Alden, Sturgis & Ames; tickers LGCP, LGN, ZKA, CWL, KSCI subject to a one-day feed and WHOIS check.

Nothing in this document is asserted clear. The recommended tier means: no collision recalled, weak marks by construction, and the residual risk named rather than buried.
