# 3. What to return in Part 2

## A. Definitions

One line per problem only: a definition that leaves open a choice that would move the split; a definition an audience would not understand.

## B. Steps

Missing steps, two steps that are the same choice counted twice, or steps cut much finer or coarser than in the other securities. One line each.

## C. Classes

Steps you think are in the wrong class **under the rule as stated**, including the exception for a choice of means the other person reads. One line each, with the reason.

## D. Importance

For each security where **your Part 1 value and the author's differ by 2 or more** on a step, say which step and why you see it differently. Then name the three splits you find least credible, and why.
