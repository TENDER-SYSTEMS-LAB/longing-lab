# 1. Fixed context — do not argue with it

LONGING RESEARCH is an artwork. It presents itself as a dry financial research terminal: indices, price charts, analyst reports. The assets it covers are human practices, not companies. Prices decline over the long run because the author believes romance is genuinely disappearing; the decline must come from structural forces the model publishes, not from a hard-coded trend.

## Why each security needs an execution/judgment split

When one person leans on another, they issue a bond; the decline of a practice is made of **calls**, people redeeming early because they can now do alone what once needed another person. A decided rule says what raises calls: **substitution of judgment** — a system deciding what the person could have decided. Handing over the *doing* while keeping the choice is not, by itself, substitution. So every security carries two shares that sum to 1: its **judgment share**, how much of the practice a system can take by deciding in the person's place, and its **execution share**, what can be handed over with the choice kept. The price model reads these shares.

## The procedure, as decided

1. Each security has a **definition sentence**, defined by act, not by medium.
2. The definition is broken into **steps**.
3. Each step is **judgment** if it contains more than one live option that differs in the practice's product or meaning; otherwise it is **execution**. Manner — medium, route, handwriting — is execution, **except a choice of means that the other person reads as part of the message, which is judgment.**
4. **Mixed steps are forbidden.** Every step is one class or the other.
5. The author gives each step an **importance from 1 to 5** (1 = barely matters, 5 = this step decides the practice). Importance is part of the definition.
6. **Execution share = sum of importance of execution steps ÷ sum of all importance.**

## The securities

Twenty-two securities need a split. Some are the two **legs** of one bond — the leaning side and the giving side (TELL/LSTN, LERN/TCHR, ASK/HELP); some are two **forms** of one giving side (CHSE/MAKE, TRET/LEND). The rest stand alone.
