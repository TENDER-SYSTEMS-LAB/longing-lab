# Japanese publisher — clearance shortlist, 2026-09-20

**Type:** document (working shortlist)
**Basis:** the seven naming-round responses (`2026-09-20-institution-naming-*.md`) and the user's decision that the publisher is a **national** paper (`2026-09-20-japanese-publisher-is-national.md`); the user's position that real-world conventions need not be followed exactly
**Attribution:** llm-proposed — a compilation for a clearance search, not a selection. Nothing here is cleared, and nothing was re-checked against the web in this compilation; every collision note carries the reviewer and basis it came from.

## What the shortlist is for

The naming round's deliverable was the collision report, not the names. The user then fixed the publisher as national, which drops every regional-qualifier candidate (東海, 北陸, 京浜, 関東, 阪神, 瀬戸内) and leaves the eight forms below, each proposed by one reviewer and none by two. This document puts them side by side in the shape a clearance search needs: the masthead, the two-character contraction the index will be named from, the index name, what the round found, what it could not check, and which checks would settle each one. The order is by how much of the ground the round could see, not by preference.

**What every candidate still needs, without exception:** J-PlatPat (JPO) search in classes 16 (printed matter), 41 (publishing), 36 (financial information) and 9 (downloadable publications); the Japanese newspaper lists and the National Diet Library periodical catalogue for pre-war and occupation-era titles, where a hit on a dead paper is a real collision for a work set in 1996; the みんなの経済新聞 member list; WHOIS on the obvious `.co.jp` / `.jp` / `.com`; and a homophone read of the contraction, since the index is spoken.

## The eight

### 1. 首都経済新聞 — Shuto Keizai Shimbun — "Capital Economic Newspaper" — contraction 首経 (Shukei) — index 首経平均 / Shukei Average

- **Proposed by:** ChatGPT, its first Japanese candidate to spend clearance effort on.
- **Form:** geographical designation + economic remit + 新聞; national because 首都 is the capital, not a region.
- **Found:** no exact publication, company, index or fictional institution verified under the title or its romanisation, on limited exact-name web searches. Confidence on non-occupation stated as *low*.
- **Not checked:** trademark in all four territories; historical occupation; `shuto-keizai.jp`, `shutokeizai.com`.
- **Contraction risk (not in the round):** 首経 is not a recalled abbreviation of anything; *Shukei* is a homophone of 主計 (accounting, as in 主計局) and 集計 (tabulation) — dry, and arguably on-register for a paper that computes an average. Needs a read by a native speaker before it is treated as clean.
- **Read:** the only candidate whose proposer ran a screen and advanced it; still unknown on every register.

### 2. 夕刊経済新報 — Yūkan Keizai Shinpō — "Evening-Edition Economic Report" — contraction 夕経 (Yūkei) — index 夕経平均 / Yūkei Average

- **Proposed by:** Claude, as its lowest-risk publisher.
- **Form:** 夕刊 + sector + 新報, the postwar evening-paper shape; the round noted (GLM) that an evening paper has first claim on closing prices, which is an operational basis for the right to measure, and (Claude) that the Sankei began under an evening publisher.
- **Found:** no such paper recalled [R]; one web search on the full name and on 夕経平均 returned nothing of that name [W]. 経済時事新報, of the same 〜経済〜新報 form, was a real Tokyo economic daily merged into the Nikkei in 1942 — the form is authentic and that exact title is taken.
- **Not checked:** trademark (classes 16, 41, 36, 9 named); domains — *Yukei* is a homophone of 有形 (tangible) and a given name, so `yukei.com` / `.jp` are probably taken [I].
- **Read:** moderate confidence it is unoccupied, from one reviewer; the domain is the likely loss.

### 3. 秋津経済新報 — Akitsu Keizai Shinpō — "Akitsu [archaic name for Japan] Economic Report" — contraction 秋経 (Shūkei) — index 秋経平均 / Shūkei Average

- **Proposed by:** Claude, second choice.
- **Form:** poetic national names were normal for Meiji-founded papers (やまと新聞, 萬朝報 cited); national by the name's meaning.
- **Found:** no such paper known [R]. Akitsu is a real place name and a railway station, and small firms use it, so domains are probably partly taken [I]. The contraction is a homophone of 集計 (tabulation), which the reviewer called harmless and dry.
- **Not checked:** trademark [U]; historical papers.
- **Read:** moderate; the archaic name is the one thing in the eight that could read as a flourish, which the register rule forbids — a judgement for the user, not a collision.

### 4. 全国経済新聞 — Zenkoku Keizai Shimbun — "Nationwide Economic Newspaper" — contraction 全経 (Zenkei) — index 全経平均 / Zenkei Average

- **Proposed by:** GLM, its recommended primary.
- **Form:** scope word + subject + 新聞, the real field-daily pattern (全国農業新聞 cited); explicitly national.
- **Found:** exact name not recalled as ever used [?]. Near neighbours: the ◯◯経済新聞 pattern is occupied at city scale by the Minkei network [R]; a Korean local paper 전국경제신문 may exist [?, low]. **全経 is the abbreviation of a real bookkeeping-education body** (全国経理教育協会) [R, small].
- **Not checked:** composite marks in classes 16/36/41 [?]; `zenkoku-keizai.co.jp` plausibly open [?].
- **Read:** the contraction collision is small but real, and it is exactly the kind of thing a clearance search is for. Confidence stated medium-low.

### 5. 商工日報 — Shōkō Nippō — "Commerce-and-Industry Daily" — contraction 商工 — index 商工平均 / Shōkō Average

- **Proposed by:** GLM, recommended alternate.
- **Form:** 商工 is the state's own SME vocabulary (商工中金, 商工組合 cited) — a paper taking the government's category word; 日報 is a live newspaper suffix.
- **Found:** not recalled [?]; 商工-prefixed trade papers are plausible [?].
- **Not checked:** everything — trademark, domain, historical trade papers.
- **Contraction risk:** 商工 is a common word, weak as a mark and heavily used in institutional names; the index name 商工平均 would be distinctive only as the full string.
- **Read:** low flavour, low collision on recall, but the contraction is a dictionary word.

### 6. 商況日報 — Shōkyō Nippō — "Commercial-Conditions Daily" — contraction 商況 — index 商況平均 / Shokyo Average

- **Proposed by:** Grok, first of its recommended tier.
- **Form:** 日報 as a live suffix; 商況 is the Meiji market-conditions word.
- **Found:** no paper of this title on current or defunct Japanese newspaper lists. **商況社 did exist: it published 中外商業新報, the Nikkei's ancestor** — lineage association, not title collision, but a clearance lawyer would flag the proximity (Grok's own caution on the adjacent 東京市況新報).
- **Not checked:** trademark [U], classes 16 and 36/41 named; 商況 is descriptive, so distinctiveness of the pair is the issue; `shokyo-nippo.co.jp` likely open or parked, not checked.
- **Read:** medium-high on "no living paper"; the residual risk is the Nikkei ancestry of the word itself.

### 7. 夕刊商業 — Yūkan Shōgyō — "Evening Commerce" — contraction 商業 — index 商業平均 / Shōgyō Average

- **Proposed by:** Qwen.
- **Form:** time-of-day + subject, after 夕刊フジ, 日刊工業.
- **Found:** no prominent exact match as a national paper; 夕刊 is a saturated prefix. The reviewer's *Confidence: High* rests on recall alone and is not carried.
- **Not checked:** everything.
- **Contraction risk:** 商業 is a dictionary word (commerce) and 商業新報 is the Nikkei ancestor's tail (中外商業新報); 東京商業新報 was rejected by Grok as too close to 東商新聞 and to that ancestor. The index name *Shōgyō Average* is generic.
- **Read:** the weakest of the eight on distinctiveness; kept because the form is plausible.

### 8. 日本取引新聞 — Nihon Torihiki Shimbun — "Japan Transaction News" — contraction 日取 (Nittori) — index 日取平均 / Nittori Average

- **Proposed by:** Qwen.
- **Form:** geography + subject + 新聞; 取引 is a standard financial term.
- **Found:** no exact match; both components descriptive. Recall-only confidence, not carried.
- **Not checked:** everything.
- **Contraction risk (not in the round):** 日本 + anything contracts to 日〇, which sits beside 日経, 日商 (Japan Chamber of Commerce — rejected by Claude and GLM), 日産; 日取 has no recalled collision but 取引 is also the exchanges' own word (取引所), so the paper reads as the exchange's organ rather than an independent measurer — a register point, not a collision.
- **Read:** plausible form, contraction in the crowded 日〇 family.

### Flagged, kept out of the tier: 帝都経済新聞 — Teito Keizai Shimbun — contraction 帝都

- **Proposed by:** Grok (recommended) and Gemini (as 帝都新報, "Clean").
- **Found:** 帝都新聞 is a well-known fictional paper in film, manga and television (Claude, [R]); 帝国データバンク is a real, prominent credit bureau one word away, and GLM's 帝国経済新聞 was flagged for the 帝経 / 帝京 homophone. Grok itself warns not to shorten toward 帝国.
- **Read:** two independent flags on the same ground. Not in the eight.

## Three things a clearance search will not settle

- **Whether the contraction is spoken well.** 首経, 夕経, 秋経 and 全経 each have a homophone (主計/集計, 有形, 集計, none of note); which of them sounds like an index and which like a pun is a native-speaker read.
- **Whether the evening-paper form is wanted.** Two of the eight are 夕刊 forms, with the closing-price argument behind them; the user's national decision does not choose between an evening and a morning paper.
- **The index count.** The user has said real-world conventions need not be followed exactly; the `<contraction>平均` form carries no count, as *Nikkei Average* does not, and none is proposed here.

## What this does not do

It does not rank, recommend, or clear. Nothing in it was re-checked against a register, a newspaper list, a domain or a web search in this compilation; it reorganises what seven reviewers reported under the decision that the publisher is national. Candidates from the reviewers' rejected lists are not revived.
