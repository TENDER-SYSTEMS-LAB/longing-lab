# LONGING RESEARCH — Widening the List of Practices That Become Securities

Purpose: widen and test a list of **candidate securities** for a fictional research institution whose securities stand for human practices that modernization and AI are taking over.

This is a **collection and critique round, not a selection round.** Nothing you write is adopted from it. The author chooses afterwards.

---

# 1. Fixed context — do not argue with it

LONGING RESEARCH is an artwork. It presents itself as a dry financial research terminal: indices, price charts, analyst reports. The assets it covers are human practices, not companies. Prices decline over the long run because the author believes romance is genuinely disappearing; nobody inside the fiction argues this, and the decline must come from structural forces the model publishes, not from a hard-coded trend.

The work is built top-down: analysis → abstraction → ontology → securities. The analysis and the ontology are decided. This round serves the last step.

## The ontology, in brief

Four layers, joined by named relations:

1. **Origin.** Something is romantic when a person's own judgment is its origin: choosing, sending and standing behind words — not composing them. The medium does not matter. Body and place are not the condition of romance; a practice tied to a body or a place carries that as an attribute. Chosen friction (a slow path taken for a reason) is romantic; imposed friction is not.
2. **Exchange.** When one person leans on another — asks, relies, promises — they issue a bond to the other. The principal is attention. A favour granted or a reply returned is a coupon; it does not shrink the stock, because a living relationship keeps issuing. A bond always stays between two people; gatherings matter because they create new two-person bonds. Exploitative relations are outside the market.
3. **Balances.** What exchange accumulates: love, understanding, trust and belief (belief: that people will gather again and someone will help). Hostility is an indicator beside them, not a balance.
4. **Handed over.** Reliance moves to non-human counterparts that respond, decide or care in a person's place (the instrument called BLIND TRUST). Two channels take: a **device** hides the effort a practice needed, the unused capacity rusts, people hand over more, and the occasion to ask a person disappears; and **substitution of judgment**, where a system decides what the person could have decided. Two channels give: **arrival**, when a technology creates a new way to reach people (post, telephone, messaging, the social web), and **restoration**, when it gives back a way illness or distance took. Every technology tends to give before it takes.

## Rules already decided

- **A security is a practice**: something a person does with a person, with steps. Balances and layers are read through indices over practices, not as securities.
- **Practices are defined by act, not by form.** "Writing to someone in one's own words", not "a handwritten letter". Medium, body and place are attributes or variants.
- **Abstract emotions are never tickers.** `LOVE ▲2.4%` is rejected.
- **A practice enters the list only if it passes four tests:** (1) *origin* — done well, someone chooses and stands behind it; (2) *issue* — it creates a bond between two people; (3) *exposure* — a device, substitution of judgment, arrival or restoration reaches it; (4) *loss record* — its decline or rise would show as a difference between records in at least one of five loss layers: practice (people stop doing it), capacity (people can no longer do it), occasion (the reason to ask a person disappears), balance, or the sense of loss itself.
- **The universe as a whole must meet three coverage rules:** every balance is fed by several practices; every channel touches several practices; and the list is not made only of declining practices — practices that arrivals raised strongly must be present, or the decline is hard-coded by selection.
- **No fixed count.** The author's condition: each security should be **easy for an audience to understand at a glance and meaningful inside the work.**
- **Do not choose practices because data happens to reach them.**

# 2. What this round is NOT for

Do not rank the final universe or say how many securities there should be. Do not propose prices, coefficients, factors, tickers or a model. Do not assess whether the artistic premise is sound.

**Do not invent sources.** If you cite research, mark it `verified`, `probable` or `unverified`. Earlier rounds in this project received fabricated sources marked as verified.

# 3. What to return

## A. Critique of the thirty

For each candidate that **fails or barely passes** a test or a rule, one line: number, which test or rule, why. Note candidates that overlap so much they would move together. Note candidates an audience would not understand at a glance, and candidates that would carry little meaning inside the work. Do not comment on candidates that pass cleanly.

## B. New candidates

As many as you think worth proposing, in a table with these columns: practice (by act, one line, plain words an audience understands); balance it mostly feeds; strongest channel and sign; loss layer; why it is meaningful inside this work (one line); and whether it is declining, rising or both from the Luddites (1811) to the present.

Range widely: work, family, neighbourhood, learning, faith, play, care, public life, travel, commerce. Include practices that are **rising** or that a technology **restored**. Avoid practices defined by a medium or a device.

## C. Coverage check

After your additions, say which balance and which channel are thinnest in the combined list, and which kinds of human life are missing.

## D. States priced through practices

Some conditions are not practices — reflection (room to form one's own judgment), waiting for a reply, serendipity. The author has decided that reflection becomes a security priced through the practices it makes possible. Without recommending, list other states that could be priced this way, and for each, the practices from the list that would carry it. Say in one line what could go wrong with pricing a state this way.
